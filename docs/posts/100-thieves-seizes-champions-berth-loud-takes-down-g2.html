Both of the day's matches had lopsided stakes. NRG and G2 had already guaranteed their places in Champions and were fighting purely for seeding and prize money, while 100T and LOUD both had their Champions fates in their own hands. The series began on NRG's pick of Abyss. 100T, who had not yet played the map, opted for the same Duelist duo of Jett and Phoenix as their opponents but reinforced it with Harbor and Cypher versus NRG's Omen and Viper.

Despite losing the first pistol, 100T took the initiative early with a surprise Stinger force buy to take a 4-1 lead on their attack. Any hope of an NRG recovery was dashed by a Timotino ace in round nine as 100T snowballed into a 9-3 attack half; a second pistol promised a quick 13-3 end. Timotino finished the map with a leading 20/8/1 KDA and six first bloods.

100T's pick was Sunset, one of NRG's weaker maps this season. Both teams made slight compositional adjustments from their previous showings, with vora going from Sova to Fade and NRG moving keiko back to Chamber.

The first half was the closest one of the series. NRG pulled off a full eco in round two, but 100T were largely in control of the buy rounds and retained a 7-5 lead at the half. The second half was no contest as 100T strung together a pistol and a bonus to win out 13-6. vora and bang stood out with 35 combined kills and respective ratings of 1.39 and 1.34.
