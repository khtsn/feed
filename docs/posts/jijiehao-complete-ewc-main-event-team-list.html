JiJieHao complete EWC main event team list

JiJieHao became the final team to advance through the Esports World Cup open qualifier after a 2-1 win over HOTU (Mirage 7-13, Cache 13-9, Dust2 13-11).

JiJieHao join 100 Thieves, BIG, and K27 as the teams to progress to EWC's main stage, with Dionis "⁠sinnopsyy⁠" Budeci's troops undoubtedly being the biggest shock of the qualifier.

They narrowly progressed through the group stage after a three-map series against Sangal in the decider match, but found their groove in the playoffs with three 2-1 wins.

DENDELE were first to fall in a big upset to start off their run before Hussein "⁠m1N1⁠" Hijazi topped the server in a victory against Phantom.
