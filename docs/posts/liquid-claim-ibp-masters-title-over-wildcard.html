Liquid claim IBP Masters title over Wildcard

malbsMd was the event's highest-rated player

Liquid have claimed the iBUYPOWER Masters FML 2026 title, capping a flawless run through the $30,000 LAN event with a comfortable 2-0 victory over Wildcard in the final.

The result marks Liquid's first offline win since 2022, when they claimed first place in the IEM Road to Rio qualifier on LAN, but their first trophy since 2019, when they went on a dominant run of five tier one LAN wins in a row.

Throughout their campaign Liquid were only once in serious danger of losing a map, cruising past Chicken Coop and NRG in the group stage before M80 gave them their closest run of the tournament in the first map of the semi-finals.
