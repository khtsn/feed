ruggah retires from coaching

The Danish coach has called it quits after a decade standing behind teams.

Casper "⁠ruggah⁠" Due has closed the curtains on his coaching career after more than ten years of service.

An EPICENTER winner with Dignitas, he went on to work with North, OpTic, OG, and, most recently, Astralis.

ruggah departs Astralis

The 38-year-old was released by Astralis and replaced by Filip "⁠NEO⁠" Kubski after a poor result at the IEM Cologne Major, and did not join a new team over the summer.
