FURIA, 9z and G2 among final invites for FPG 3

FURIA won FPG 2 last year

The final set of invited teams for FISSURE Playground 3 have been announced, with FURIA the headline name among the latest additions to the tournament's team list.

9z, FUT, G2, Astralis, BIG and GamerLegion are also now set to attend the $1,000,000 event in China, which is scheduled for September 8-13.

The addition of FURIA and 9z, who are currently among the top 10 in the VRS, represents a boon for the tournament, which was already set to feature big names like FaZe and The MongolZ.

The tournament kicks off just after the conclusion of BLAST Open Porto so any teams competing in both events, including the likes of FURIA, Legacy and 9z, will have little time to fly to China should they reach the final in Porto.

The team list for FPG 3 is now almost complete, with one more team to be found via the Asia closed qualifier, which will take place August 31-September 3.
