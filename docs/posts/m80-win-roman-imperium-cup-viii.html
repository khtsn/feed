M80 win Roman Imperium Cup VIII

M80 laid claim to the trophy at Roman Imperium Cup VIII in Portugal after sweeping Luminosity in the grand final, bagging 13-10 wins on Dust2 and Cache to walk away with the $6,968 top prize and a 17-place jump in the VRS.

M80 were seeded directly into the double-elimination playoff bracket and began their campaign in Vila Nova de Gaia with wins over 6666 and Iberian Soul.

They were forced to rematch the Spaniards in the consolidation final after getting reverse-swept by Luminosity in the upper bracket final, but had no trouble repeating the victory before getting revenge on Luminosity in the title decider to lift the trophy.

It is the third LAN title of the year for M80 after they previously secured trophies at fl0m Mythical LAN and DraculaN Season 6, and comes ahead of the team's next outing at Logitech G Play Connect in Warsaw, Poland on September 17.
