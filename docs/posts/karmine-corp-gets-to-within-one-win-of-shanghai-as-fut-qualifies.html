Red-hot after a dominant win, FUT kept its foot on the gas. While the team has struggled to find its identity on Ascent this year, going through a few comp changes, it had no issues with BBL's double-Sentinel on this occasion. Karmine Corp and FUT Esports have sealed their spots in Barcelona to square off for a spot in the Grand Final, but FUT has already qualified for Shanghai. With the win over BBL, FUT has guaranteed a top-three finish, and with the top spot in the Championship Points standings already claimed, will go to Shanghai.
