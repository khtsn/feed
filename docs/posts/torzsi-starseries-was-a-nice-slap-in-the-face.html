We didn't really think about that, to be honest. But as you said, I think it's good that we beat them. Of course, it was also, just overall, we faced each other three times this season. Or no, two times, sorry, two finals. And in maps, we were four-four. They won four maps, we won four maps. And now we just wanted to win, and we are up six-four right now in overall with this lineup.
