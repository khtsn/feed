fnatic and Nemiga round out Stake Ranked Episode 4 team list

fnatic and Nemiga have secured the final two spots at Stake Ranked Episode 4 after making it through the European closed qualifier for the event.

The $100,000 tournament will take place in Barcelona, Spain, from October 1-4 and have six other directly invited teams in attendance: FaZe, Astralis, B8, BIG, Luminosity and Alliance.

The road through the qualifier was a tough one for fnatic, played three maps in all three series. The Ukrainian side recovered from 5-12 down against Virtus.pro and nearly gave away a 10-2 lead against BBL, before locking down their spot in the deciding match against Ninjas in Pyjamas.

On the other side, Nemiga kicked off their run with a narrow win over Iberian Soul before they locked down 2-0 wins over Nemesis and FOKUS to make it to the organization's first international LAN in over a year.
