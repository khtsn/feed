BLAST Open Porto 2026 - Playoffs

Format

  • You can create your teams on the Fantasy page here.

  • Six-team single-elimination playoffs.

  • Quarter-finals and semi-finals are BO3, grand final is BO5.

Teams

Things to note 

  • Spirit and MOUZ start from semi-finals and their players will get 6 points for skipping quarter-finals.

  • BO5 grand final will deliver 25% more rating points than regular BO3 games.

Player prices & pick stats 

  • donk is the most picked player in this Fantasy, being present on roughly 50% of the teams despite his usually hefty price tag of $245k. His pick count by far exceed the popularity of other “golden cards” in this game, but the investment will rely heavily on Spirit’s ability to win the semi-final match.

  • jL has put up a very solid showing in the group stage at a 1.17 rating, second only to ZywOo on Vitality, but he still goes for a very moderate price of $194k as Vitality are far from being the favorites to take the title. The Lithuanian is the third-most picked player in the game.
