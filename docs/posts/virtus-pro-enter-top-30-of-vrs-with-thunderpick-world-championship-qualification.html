Virtus.pro enter top 30 of VRS with Thunderpick World Championship qualification

Virtus.pro have earned a spot at Thunderpick World Championship, a $1,000,000 LAN in Malta slated for October 14-18, after completing a grueling run through the Closed Qualifier.

Nikolay "⁠mir⁠" Bityukov's troops began the qualifier with a loss to K27, but beat BESTIA and got revenge on K27 to make the playoffs. There, they overcame several of the qualifier's biggest names in FOKUS, HEROIC, and HOTU, emerging victorious from hard-fought series, including a sweep in the title decider.

It will be Virtus.pro's first big international LAN since IEM Chengdu 2025, and continues the organization's upward trajectory since pulling the plug on its struggling lineup and rebuilding around four of its former academy members.

Virtus.pro had cratered below 100th on the VRS before embarking on its rebuild and played no officials on HLTV before returning in May with a title run at MPKBK CIS LAN Season 5, and have since finished as runners-up at MPKBK CIS LAN Season 6, ESL Challenger League Season 52 Europe Cup 5, and made the playoffs at the Esports World Cup Open Qualifier.

Those results, alongside their run in the Thunderpick qualifier, have pushed the team into the top 30 of the VRS — enough to give them a slim shot at qualifying for PGL Major Singapore.
