Falcons announce m0NESY contract extension

The Russian sniper has committed his future to the Saudi organization.

Falcons have announced a multi-year contract extension with Ilya "⁠m0NESY⁠" Osipov, set to run until 2030, the organization announced on Monday.

The Russian sniper joined the Saudi organization in April 2025 in a blockbuster move and has averaged a 1.29 rating across his tenure with the team.

Despite his strong individual output, the 21-year-old needed a long time to break his trophy duck with Falcons — doing so in style by securing the IEM Cologne Major in June, where m0NESY also took home the MVP award after a fantastic individual performance.

With m0NESY's future now set in stone, Falcons can set their sights on defending their Major crown in Singapore.

Their next tournament will come at the Thunderpick World Championship, set to begin on October 14, with IEM Beijing also on the team's calendar ahead of the Major.
