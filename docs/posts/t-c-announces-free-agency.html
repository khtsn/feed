T.c announces free agency

Tiaan "⁠T.c⁠" Coertzen has taken to X to announce he is a free agent and open to opportunities, stating that he has not been contracted with Passion UA since June.

His post to social media comes in the wake of a recent HLTV article detailing the issues behind the scenes at Passion UA, who have not played a HLTV official since ending their season early back in May by withdrawing from BC Game Masters Championship Season 2.

As Passion UA crumbles, players and staff are owed significant sums

T.c originally joined Passion UA in April 2025 after three years with Complexity, marking the first step in Passion UA's pivot away from an all-Ukrainian approach. In less than six months the former Complexity core that T.c had coached joined him at Passion UA.

Under T.c Passion UA enjoyed some success, taking first in Urban Riga Open S1 and top-four placings at several DraculaNs, and made Stage 3 of StarLadder Budapest Major, narrowly missing out on a playoff spot.

Results in general took a downward turn in 2026, however, with the team most notably failing to make IEM Cologne Major. T.c's time with Passion UA essentially came to a close in May when the team ended their season early by withdrawing from BC Game Masters Championship S2.
