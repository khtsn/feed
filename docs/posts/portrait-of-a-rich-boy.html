The Latvian has handed the reins back to FalleN on Cache, Anubis, and Ancient.

In a post-match interview with James "⁠BanKs⁠" Banks after FURIA beat MIBR at StarSeries Fall, Mareks "⁠YEKINDAR⁠" Gaļinskis revealed he has relinquished calling duties to Gabriel "⁠FalleN⁠" Toledo.

FURIA had been testing YEKINDAR as a caller partly in preparation for FalleN's impending retirement at the end of the year, with the Latvian calling T sides on Anubis, Cache, and Ancient. However, he has since repeatedly stated in interviews that he wants to explore in-game leading later in his career, not now while he still feels he can perform as a role player.

"Credit where it's due: I'm not calling anymore, and hopefully I'm not going to be anymore in the future," he said when asked about FURIA's Cache T side after the match. "Because you know, we tried it, we tried it to understand like, is this something I want to pursue? Is this something the team needs?

"And I came to the conclusion that this is too much cortisol, I don't want to IGL, and I was like, 'FalleN, you need to take these maps back,' (laughs) and he was like, 'Of course I'm going to take it back, no problem.'"

Asked to confirm if he isn't calling on any maps anymore, he added: "Well, sometimes, when you feel it, maybe I would call something. But most of the time, when it's the hard situations, it's always FalleN.

"[...] It depends on the perspective of calling. The overall idea or something is always a lot more on FalleN, but the micro-adjustments and how to get that idea is something I can help with. But mainly FalleN."
