NIP sign adamb

14-9-2026 18:28

The Swedish rifler re-joins the organization after more than three years away.

Ninjas in Pyjamas have announced the signing of Adam "⁠adamb⁠" Ångström from OG ahead of their appearance at Logitech G Play Connect.

The 21-year-old will take the place of academy player Roman "⁠n0te⁠" Hamze, who was temporarily promoted to the main team after Artem "⁠cairne⁠" Mushynskyi's benching.

The news continues a period of change in the Ninjas in Pyjamas camp, with the organization also recently signing Karim "⁠Krimbo⁠" Moussa as their IGL.

Read more

NIP sign Krimbo on loan from BIG

adamb previously played for the Young Ninjas academy program during 2022-2023, eventually joining Metizport and OG. He represented the latter for just over a year and averaged a team-high 1.13 rating across his tenure.

adamb will make his Ninjas in Pyjamas debut at Logitech G Play Connect, where Krimbo's troops are set to face M80 in the opening round.
