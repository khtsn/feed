MIBR eliminate understrength magic from StarSeries

MIBR have kept their StarLadder Staries Fall campaign alive with a lower bracket victory over magic, picking up wins on Ancient (13-8) and Inferno (13-8) either side of a loss on Mirage (11-13).

It was assumed that MIBR would take the win in this series as magic are attending StarSeries with their coach standing in for Andrey "⁠AW⁠" Anisimov, but the fact magic managed to take a map victory was a surprise.

The series was going as expected on Ancient, two magic pistol wins in both halves serving up a closer score line than was perhaps appropriate, as a masterful Felipe "⁠insani⁠" Yuji performance granted MIBR a straightforward 1-0 series lead.

Abdurakhim "⁠mo0N⁠" Issa led a surprise magic fightback on Mirage, a stellar 17 kills on the CT side guiding the team to a narrow 13-11 victory, but MIBR duly righted the ship on Inferno and once again dominated despite magic winning both pistols.

The result breaks the momentum magic were starting to generate and does a small amount of damage to their VRS point total. The team should still be in a solid position to qualify for PGL Singapore Major later this year, and have a good chance to push on in their next LAN appearance at 1win Private Club starting September 25.

MIBR meanwhile continue their positive first steps with Tomas "⁠tomaszin⁠" Corna among their number, and can now look forward to a lower bracket semi-final against the loser of Aurora vs. NRG.
