Aurora march past NRG in StarSeries Fall

Aurora are through to the upper bracket final of StarLadder StarSeries Fall, making their way past NRG with victories on Cache (13-7) and Mirage (19-17).

Whilst Aurora were the comfortable favorites in this matchup, despite NRG's surprise upset of MOUZ, they will still be pleased with the manner of their victory. The international side stole away their opponents pick with ease, before showing real fortitude to survive a stiff challenge on Mirage.

In recent interviews Aurora have spoken about their mentality issues costing them in games, as Jimi "⁠Jimpphat⁠" Salo said to HLTV at BLAST Open Porto, so their ability to shake off the loss of a 6-0 lead on Mirage is a step forwards.
