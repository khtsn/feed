karrigan: "We have played too much Dust2; we need to play other maps"

Falcons experienced mixed fortunes during their group stage campaign in BLAST Open Porto, getting revenge over Legacy to book the playoffs and posting a dominant map win over MOUZ, but struggling against Lynn Vision in their opening game and eventually losing the aforementioned series to MOUZ.

A feature of their group stage run was an insistence on picking Ancient, despite the pick regularly letting them down. "We have been working on it a lot," Finn "⁠karrigan⁠" Andersen explained in the wake of his team's loss to MOUZ. "I think it felt always decent when I joined but especially this season, practice has been really good on it."

The main reason for picking Ancient, however, was to expand the team's map pool, according to karrigan. "We have played too much Dust2 and, for this reasoning, we need to play other maps as well. I think this was our fifth Nuke in the past 90 days at this point, so it was good to play some other maps as well."

Ultimately, karrigan feels the reps they gained were the best feature of their group stage efforts. "We're still not there, but I think we start to see some things that work and some things that don't, and those things we need to fix during officials."

What do you think went wrong for you in that series?

I think our map pick, Ancient, we've tried three times this event and it's not working out, so still some things to work on. Inferno I think was a little bit rough, but I'm super proud of how we played the Nuke game. So some things to learn from here, and it's a game where we can expand our map pool a bit because we are always picking Dust2. For me, there was some data we can use to improve, because we're still not there yet.

When it came to that Ancient pick at this tournament, was the intention to get more reps on Ancient, or did you really feel it was worth picking in these series?

We have been working on it a lot. I think it felt always decent when I joined but especially this season, practice has been really good on it. Obviously we know sometimes practice results can lie with that, but that's one of the reasons. And also we have played too much Dust2 and, for this reasoning, we need to play other maps as well. I think this was our fifth Nuke in the past 90 days at this point, so it was good to play some other maps as well.

I think in some sense it's been better for us to fight during the group stage

How do you rate this group stage overall? Obviously you got the job done and got to the playoffs, but I feel we saw the best and the worst of Falcons in this group stage: Lynn Vision, not so great, and then great map here, but lost the series.

I think in some sense it's been better for us to fight during the group stage. I think we did it at the Major, the tournament got better and we had to fight games, we lost some maps that we learn from and then keep improving. In EWC we were cruising all the way into Legacy and lost 2-0. So today there were some flaws, and I think... not the best group stage, not the worst. We are through, and MOUZ played great the last two maps.

Do you feel you're a little bit more warmed up into the season now? Because it was a slow start, obviously, BLAST online and then EWC against Legacy didn't look great. Do you feel you're starting to build up a bit of pace now?

Yeah, at this point I think we are. I think every official is really important for us, and now we have a few days off before we play in the arena, so I'm looking forward to that game. A few more reps is really, really good for us, especially now, as after this tournament we have I think two and a half weeks before next tournament. We're still not there, but I think we start to see some things that work and some things that don't, and those things we need to fix during officials.

Sometimes our B game is not the best

What do you attribute the slightly slow start to the season to? Because obviously you had the high of the Major win, and then you come back and it looks a little bit shaky.

I still think we are early in the journey with me. Obviously having a player break after 60 days when I join a team is not the best, but it's just how the situation is. We played really good at the Major and we want to reach that level again, but we need to be able to do it consistently. That comes with some bad losses and some good wins.

I don't really know why, I think individually we are having some good games, we are playing good as a team, but sometimes our B game is not the best. If you want to be the best team in the world, you need to be able to beat other teams on your off day. That's what we are trying to improve all the time because that's how we become the best team during the year.

I can't remember last time I won a group stage, so there I might be the problem

Looking ahead to the playoffs, do you think we'll see a better face from Falcons? I feel your teams have always had the hallmark of turning it on in the arena.

I hope so. I think that's where the value of me coming into the team is probably what they looked into when they signed me. So obviously I'll do my best for us to be really ready for the game. Obviously all this team loves to be on the stage, the pressure is the highest, but we still need to perform to be able to do that. So that's not an excuse.

But we made it through the groups. That was the first goal. Would've been nice with a first place, but I can't remember last time I won a group stage, so there I might be the problem.
