Santino "⁠try⁠" Rigal was the star of the show for the majority-Brazilian squad on Ancient, putting up 26 kills and a 1.60 rating and helping Legacy overcome several disadvantageous situations alongside Bruno "⁠latto⁠" Rebelatto, who put the final nail in the FUT coffin with a cerebral 1vs2.

While try cooled off somewhat on the second map of Dust2, latto's impact continued throughout. Legacy looked to be cruising to a dominant win after going 8-1 up early, but it wasn't smooth sailing for the Brazilians as FUT showed signs with their backs against the wall.

They brought the scoreline all the way back to 11-11, but latto was once again the hero as he mowed down the entire B attack with a crucial ace to win the map.
