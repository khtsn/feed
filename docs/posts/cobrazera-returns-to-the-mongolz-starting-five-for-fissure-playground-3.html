cobrazera returns to The MongolZ starting five for FISSURE Playground 3

The MongolZ have announced that Anarbileg "⁠cobrazera⁠" Uuganbayar will be in the team's starting five at FISSURE Playground 3, which kicks off on September 8 in Suzhou, China.

The 21-year-old will take the place of Sodbayar "⁠Techno⁠" Munkhbold, leaving IGL Garidmagnai "⁠bLitz⁠" Byambasuren and AWPer Usukhbayar "⁠910⁠" Banzragch as the two veterans alongside teenagers Dugarsuren "⁠DarkMeister⁠" Ireedui and Zolbayar "⁠tikuak⁠" Chimedtseren.

The MongolZ bench cobrazera

cobrazera joined The MongolZ in December 2025 from The Huns, where he had posted a 1.13 rating against mostly regional and tier-two opposition. He had big shoes to fill as Azbayar "⁠Senzu⁠" Munkhbold's replacement, and struggled to translate his previous success to the big stages before being benched in July with a 1.02 rating.

The MongolZ are currently 18th in the VRS, their lowest point since May 2024. They spent much of 2025 in the top five, even peaking in first place in September.
