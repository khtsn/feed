You've just joined MIBR, and this marks the first LAN appearance for this new roster. How has your boot camp been, and how well have you adjusted so far?

We only had one week of training, but the guys made me feel comfortable with them. I think the pracs went good, so I'm excited to play the tournament.

How does your role at MIBR differ from BESTIA?

I changed a bit of positions on some maps, and I'm trying to play that, but I think it's going good.

You led BESTIA to qualification for the Austin Major with a 1.43 rating at the MRQ, but visa issues meant your spot ultimately went to Legacy. What was it like to miss out after that performance? Can you share your feelings at that time?

Obviously, I felt good at that time, but unfortunately we couldn't travel. I don't remember exactly how I felt. Obviously happy, but yeah, only that.

Now that you've joined MIBR, what goals have you set for yourself for the rest of the year?

As I said on my Twitter, I try to help them reach their goals as an organization. And I will try to do my best to win tournaments. That's my goal, my personal goal. And to be a better player with the days.

Apart from the role we've talked about, after three years at BESTIA, what has been the biggest difference for you at MIBR? The other aspects.

Obviously, switching languages. That's the biggest difference, but I'm adapting well to that. And obviously, they have players with more experience. I don't want to say better players, but yeah, with more experience under their belt. But that's the biggest difference, I think: the language and also the players.
