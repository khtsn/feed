ONSIDE chose to open with Abyss, seeking to take advantage of PRX's struggles versus Nongshim and the fact that it was their first time showing the map. PRX made a slight compositional adjustment, moving d4v41 from Astra to Omen, while ONSIDE opted for a Sova-KAY/O double-Initiator look.

ONSIDE's plan seemed effective at first as they assumed a 5-1 lead on the typically less-favored defense side; however, a successful thrifty round late in the half gave PRX a way back into what turned into a 6-6 scoreline. Over in the second half, f0rsakeN made a huge impact for PRX, winning them another thrifty round with a Sheriff 4K and enabling consistent A site floods with his Harbor utility. PRX pressed forward to a 12-9 lead and eventually closed it out 13-11.

Over on Ascent, PRX again adjusted their composition slightly from their last showing, putting Jinggg back on Sage for a Sage-Chamber lineup. Despite having two Sentinels, PRX's attack side was an unstoppable 9-3—d4v41 farmed early picks on Chamber and Jinggg's wall facilitated repeated B splits that ONSIDE could find no answers to.

ONSIDE prolonged the inevitable with a successful force buy before eventually succumbing to the regional titans 13-5, exiting their foray into VCT Pacific with a respectable top eight finish. f0rsakeN led the series with 38 kills and a 1.43 rating.

Despite immediately entering the lower bracket due to their loss to Nongshim in the play-in stage, T1 looked unfettered versus KRX, standing strong on Lotus and coming out with an impressive Abyss to take a 2-0 victory.

T1 took the first map to Lotus, where they had defeated KRX in their last encounter during the group stage. Over the course of the map, strong starts into the halves and individual clutches proved to be the difference-maker for T1 as they won both pistol rounds, both bonus rounds, and four total clutches, one of which was a 1v3 from stax in the second pistol. KRX kept it close with multiple strings of buy rounds, but it wasn't enough to make up the deficit as T1 took the map 13-10.

Abyss was a Yoru-Waylay mirror matchup with the exception of yong on Cypher versus Munchkin on a more unusual Brimstone pick. While yong turned out to be KRX's best individual performer, Munchkin's Brimstone proved to create major headaches for KRX as T1 strung together eight consecutive attack rounds off of deep lurks and unbreakable postplants.

A failed fake attempt from KRX allowed T1 to take the bonus in the second half, all but putting an end to things as T1 went up 12-4 and later won 13-7. BuZz topfragged the series with a cumulative 47/32/10 KDA; Munchkin had the highest rating of 1.46 and a 37/25/30 KDA.
