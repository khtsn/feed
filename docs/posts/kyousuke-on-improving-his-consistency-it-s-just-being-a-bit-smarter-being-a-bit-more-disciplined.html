kyousuke on improving his consistency: "It's just being a bit smarter, being a bit more disciplined"

Falcons go into Pro League knowing that the second half of the year has been below par. Maksim "⁠kyousuke⁠" Lukin, a star in the playoffs of the Cologne Major, needs no reminder. "We expected better results after the Major," he says. "Everyone expected better."

NiKo: "We are not carrying the confidence we had from the Major well, it hurt us more than we benefitted"

Getting back to that level is a work in progress, and the 18-year-old is also putting pressure on himself in order to get the team where they need to be.

An aggressive star, his highs can sometimes be matched by his lows. Too often when his individual level isn't as high as he is used to, his floor can disappear.

"I'm trying to figure out myself how to be more consistent in a way if my game goes in not the way I like," he explains. "I'm just trying to understand how the game goes. Can you afford to peek, basically, or no?

"And just trying to be more consistent in a way, don't take over-fights. It's just being a bit smarter, being a bit more disciplined."

Listen to the interview here.

Okay. First of all, I just wanted to ask, how do you feel about the second half of the year so far?

I don't know. We will see how it goes.

But the results since the Major, basically, have you been disappointed by how it's been?

It was hard for us. We lost to Legacy. We lost in Porto to... What did we lose to?

I'm trying to figure out myself how to be more consistent, if my game doesn't go the way I like

Was it Spirit?

Yeah, Spirit, I guess. So yeah, it was a hard, rough tournament. I don't know what to say. Yeah, of course, we are disappointed with the results. We expected better results after the Major, everyone expected better.

We know how good your A game is. Have you talked more about being better in the B game and the C game type thing, when the individuals aren't as strong?

Yeah. I'm trying to figure out myself how to be more consistent in a way if my game goes in not the way I like.

What things are you trying to do to get towards that goal?

Just understand how the game goes. Can you afford to peek, basically, or no? And just trying to be more consistent in a way, don't take over-fights.

Is it hard to balance wanting to be aggressive, wanting to re-peek with being a bit smarter in those situations?

Yeah, it's just being a bit smarter, being a bit more disciplined maybe. I don't know. So yeah, just figure out the balance.

Since karrigan came in, one of the big things was activating you. Do you feel the confidence as the star rifler? Do you think that means you re-peek more, basically?

I don't know, just the player who I am, I was always like this, that I'm a star player, star life, star rifler, so I can afford the peeks. Yeah, it's just who I am. I was basically born in this role.

We fixed some maps that we didn't look great on, for example, Ancient

And I wanted to ask about the map pool because it was something that was a bit hard, losing your map pick quite a lot. How's the team feeling going into this tournament?

We're feeling good. We fixed some maps that we didn't look great on. For example, Ancient, I remember we picked it two or three times and basically myself, I was not really confident on this map before because, I don't know, I just didn't like the map. You had to basically fight mid every round and if you lose your fight, you lose your game. You cannot even play mid round. Yeah, now we fixed, and we found a solution on the map how to play. I think we became more confident and comfortable on this map, every one of us. So yeah, we fixed our map pool as well, not only Ancient.

Yeah, and maps like Inferno, Ancient, do you enjoy them? Because it feels like they're just so utility focused and like you said, one duel can settle the round. What do you think of maps like that?

Actually, nowadays, Inferno, it's okay for me. I don't mind to play this map, but in the past, I hated the map because I basically had no clue how to play it and I still don't like it because how the map is. Still people play it, but I am more comfortable on this map, so I don't mind to play it.

This change will be useful only in the clutches

And also on the game change recently with the silent reloads, how are you feeling about that? Have you noticed it much in the server so far?

I actually played a lot with this update, but I only used it one time just for fun. I was just shooting in the booths in the vault and they just use it because I never use it. I only saw karrigan use it because he said to me, 'I need to practice this shit,' and he always was reloading his 20 [bullets].

Do you think it's just a pointless change then in that way?

I think it's not pointless change, but it's... this change will be useful only in the clutches because, for example, when you have 10 [bullets], you're in Palace on Mirage and you're going A 1v2, 1v1, you can silent reload and they're going to help you. But most of the time you won't get to a position when you can use it. It's only for clutch people who are always in the clutch.

Final question, just how important is this tournament for you guys? Because we know the Major's coming up. But it's in Katowice, it's in Spodek. How much priority are you putting on Pro League?

We are putting a high priority on Pro League because it's a tournament where we need to show our best result, because we were not showing our best results in the last tournaments and we need to show the people what we are basically capable of. Yeah, it's a high priority tournament for us.
