ENGH and FNATIC set to go separate ways

FNATIC FNATIC Europe Rank #16 crashies Austin Roberts kaajak Kajetan Haremski Cloud Kirill Nehozhin Alfajer Emir Ali Beder head coach Andrey "ENGH" Sholokhov has announced that he is open to new projects away from the team. The announcement comes shortly after FNC team director Colin "CoJo" Johnson spoke on X Spaces, revealing plans for the offseason: that players with expiring contracts would be allowed to explore options, and that trials for next year's team have already begun.

ENGH's announcement shared that he is most open to opportunities in Japan, Americas, and EMEA. In Japan, he is looking for a long-term project to build a competitive roster while also developing local staff with an intent on learning Japanese. In Americas, he shared that he is open to projects in North and South America. In EMEA, ENGH shared that he has an interest in building and leading an all-Russian/Commonwealth of Independent States roster, and that he is open to organizations ready to invest in CIS talent. ENGH also shared that he is open to other opportunities outside of that list.
