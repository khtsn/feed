dziugss: "Winning EWC would mean a lot. A lot of people were saying PGL Bucharest was a Mickey Mouse tournament"

FUT are through to the grand final of EWC after a hard-fought 2-1 victory over FURIA, sealing their first-ever appearance in the title decider of a Big Event.

Speaking to HLTV after the match, Džiugas "⁠dziugss⁠" Steponavičius gave his thoughts on the "tough" clash on Ancient, the heavy loss on Nuke, and the team's mentality heading into the decider map of Dust2.

I think we never gave up in this game, and that's why we managed to win it

Next up is a clash against the winner of Spirit and Legacy, with the Lithuanian saying he would relish the chance to meet the Russian side.

"I think I would want to play Spirit," he said. "We have never played them on this team, and yeah, I want to play against them just to experience it.

"Also, grand final, I would say they're a better opponent. I would say Legacy is also an underdog like us. I would like to play Spirit."

Congratulations on beating FURIA and reaching the final here at EWC. A massive achievement for the team. How does it feel?

It feels really great. Didn't expect to go to the final of this event. So yeah, it feels really good.

Aurora and G2 both picked Ancient against FURIA, and FURIA managed to win both of those games. Today, you also picked it. It was a very close game. Did you expect it to be this tough?

Yeah, we expected this to be tough. We think they have a different play style than other teams on that map, but I think we are playing that map better than them right now because it was their permanent ban, and I think we have more experience on that map.

You didn't stand much of a chance on Nuke, but then you bounced back on Dust2, the final map. After Nuke, what was the atmosphere like, and what did you discuss outside to reset from that loss and come back stronger?

Nuke is not our map, let's say. We don't play that map really well. So yeah, we just said, 'We need to reset. It's a new map, and yeah, we just go into it and do our best.'

You showed great fighting spirit on Dust2. You were down 8-4, then 10-7, and you closed it out in overtime. For such a young team that is playing against a very experienced team like FURIA on stage, that's very impressive. What was the secret behind those comebacks?

Nothing. I think the secret is just fighting until the end. I think we never gave up in this game, and yeah, that's why we managed to win it.

You're now going up against Legacy or Spirit in the final. Do you have any preferences?

I think I would want to play Spirit. We have never played them on this team, and yeah, I want to play against them just to experience it. Also, grand final, I would say they're a better opponent in the grand final. I would say Legacy is also an underdog like us. I would like to play Spirit.

How do you think that would match up against Spirit in a best-of-five?

It would be a really good game, I think. I think we have strengths, they have strengths. I think it would be at least, like, four maps, I would say, for sure.

I talked to xfl0ud during the group stage. He said that he believes that with this team, you guys can be in the top 5. Do you believe that, too? That FUT can be a stable top 5 team in the world?

Definitely. In the future, we can be a stable top 5 team. I don't know, in like six months, let's say, I think we can be really good and always be a trophy contender.

And did you expect the team to click so well right after adding xfl0ud, or did you expect maybe it would take a little bit longer for the team to gel and things to get into place?

I expected it to take longer because practice was going pretty well, but at the same time, we had some problems. And yeah, xfl0ud is a new player. He needs to learn our things. And yeah, I expected us to not do that well that quick.

This is already a phenomenal run, and winning the tournament would be a massive achievement, even better than what you did in Bucharest. What would it mean to you personally and the team to go all the way and win the title here?

It would mean, I think, a lot. I think we want to prove that win in Bucharest... A lot of people were saying that it was a Mickey Mouse tournament because it didn't have the top teams. So yeah, it would mean a lot to us. Also, we are playing, let's say, for the first time in front of a big crowd. We really want to win it.

The Accor Arena will be massive, right? How do you think that the team will perform in front of such a big crowd? You are still very young.

I think we will just try our best. I don't know how everyone feels. We have kind of never talked about it, but for myself, for example, even today, the crowd was, let's say, the arena was full. I don't know the capacity.

4,000, I think.

Yeah, it was pretty good. About 20,000, of course, is a different level, but yeah, we will just play our game, the same game we love, Counter-Strike. That's all.
