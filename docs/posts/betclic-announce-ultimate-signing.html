Betclic announce ultimate signing

Roland "⁠ultimate⁠" Tomkowiak has signed for Betclic from Liquid to fill the team's vacant AWPer slot.

The 22-year-old joined Liquid in July 2024 from Illuminar and initially made a splash with his aggressive style of AWPing, but his form deteriorated over time and he was replaced by Georgi "⁠Jorko⁠" Mitev this summer.

ultimate has already joined Betclic's quartet in action this summer when he turned out for Poland and helped them secure qualification to the Esports Nations Cup.
