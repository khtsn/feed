Valve release October VRS update

The 2027 edition of PGL Bucharest will issue invites based on the October ranking

A monthly update to Valve's Regional Standings was released on Monday.

There is no change at the summit, with Spirit retaining the No. 1 ranking, but Legacy surged into second after their FISSURE Playground 3 victory. G2 made their way back into the top five following an impressive month, and Astralis returned to the top 10 for the first time since June.

The October ranking will determine invites to BLAST Rivals S2 and PGL Bucharest 2027, with the invitations set to be distributed as follows:

BLAST Rivals 2026 Season 2 (November 9-15)

  • 8 Global spots to main event

PGL Bucharest 2027 (January 17-24)

  • 14 Global spots to main event

  • 6 Europe spots to European closed qualifier
