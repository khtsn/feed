    MIBR have announced the return of their academy project, MIBR Academy, through the FEITOS NO BRASIL project.

    The program is a tryout that will determine the next players to join the organization's academy, with 40 chosen contestants set to face off across 7 days of competition.

    After the process is over, MIBR will select at least three players to play for their academy.

    You can read more about the project here.

    PGL has disqualified acidhouze from the PGL Bucharest 2027 open qualifier due to competitive integrity concerns.

    Inner Circle Academy played acidhouze during the qualifier but forfeited the match partway through, the organization stating on X that they chose to forfeit "so the cheaters couldn't profit from betting."

    Inner Circle Academy have been reinstated to the qualifier in the wake of acidhouze's disqualification.
