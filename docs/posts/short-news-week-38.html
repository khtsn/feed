Short news: Week 38

In a post on X, ESEA announced that Fever Squad have been disqualified from the ESEA Season 58: European Finals after one of their players was banned for ban evasion.

The Ukrainian team had made it to the semi-finals by beating NAVI Junior 2-1 in the quarter-finals. The affected branch of the bracket will be replayed from Round 3 as a result.

Ninjas in Pyjamas are through to the upper final at the 1win Private Club Season 1 after a come-from-behind win against SINNERS.

The Ninjas completed the turnaround with a 13-0 thrashing on Inferno, where Martin "⁠stavn⁠" Lund posted a career-high 2.87 rating.

The organizers of XSE Pro League Luoyang 2026 have announced the cancellation of the tournament, which was scheduled to be played just before the Singapore Major invite cut-off.

The $50,000 tournament will not take place due to a local goverment event scheduling conflict.

Relog Media have announced TESLA CUP, a $50,000 LAN tournament set to be held in Relog's studio in Belgrade, Serbia.

The event will be played from October 27-29, just before the Singapore Major invite cutoff, with signups opening on October 3.
