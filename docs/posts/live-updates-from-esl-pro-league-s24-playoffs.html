Live updates from ESL Pro League S24 playoffs

Back in 2019, Robin "⁠ropz⁠" Kool and Özgür "⁠woxic⁠" Eker wore the same MOUZ jersey as they swept fnatic 3-0 in Odense. These two young players secured the organization's first ESL Pro League title. It was also the tournament where ropz earned his first HLTV MVP medal.

Nearly 7 years later, both have long since moved on, but they are once again in contention for a Pro League trophy. This time, however, ropz represents Vitality, and woxic plays for Aurora, with only one of the former teammates able to reach the final.

Their old organization remains in the hunt on the other side of the bracket. If MOUZ overcome Spirit, they will face one of the players who helped deliver their first Pro League trophy.

The Spodek has long been more than just a venue for Counter-Strike. In 2014, Virtus.pro defeated Ninjas in Pyjamas here to lift a Major trophy in front of more than 10,000 spectators, with the home crowd becoming an inseparable part of the tournament's story.

Ten years later, the arena witnessed a different kind of breakthrough. At just 17, Danil "⁠donk⁠" Kryshkovets won the title with Spirit and claimed the MVP award on his Big Event. There was no lengthy adjustment period: he immediately delivered a performance that made the entire scene sit up and take notice.

For Dan "⁠apEX⁠" Madesclaire, the journey could hardly have been more different. Before Vitality's victory over The MongolZ in the 2025 semi-finals, he had never won a playoff match in the Spodek. Already 15 years into his professional career, he marvelled afterward that it had taken him 11 years to finally win it. It's a remarkable wait for someone who had already conquered both the Majors and Cologne's stage.

The next day, the team standing between him and the trophy was none other than donk's Spirit.

Vitality swept the final 3-0, finally giving apEX the Katowice title that had eluded him for so long. "I've finished the game," he said afterward. The same stage that had crowned a teenage newcomer a year earlier now belonged to a veteran who had finally fulfilled a long ambition.

Now, both teams are back at the Spodek, each one victory away from another title decider in this arena. The competition has changed from IEM Katowice to ESL Pro League, but the two finalists from 2025 could yet meet again on the same stage.

However, don't forget, Aurora and MOUZ, of course, have no obligation to let that story follow a familiar script. The former face Vitality in the semi-finals, while the latter take on Spirit. Will two former champions meet again, or will someone change the course of the Spodek's next chapter?

Today's semi-finals will provide you the answer.

Finn "⁠karrigan⁠" Andersen, speaking to Freya "⁠Freya⁠" Spiers on broadcast after the match:

On getting exploited on Mirage: "They had a good game plan to make some noise on the map and take some situations and timings. Our communication was not on point, there were multiple times I died on B from the back, and those things were not good enough today. Because of the way they had their game plan and we were not able to communicate on a high level, it made the game very one-sided on the first half. We came closer to coming back on the T side, but in the end we didn't get enough CT rounds."

On donk MP7 round: "I think I made a mistake going too early in Cave, which meant that donk was able to get a better position when they wanted to trade on B. I did a misplay there and put my teammates in a bad position, and then donk kills two more on the MP7. Obviously when you give him those situations, he's going to capitalize because he's the best rifler in the world right now, and that kind of put a stop to the momentum. As you saw, we got the grab of the game again, got close in the end, and that was the story of today: close to a comeback, but not really."

On plan ahead: "We're going into Thunderpick with other good teams, and we have to perform really good there. We're not happy with our season at all. The last two tournaments we got eliminated by Spirit and they're a pretty good team, but on these days we need to play better. We are not playing our best level when we meet these teams, and that's what the result looks like. We need to play way better in Thunderpick and hopefully lift the trophy; the first trophy for us this season."
