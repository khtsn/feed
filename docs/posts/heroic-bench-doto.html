HEROIC bench doto

HEROIC have announced that they have moved head coach Joonas "⁠doto⁠" Forss to the bench, ending the 30-year-old's short tenure with the squad.

doto took over from Tobias "⁠TOBIZ⁠" Theo in July, overseeing the team's title runs at Stake Ranked Episode 3 and Stake Pulse Beat II, as well as the qualifying campaign for IEM Beijing. It was his first time in charge of a tier-one team after previously coaching ENCE Prospects and HEROIC Academy.

"Joonas has delivered great results for us, both with the Academy team and now with the main team," HEROIC CEO Joachim Harg said in a statement. "He has won us two trophies and put us in an excellent position to qualify for the PGL Singapore Major.

"More importantly, he is a genuinely great guy, which made the decision to bench him incredibly difficult. But sometimes you have to make difficult decisions and choose the direction you believe is best for the project in the long term."

In a post on X, doto admitted to being "disappointed" with the decision.

"We won two trophies and put ourselves in a strong position to qualify for the Major," he wrote. "I’m proud of what we achieved together and would have loved to finish what we started.

"From building the academy team to taking over the main roster, thank you to all the players and staff I’ve worked with at HEROIC. Wishing the team the best for the rest of the season.

"I want to keep coaching and am open to discussing new opportunities."

HEROIC, who are currently ranked 17th in the global VRS, will make their next appearance at Stake Pulse Beat III, scheduled for October 12-17. Their lineup now looks as follows:
