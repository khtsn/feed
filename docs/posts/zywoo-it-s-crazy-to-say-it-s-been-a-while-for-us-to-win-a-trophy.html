ZywOo: "It's crazy to say it's been a while for us to win a trophy"

Mathieu "⁠ZywOo⁠" Herbaut was crowned the StarLadder StarSeries Fall 2026's Most Valuable Player for his 1.50-rated performance in Barcelona after a Championship Sunday double-header against FURIA in the consolidation final and Aurora in the grand final.

ZywOo claims uncontested StarSeries Fall MVP

We caught up with Vitality's star after he secured his 33rd career MVP to hear about him and his team's road to the trophy, and what lies ahead.

Listen to the interview here.

All right, I'm here with ZywOo, just crowned MVP of StarSeries Fall. Your 33rd, that's a crazy number. It just keeps going up. But also the first one this season. Last time was in Fort Worth. So how is it to get another one under your belt?

Nah, it feels good and feels [like] a release a bit to have this MVP. I wanted at least one MVP for the second part of the season just to be a bit more confident and to win also a trophy. So I think it will come also with that. So now it feels good to have this MVP, like you said, 33rd. And yeah, I should say I think for this, it's not only me, I think the team played well. So I think I just need to say thanks to them also. Feels good.

And you talk about getting a little bit more confidence. You seemed pretty confident while you were here in Barcelona. It was a great event for you. I heard apEX joking a little bit after the match saying, 'Oh, just let ZywOo go and tap everyone,' and so on. So how was it for you being loose on the server like that?

After the last tournament in Porto, we had a talk individually with almost everyone just to know what our feelings were, and I think it released the thing we had in our back, and I think we all feel better and more confident in a way, of knowing more about each other, about the feeling, what they think about the game, what they think about each other as well.

So after we had this talk, I think for me it was also more to know what we want to do during the game, also outside, and I think it just released a lot of things, a lot of stress, it's more confidence in a way. So I think it helped us to be more, not adult, but to be more professional and to be more like when you need to be into the game, what we need to do to be back at the top level. And I think this tournament, even though we lost yesterday against Aurora, I think we showed a good face overall. So I think that means a lot for us.

And tell me about losing to Aurora yesterday, and coming into it again today. Also, a very long day. It could have been potentially eight maps. You got it done in six. So tell me a little bit about coming in strong, especially in the Anubis today, which you lost yesterday. What changed today? What clicked compared to yesterday?

I think today what's changed is our T side was way better. Yesterday we won like three T rounds and today we were much better as a team, and playing individually. Also on my side I was better and I think everyone was better. So I think we just fixed the mistakes. We watched the mistake yesterday and we just adjusted everything. Yesterday we played too passive. We were just waiting for them to do things. They didn't do anything and we were just, 'Okay, now what do we do?' We were a bit fucked.

But today we're just taking our balls in a way and just playing the game we want to. So Anubis, today was pretty smooth on our side even though it's not our best map and we're working a lot for this, but we know we can be really good on this map when we play together, when we know what to do. So I think, yeah, it's been really good.

And about the six maps, I think everyone was ready to play eight maps already. We could have a really long day. We could play like eight or nine hours in a row, and we were ready to fight at any moment. So I think that's why our mentality was in this way, even though we're winning good maps or anything, it doesn't matter. In the end, we know we can play a lot, so we don't keep energy. We need to grind every round, every map. So that was the thing today.

And how is it also, again, just getting a trophy? You had mezii gone, and you hadn't been, maybe, at your best, at your peak. But now you got to raise the trophy here. How was it getting another win?

I think it just also released a lot for us, not the pressure, but just to win one more trophy again because people are saying maybe, not that we are done, but maybe we are done. We're not going to win anymore or whatever because we don't play the same. But I think this tournament showed we're still one of the best teams.

We can show a really great face even though sometimes we have some drop down; it makes sense, we're human, but we just need to realize each other, we play on each other, that when we have some difficulty, just to talk to our teammate and to talk with the team. That helped a lot.

So that's why we're looking forward to that, and of course, we released a lot after this trophy because even mezii is back, and I think for him it's also good to be at the high level because he played really good this final as well. So I think everyone was really happy for him and for the performance. So yeah, I think overall we're happy. We lifted another trophy. I think it's been three months. It's crazy to say it's been a while for us to win a trophy, but we're happy for that.

And then just to close it out, you have EPL next. So that's a couple of weeks away. Are you going to take a little minute to catch your breath and then get back into it? What are your plans for EPL and the time between?

I think we're just going to practice again. Because after that it's Bucharest and we're going to play with jL again. So it doesn't matter if we won this one, I think we want to improve again and improve every day. So anyway, our main focus is the Major, so if we can be more confident, more ready for that, doesn't matter if it's EPL, Bucharest or... we're here to win, we're here to improve every day. So that's going to be the main goal. We're going to see what was the mistakes during this tournament and fix it. That's how it's going to be again.
