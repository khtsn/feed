Maden steps in for GamerLegion in IEM Beijing closed qualifier

GamerLegion will play the IEM Beijing closed qualifier with Pavle "⁠Maden⁠" Bošković instead of Sebastian "⁠Tauson⁠" Tauson Lindelof, who will be "unavailable due to health reasons," the organization said.

The 21-year-old Dane later revealed he has been hospitalized for five days with reactive arthritis and that he hopes to return to play in time for next week's FISSURE Playground 3.

The qualifier will take place online from September 2-4 and have two spots at November's IEM Beijing on offer.

The 16-team competition will be played in a double-elimination format, with the Tauson-less GamerLegion facing Nuclear TigeRES in the opening match.
