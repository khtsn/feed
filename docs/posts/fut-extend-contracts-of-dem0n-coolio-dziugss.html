FUT extend contracts of dem0n, coolio, dziugss

The runners-up at the Esports World Cup have renewed the deals of three key members.

FUT have renewed the contracts of Dmytro "⁠dem0n⁠" Myroshnychenko, András "⁠coolio⁠" Fercsák, and Džiugas "⁠dziugss⁠" Steponavičius in the wake of their rise to number three on the VRS.

Since signing from NAVI Junior, FUT have established themselves in tier one and most recently placed second at the Esports World Cup.

The Turkish organization did not give an update on the contracts of Aulon "⁠Krabeni⁠" Fazlija or Nikita "⁠cmtry⁠" Samolotov, who joined from NAVI at the same time as the now-extended trio.

HLTV have contacted FUT for a comment on the status of that duo, and asked how long the trio have extended the deals for.
