1win bench team

The whole starting lineup has been moved to the inactive roster.

1win have announced that their whole starting five has been moved to the bench, making a victory over los kogutos in Thunderpick World Championship 2026 Europe Series 2 and a loss to Butterfly at CCT 2026 Europe Series 5 on Thursday their last matches before the announcement.
