max: "We are not confident right now and we are not making good decisions"

9z are enduring a poor period of form to kick off the new season, losing four matches in a row amidst early exits from both Esports World Cup and now BLAST Open Porto. Considering their top-eight finish at IEM Cologne Major and trophy-winning campaign at XSE Pro League mere months ago, this is a dramatic fall from grace.

"I don't know to be honest what is happening," Maximiliano "⁠max⁠" Gonzalez admitted when speaking to HLTV after his team's loss to Vitality. "We are not confident right now and we are not making good decisions, and at the same time we are forgetting tactics, like we are doing shit in a lot of rounds. We forget what we need to do and we are not adapting to what the round is asking of us."

What do you think went wrong for you in that series?

Everything. We are not playing how we like to play, it's a very tough start to the season for us. I don't know, to be honest, what is happening, but we are going to resolve and try to go back to our real level.

Do you think it's a mental problem, a tactical problem? What issues do you see in the server right now.

I think it's a mix. We are not confident right now and we are not making good decisions, and at the same time we are forgetting tactics, like we are doing shit in a lot of rounds. We forget what we need to do and we are not adapting to what the round is asking of us. So yeah, it's a mix.

Are you surprised? Because obviously you had such a good Major and won XSE Pro League.

Yeah a little bit. I didn't expect we were going to play like this in this tournament, but it's time to rest a little bit right now and see what is happening, discuss, and look forward.

To play against the best teams in the world every day is not easy

What's next for you now?

I don't know if it's public but we are going to China to play FISSURE. I think it's a very important tournament for us to gain confidence again, because we are playing against the best teams in the world right now. It was not the same in the first months of this year, so we need to get confidence again and go back to our real level.

Do you think it's easier to get back to your real level with lots of officials, or would you rather have a bit of time off to practice?

For us it's good to play tournaments, but to play against the best teams in the world every day is not easy, and we are not on the same level right now. I think for us it's very important to play against tier two or tier three teams to get confidence again, and go back to tier one.
