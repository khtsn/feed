5star settle for third as Chinggis Warriors win ESN Fall Showdown over The Huns

The ESN Fall Showdown has come to an end in Mongolia, with Chinggis Warriors beating The Huns 2-0 in the grand final to secure the title.

5star, the highest-rated Mongolian squad in the VRS after The MongolZ, let an opportunity to continue surging in the ranking after their big victories over The MongolZ and Astralis at FISSURE Playground 3 slip away as they lost to Chinggis Warriors in the upper bracket final and then fell to The Huns in the consolidation final.

The squad led by Uuganbayar "⁠NEUZ⁠" Nyam-Otgon secured 63 points at the event and currently have 1097 to slot in fifth place in the Asia VRS — the last invite spot — behind Lynn Vision's 1185.

Chinggis Warriors gained much ground with 334 points, jumping to seventh in place in Asia with 1074, while The Huns added 139 and sit in 11th place with 935 points.

Chinggis Warriors and The Huns will compete next in the online ESL Challenger League Season 52 APAC Cup 3 starting on October 7, while 5star's next scheduled event for the time being is the four-team GLuck Moscow Cyber Games on October 31-November 1 with an imposing $181,214 prize pool.
