Legacy take third place at EWC over FURIA

try ended the tournament with a notable 1.26 rating in 14 maps

Legacy beat FURIA 2-1 in the Esports World Cup's third-place decider despite losing their Ancient pick, 9-13, before turning it around with back-to-back 13-9 scores on Nuke and the Dust2 decider.

The bronze match, often a humdrum affair, was brightened by the national clash between two Brazilian-majority teams that last faced off at BLAST Open London, where FURIA won 2-1, adding the possibility of revenge to Legacy's turnaround in the final two maps of the series.

Legacy's Ancient pick kicked off the match, but FURIA drew first blood off the back of two Danil "⁠molodoy⁠" Golubenko entries on the attack, winning the first pistol round and taking a 4-0 lead.

Legacy finally settled in and won five to keep it close at the half, and then held long enough against the retake to allow the bomb to go off in pistols despite molodoy taking down Santino "⁠try⁠" Rigal and Andrei "⁠arT⁠" Piovezan in the 1v2.

Legacy briefly took the lead, 9-8, but as FURIA got proper equipment and an economy going, they quickly locked it down, with Kaike "⁠KSCERATO⁠" Cerato and molodoy leading the defense to a 13-9 victory.

FalleN tries to kickstar FURIA's defense on Nuke

The map turned to Nuke, and FURIA once again got the early lead as they looked to end the series on their pick, but Legacy didn't give up in the bronze match and rallied to a 7-5 lead at the half.

try started the second half with triples in the pistol round and the anti-forcebuy as Legacy took an 11-5 lead, but a big triple in a B defense by Gabriel "⁠FalleN⁠" Toledo followed by a molodoy quad kept FURIA alive.

Vinicius "⁠n1ssim⁠" Pereira then had a big round of his own with four kills to reach map point as FURIA crumbled in their comeback attempt. Two rounds later, the series was headed to Dust2.

FURIA once again were first out of the gate, with a 4-1 lead on the decider, but Legacy yet again brought it back on the defense, this time to take another 7-5 lead at the half.

n1ssim, one of Legacy's quiet performers at EWC with a 1.11 rating at the tournament, had big swing rounds in the second half, helping clear the way for Legacy to win the map out, 13-9. He averaged a +8.29% swing on Dust2 and averaged a 1.70 rating.

try was the highest-rated player in the series overall, with a 1.33 rating, capping a great tournament in which he averaged a 1.26 in 14 maps as the team's top performer at the event.
