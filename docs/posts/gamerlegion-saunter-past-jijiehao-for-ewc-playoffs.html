GamerLegion saunter past JiJieHao for EWC playoffs

GamerLegion are the first team into the Esports World Cup playoffs after defeating JiJieHao 2-0 (13-3, 13-8).

Janusz "⁠Snax⁠" Pogorzelski's troops began the season by getting knocked out of Bounty in the first round by HOTU, but they have shaken off their ring rust to dominate their first two matches of EWC with a 74% round win rate.

FL4MUS: "I'm feeling more relieved. It's probably my first [time] feeling that I'm finally back on a team and able to play CS"

Returning rifler Timur "⁠FL4MUS⁠" Marev spoke of his "relief" to be back in a stable project afer a tough time with Virtus.pro and short stints with OG and BetBoom, and he was a force to be reckoned with on Dust2 as he posted a 2.33 rating.

The 21-year-old set the tone with an instant Dualies 4k in the first pistol of the series, and followed it up with a brilliant M4A4 spraydown and the closing kill of the 13-3 paddling.

Ancient began in a similar vein, although JiJieHao did pick up a smattering of rounds later on. Sebastian "⁠Tauson⁠" Tauson Lindelof in particular was excellent, following up his flying one-tap from Dust2 with a great 3k entry in map two and a 1.80 rating.

GamerLegion now progress to playoffs with their confidence boosted, while JJH are left wondering what happened to the team that beat the VRS #1 just yesterday.
