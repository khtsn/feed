CYBERSHOKE sign nota and H4SAN4TOR

CYBERSHOKE have announced the signings of Emil "⁠nota⁠" Moskvitin and Vladislav "⁠H4SAN4TOR⁠" Tuchin, the new arrivals set to replace the recently-benched duo of Aleksandr "⁠glowiing⁠" Matsievich and David "⁠bl1x1⁠" Stepanyants.

nota, the headline signing of the pair, joins on loan with an option to buy. The 19-year-old established himself with PARIVISION over the course of the last 18 months, grinding through the obscurity of Europe's online scene to make it to tier-one tournaments.

The peak of nota's time with PARIVISION came at the end of 2025 and beginning of 2026, when the team won a string of online events and made it to Stage 3 of StarLadder Budapest Major, before capping things off with a victory at BLAST Bounty S1 and a second-place finish at PGL Cluj-Napoca.

nota turned heads during PARIVISION's most-successful stint, finding himself just shy of the MVP award after an impressive final at BLAST Bounty S1, but his form dropped off a cliff after that and he was benched during PARIVISION's revamp in the most recent player break.

H4SAN4TOR arrives for free on a full contract having played for ex-RUBY across 2026 thus far. The rifler was a consistent presence for his team, producing a 1.07 average rating as ex-RUBY made regular deep runs in online competitions, winning a few notable ones like CCT Season 3 Europe Series 17.

The 21-year-old has enjoyed an impressive upturn in form in recent times, posting a 1.16 rating or better in five of his six most recent events.
