The first match of the day pitted a white-hot LOUD against NRG, who were making their Stage 2 playoff debut. The extra days of rest gave NRG a boost, allowing the North American squad to slow LOUD's recent momentum and close out the series 2–1.

The series started on LOUD's pick of Ascent, where they looked at home with a rousing 3-0 start. A patented Odin 3k from brawk led to an eco win for NRG and a subsequent run. LOUD finished the half winning only two rounds, trailing 7-5 at the break. NRG took control in the second half with a stifling defense, allowing only two rounds and claiming victory on a map they had not yet played in Americas Stage 2. brawk stood out with a server-high 2.0 rating and a 26/11/8 KDA on Sova.

brawk's Odin eliminates three

Abyss recently came back to the competitive pool, and NRG wasted no time picking it. NRG had found plenty of success on the map before its rotation out of the pool, posting a 67% win rate. LOUD entered Abyss aiming to put a dent in that number.

Both squads showcased different double-Duelist compositions. tkzin led LOUD on Waylay while lukxo played Yoru for the first time in his career. mada and keiko continued their Phoenix and Jett shenanigans from Ascent. The two squads traded blows throughout the first half, entering the break deadlocked at 6-6.

NRG held down both A and B sites on defense, winning five of the first seven rounds. The match looked over, but LOUD rediscovered their group-stage form. A flawless A-site retake, a 3v5 clutch, and a final lukxo 3k fueled five straight rounds, completing the comeback 13-11. Heroics from erde and lukxo, including a combined 50 kills, forced a deciding third map. Despite the loss, mada led the server with a 1.62 rating.

lukxo takes three kills on Yoru

Lotus settled the series. LOUD's momentum from Abyss carried over, as the squad claimed the pistol round and the next three rounds behind a monstrous early performance from lukxo on his resident Chamber pick.

lukxo gets an eco ace and celebrates

NRG adapted to LOUD's default playstyle, locking down multiple executes across all three sites to take the half 8-4. Very little changed after the side swap, as NRG took a page out of LOUD's book, stealing the first four rounds of the half. A round 17 win gave LOUD a glimmer of hope for another comeback, but NRG crushed it, closing the map 13-5.

mada stole the show across the series, posting a 1.39 rating and 59/40/15 KDA. NRG will meet 100 Thieves in São Paulo where the winner will move on to the grand final.
