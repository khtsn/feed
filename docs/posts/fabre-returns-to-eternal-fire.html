Fabre returns to Eternal Fire

Eternal Fire have announced the return of Sezgin "⁠Fabre⁠" Kalaycı as the organization's head coach, replacing the recently departed Yiğit "⁠yT⁠" Özden.

Fabre coached Eternal Fire's Turkish roster from 2022 to 2025, leaving the organization when the entire lineup made the switch to Aurora in April 2025.

He oversaw two Major playoff appearances by the squad, one under the Eternal Fire moniker at PGL CS2 Major Copenhagen, where they reached the quarters, and one with Aurora's semi-final appearance at the IEM Cologne Major.

In Eternal Fire, Fabre reunites with Samet "⁠jottAAA⁠" Köklü and Caner "⁠soulfly⁠" Kesici, both of whom he coached in Aurora, but will now stand behind the first English-speaking roster of his career.

Eternal Fire, currently ranked No. 67 in the VRS, are now:

Caner "⁠soulfly⁠" Kesici

Sezgin "⁠Fabre⁠" Kalaycı (coach)
