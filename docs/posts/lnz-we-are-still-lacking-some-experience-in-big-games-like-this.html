LNZ: "We are still lacking some experience in big games like this"

MIBR's first semi-final with their new lineup ended in painful fashion as Legacy recovered from a deficit on Inferno and closed out the series 2-1, overturning a 2v5 in the final round of overtime.

Speaking to HLTV after the defeat, Linus "⁠LNZ⁠" Holtäng pointed to a lack of experience in high-pressure matches and an off individual day as the difference between the two teams.

The Swedish captain also revisited the five-player balance he called for in June, explaining that MIBR still need to improve how they convert early advantages and collectively react to different situations.

You built an early lead on Inferno but couldn't hold onto it, and overtime went back and forth. What stopped the team from closing out the match?

I think we are still lacking some experience in big games like this. This was the first semi-final we've played with this lineup, and maybe none of us had the kind of individual day we usually have. They were hitting harder shots and making fewer mistakes than us at the end of the day.

Back in June, you said you didn't want MIBR to rely on one or two players having a perfect game. On Nuke, MIBR won the opening-kill battle 14-9, but much of the impact still came from insani and nqz. Why didn't those early advantages turn into enough round wins, and is that five-man balance still a work in progress?

I think with tomaszin still new to the team, and because we haven't played that much with nqz, it's still difficult to play these situations perfectly. But I think we're getting there. We just need to go through some trial and error, and we'll learn from it before the next event.

Today we felt closer than ever to beating them

Linus "⁠LNZ⁠" Holtäng

You played Alliance on this stage yesterday, while Legacy advanced directly to the semi-finals and had an extra day to prepare. With only one week of practice with tomaszin, did that extra match help sharpen the new lineup, or did it leave you with too little time to update your preparation for such a familiar opponent?

I think it was fine. It wasn't so much that we didn't have enough time to prepare. It's more that we haven't played much with the new lineup, and we need to get better at playing together and knowing how to react in different situations.

It's the seventh time that you've faced Legacy this year and your roster has changed during that period. How would you assess them as an opponent at this point?

I think they're a bit of a joker in a way. They can pull out any round at any time, they're always really tough to play against, and they have really strong individuals. It's always a difficult game, and you can't always read them.

Maybe today we felt closer than ever to beating them compared to the other times we've lost. We also tried to approach our game plan a little differently this time, and I think it worked. But at the end of the day, we still didn't convert all the situations.
