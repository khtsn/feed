Map one was Split, a historically good map for Nongshim at internationals. While both teams ran the same comp of double-Duelist with Neon and Yoru alongside double-Controller of Omen and Viper, the Vitality side reintroduced Chronicle on Neon. Vitality earned the first pistol win, but Nongshim used Bandits to immediately convert the eco. That placed Vitality in a horrid spot financially, as Francis and Rb seized that lead. Vitality were able to balance the round as much as they could, only down 7-5 at the half.

Though, when Vitality took the second-half pistol, the only round they lost in the second half was the bonus round. Everyone on Vitality had their time to shine, as the EMEA representatives finished map one 13-8.

Abyss was a footnote in this best-of-three, as despite this being a Vitality map pick, Nongshim wiped the floor with them. Once again, Vitality won the opening pistol and were immediately ecoed, but Nongshim truly pushed that advantage on this map. Ivy on Omen was cooking, and Xross on the attack side was to be feared. Despite losing pistol, Nongshim entered the half up 8-4, and ended the second half perfectly to complete a quick map two 13-4.

The final map of Haven was an interesting final map, as Nongshim were clearly favored on it over Vitality based on previous matches. Vitality did change their comp up for the match, running double-Duelist with Neon and Yoru alongside Derke on Chamber, but the true factor was their pistol round dominance. Taking the first, Vitality was able to hold in a half that Nongshim looked strong on, attacking B site as frequently as the team did against Karmine Corp. Vitality were able to keep the half moderately close, entering the half down only 7-5.

Then, with another pistol round win and a bonus conversion, Vitality just needed to keep the pressure on the Santiago champions to secure the map and series. An eco from Nongshim offered hope, then a 9-9 tie was followed up by a late Francis A long hold to revitalize the Pacific squad. Rounds kept trading back and forth, reaching another tie at 11-11 with Nongshim on a force that could lose them the series. A hero would rise, and while Jamppi started with a 1v1 post-plant, a genius Killjoy ultimate took the map to overtime, punishing the positioning from Vitality.

Derke survives and thrives against Nongshim in OT.

Overtime began with Derke living longer than anyone expected, nailing shots with the Operator to push EMEA ahead. Nongshim tried to match that with two glass cannon Operators on defense, but an early spam kill shut that down. Vitality earned a victory on Haven 14-12, taking the 2-1 series win with an entertaining map three.
