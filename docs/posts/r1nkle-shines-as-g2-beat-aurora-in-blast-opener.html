r1nkle shines as G2 beat Aurora in BLAST opener

Artem "⁠r1nkle⁠" Moroz was the difference as G2 ensured Aurora's international era stayed firmly on the ground with a 2-0 win.

Inferno was a convincing win in favor of G2, 13-5, but the crucial map was Anubis where Aurora led 12-7 only to lose 12-16 after a stunning collapse.

Key to that reversal of fortune was r1nkle, who proved his explosive potential once again by posting 32 frags and a 1.86 rating on map two.

Eetu "⁠sAw⁠" Saha spoke of his side needing an aggressive sniper to make up for their relatively passive rifle trio, and Anubis showcased how effective r1nkle's style can be.

sAw: "We needed someone more aggressive"

On CT side, r1nkle kicked off the half with an ace on the FAMAS and then picked up his AWP to post a 23-4 record on the defence, popping up all over the map as he put his freedom to use.

It may have come against a disjointed Aurora side whose engine is still refusing to come online but, as a blueprint, the benefits of G2's change of sniper were laid bare for all to see.
