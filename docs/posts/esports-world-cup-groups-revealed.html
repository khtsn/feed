Esports World Cup groups revealed

Two Last Chance Qualifier teams landed in Group A

The organizers of the Esports World Cup unveiled the group distribution for the $2 million main event shortly after the final four teams were determined through the Last Chance Qualifier.

The 32 participating teams have been seeded into four groups of eight based on their placing on the July edition of Valve Regional Standings.

That has notably turned out with two of the four Last Chance Qualifier teams, BIG and JiJieHao, landing in Group A thanks to BIG's standing ahead of several teams who had been directly invited to the event as of the previous month's VRS.

The group stage of the event will kick off on Wednesday, August 12 and run through to Sunday, August 16, and be played in a double-elimination format with the opening matches played in best-of-ones and the remainder in best-of-threes.

The top four teams from each group will move on to the single-elimination playoffs, taking place from August 19-23 and culminating with the grand final in the Accor Arena.
