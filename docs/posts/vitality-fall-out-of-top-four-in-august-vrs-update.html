Vitality fall out of top four in August VRS update

For the first time in two years, Vitality fall outside of the top four in a monthly VRS update.

Valve has released the August update to the VRS, with a raft of tournaments set to use the update to dole out invites.

Of note is Vitality's drop to fifth in the Global VRS, marking the first time the team have been outside the top four in two years, since the August 2024 update. Despite their slide, Vitality are of course well within invite range for all significant events.

Several $1,000,000+ events are to deliver their VRS invites based on the August update, namely FISSURE Playground 3, ESL Pro League Season 24, Thunderpick World Championship and IEM Beijing. A number of notable tier-two events will also be sending their invites out based on this update, all of which could have implications for Major qualification.

Here is how the invites break down for the events:

FISSURE Playground 3 (September 7-13)

  • 15 Global spots to main event

Logitech G Play Connect (September 17-23)

  • 10 Global spots to main event

  • 8 Global spots to playoffs of closed qualifier

  • 10 Global spots to group stage of closed qualifier

Stake Ranked Episode 4 (October 1-4)

  • Six Global spots to main event

  • 10 Global spots to closed qualifier

ESL Pro League Season 24 (October 3-11)

  • 12 Global spots to main event

  • 12 Global spots to closed qualifier

  • 4 Asia spots to Asia closed qualifier

Thunderpick World Championship (October 12-19)

  • 7 Global spots to main event

  • 14 Europe spots to closed qualifier

  • 1 North America, 1 South America spot to closed qualifier

IEM Beijing (November 2-8)

  • 10 Global spots to main event

  • 1 North America, 1 South America, and 1 Asia spot to main event

  • 12 Global spots to closed qualifier

  • 4 Asia spots to Asia closed qualifier
