tacitus joins Imperial as head coach

Imperial have announced that they have named Marcos "⁠tacitus⁠" Castilho the new head coach of their CS team, filling the slot left vacant by Rafael "⁠zakk⁠" Fernandes's departure in mid-June.

tacitus had been without a job since February, when he was dismissed by Fluxo after almost two years in charge. He was later replaced by Gustavo "⁠Juve⁠" Alexandre, who is mostly known for his time coaching Into the Breach.

Imperial had a positive start to the new season, winning their first two tournaments with Guilherme "⁠saadzin⁠" Pacheco, CCT 2026 South America Series 3 and the Thunderpick World Championship 2026 South America Series 2.
