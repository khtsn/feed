Vitality move past PARIVISION to make EPL semi

Vitality were in top form as they demolished PARIVISION in the ESL Pro League Season 24 quarter-final with a 2-0 scoreline (Inferno 13-2, Dust2 13-10).

Shahar "⁠flameZ⁠" Shushan and company now move to the semi-final, where they will face Aurora, who breezed past 1win earlier on Friday.

The series began on Inferno, where the teams were embroiled in force-buy wars early on. Vitality prevailed and took a 3-2 lead after breaking PARIVISION's economy, which opened the floodgates.

A streak of eleven straight rounds gave Vitality an incredibly one-sided 13-2 victory, during which PARIVISION totaled 21 kills across the team, while flameZ and William "⁠mezii⁠" Merriman both finished the map with 2.00+ ratings.

Dust2 began in similar fashion to the opener, with Vitality running away to a 6-0 lead, but PARIVISION began showing some signs of life at the end of the half.

Vladislav "⁠xiELO⁠" Lysov, who was his team's shining light throughout, found some crucial openings and multi-kills to start PARIVISION's comeback, with Abay "⁠HObbit⁠" Khassenov's 1vs2 reducing the deficit to two.

Still, Vitality weathered the storm before Mathieu "⁠ZywOo⁠" Herbaut took matters into his own hands with an aggressive 3k to win the map 13-10.
