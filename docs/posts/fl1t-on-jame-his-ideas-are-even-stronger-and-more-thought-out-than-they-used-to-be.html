The rifler believes leading a younger roster has refreshed Jame, bringing new ideas and a more hands-on approach to guiding his teammates.

Speaking to HLTV ahead of FISSURE Playground 3, FL1T discussed his reunion with the Major-winning duo, his responsibilities alongside three younger players, and the challenges of returning from the bench.

I have to be more focused on the team, helping the team, helping the young players

You reunited with Jame. What's different in how he led back when you played together in Virtus.pro, versus now?

There are differences because the game's meta keeps changing. There are some interesting fresh new ideas and fresh new tricks. He's still growing as a captain, as a leader, and he's had new young players.

You can feel that he's refreshed. He's more controlling of the young players in certain areas. His ideas are even stronger and more thought out than they used to be.

How does your role in PARIVISION differ from your role in Virtus.pro?

It's different in that we already have three young players. [Then] there's me, Jame, dastan, and in some sense I'm probably now less focused on myself. I have to be more focused on the team, helping the team, helping the young players.

Overall, in terms of the role, if we're talking about me specifically, it's basically all the same. I just need to get kills, help the young players, and help with mid-round and end-of-round calls.

You spent a certain amount of time on the bench. Was it hard to get back into the game, into matches?

Honestly, it probably wasn't that hard, because the whole time I kept up my in-game form. It was hard in terms of communication and in terms of the round meta.

When you're not playing competitively, you sort of fall out of the meta a bit. You no longer know all the rounds in as much detail, and there were issues with that. But overall, in terms of how it felt, it was actually pretty comfortable.

During the time you spent on the bench, you maybe had the chance to reflect a bit. Did you make any adjustments to your game?

Yes, absolutely. Because that time actually turned out to be productive for me. I thought about a lot of things, and I arrived at a lot of new conclusions. We'll see how it works out in the future. For now, there's probably no point in talking about it. If everything turns out solid, I'll tell about it [laughs]. That's how it is.
