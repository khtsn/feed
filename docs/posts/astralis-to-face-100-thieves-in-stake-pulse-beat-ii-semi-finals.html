Astralis to face 100 Thieves in Stake Pulse Beat II semi-finals

The Stake Pulse Beat II field has been whittled down to its final four teams after a quarter-final round where all the favorites went through.

HEROIC and Astralis had to come from behind to beat fnatic and Betclic, respectively, with the Danish-majority team completing the turnaround with a 13-0 thrashing on Cache.

In the semi-final stage, Astralis will lock horns with 100 Thieves, who moved past BBL with back-to-back 13-9 victories. It will mark Nicolai "⁠device⁠" Reedtz's first match against his former team since he departed for 100 Thieves at the start of the year.

Meanwhile, HEROIC have been pitted against Sangal, who beat JiJieHao in two maps with Bartosz "⁠bnox⁠" Niebisz leading the charge (1.47 series rating).
