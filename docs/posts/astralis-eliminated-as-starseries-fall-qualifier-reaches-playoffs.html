Astralis eliminated as StarSeries Fall qualifier reaches playoffs

Astralis have been eliminated from the European closed qualifier for StarSeries Fall following a 0-2 loss to HEROIC in Group B's decider match.

Three top-5 teams to attend StarSeries Fall

The top two teams from each group will now enter the single-elimination playoffs, which will be played out over the weekend. The opening round will be headlined by a clash between Ninjas in Pyjamas and HEROIC in a rematch of last week's Stake Ranked Episode 3 upper bracket final.

Two teams will lock in spots at StarSeries Fall, joining Vitality, Natus Vincere, FURIA, MOUZ and the winners of the North American and South American qualifiers at the $500,000 LAN in September.
