Perfecto enters free agency

Virtus.pro have announced that Ilya "⁠Perfecto⁠" Zalutskiy has departed the organization and become a free agent, ending his year-long stint.

"Since joining the Bears in June 2025, Perfecto has represented our colors at the highest level of CS2," Virtus.pro's post announcing Perfecto's departure said. "We'd like to thank him for his professionalism, dedication, and everything he brought to the team."

The announcement ends Perfecto's time with Virtus.pro which began in June 2025, when he was signed to fill the roster spot left vacant by the benching of Timur "⁠FL4MUS⁠" Marev.

Virtus.pro were already a team in decline upon the signing of Perfecto and the tenured Russian did little to arrest their slide, with the team struggling and suffering early exits in nearly every event they attended, even after he took over as the team's IGL when Denis "⁠electroNic⁠" Sharipov was benched.
