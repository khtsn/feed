AW: "We're not ready to actually win tournaments; I think we'll get there"

magic have steadily been building a name for themselves in recent times, putting together playoff runs at the likes of PGL Astana and Esports World Cup, whilst quietly collecting a solid resume of wins in the process.

Unfortunately for the young squad, they were unable to build upon their momentum at FISSURE Playground 3, finding themselves unable to get past Alliance into the playoffs despite taking down FaZe and PARIVISION earlier in the tournament.

"Damn, I'm upset," Andrey "⁠AW⁠" Anisimov admitted to HLTV in the wake of his team's elimination in China. "Individually I played poorly at this tournament. I think we should have been winning, but if we should have won, we would have won, right?"

The 20-year-old was disappointed in his team's campaign, but remained hopeful for the future. "You always want more, but I guess for now that means we're not ready to actually win tournaments, to play at that high a level consistently. I think we'll get there, it just takes time."

This interview was conducted in Russian and translated by HLTV.

Tell me about your impressions about your result at this event. Are you satisfied overall or not really?

Damn, I'm upset. First of all, individually I played poorly at this tournament. I think we should have been winning, but if we should have won, we would have won, right? We lost to Alliance in the playoffs. It's a shame but we need to move forward, there are more tournaments, and keep working on the maps, that's it.

During the on-stage interview you said that you were looking forward to this match against PARIVISION. Why were you looking forward to it?

I guess we still parted ways with PARIVISION on good terms, but I wanted to beat that team because I played there. Not that it's fundamentally important, but there's a lot more interest because of the fact that I played there. It's like playing against friends and acquittances, you always want to win more.

I didn't say anything bad

So it was more interest of that format, 'I played there, it's interesting to be on the other side,' or 'I played there, now I'm on another team, and I want to show what I'm capable of'?

I guess it's more just about playing, with the interest of seeing the strength of the former team versus the current one. That's probably it more than anything.

At the end of last year, when you were benched by PARIVISION, how did you picture your future? Could you have imagined that fairly soon you'd be traveling to big LANs with a new team?

Probably not, honestly. But I believed in it and worked with my team to achieve it, and somehow we got here. That's it.

I was there during the handshakes with PARIVISION, and the handshake with dastan felt personal, so to speak. It looked as if you had proved something in that moment.

Damn, maybe, it could be... Honestly I didn't say anything like that to dastan, I just understand that he'll obviously be upset about losing, just like I'm upset now that we lost. I probably could have said nothing, but it can be perceived differently, troll or not, but I didn't say anything bad. Maybe I shouldn't have said it, because we won, but I think dastan will understand and take it well over time. I don't think it'll be today, but over time he'll understand it was just banter. Anyway, we lost right after them too, so there's not much difference, because if you don't win the tournament, only one team ends up happy in the end.

I think we'll make it to the Major

How satisfied are you overall with your team's results recently? What do you think about the level you've managed to reach?

You always want more, but I guess for now that means we're not ready to actually win tournaments, to play at that high a level consistently. I think we'll get there, it just takes time. That's it.

What specifically do you feel your team is missing? What do you need to work on?

Honestly, probably on veto. We only pick Ancient because we're very confident on it, but I guess it's just the maps and that's it.

What are your plans until the end of the year? How do you see it?

I don't know. We want to qualify for the Major, but I think we did well by winning those two games, because we had a gap in points, and now we're fairly high up. I think we'll make it to the Major, and that's probably the main goal. That's about it.
