Sources: NIP exploring new IGL options

Snappi's time with the Ninjas is coming to an end

Ninjas in Pyjamas are scouring the market for new IGLs as they will replace Marco "⁠Snappi⁠" Pfeiffer after a run of mixed results, multiple sources told HLTV and Dust2 Brasil.

HLTV understands that the decision to bring in a new IGL was mutual, and that Snappi had previously said he would step down from the roster if the organization wished to go in a different direction. Sources added that the 36-year-old is open to transitioning to a new role within the scene.

According to Dust2 Brasil, MIBR turned down Ninjas in Pyjamas' offer for Linus "⁠LNZ⁠" Holtäng, who has been with the Brazilian organization since January.

Sources added that the Ninjas are also weighing up their options to permanently fill the vacancy created by Artem "⁠cairne⁠" Mushynskyi's benching in June. Academy player Roman "⁠n0te⁠" Hamze has been playing for the team since then on an interim basis.

Ninjas in Pyjamas are currently ranked 20th in the Europe VRS, with no tier-1 event invites on the horizon. They recently crashed out of the Esports World Cup in 17th-24th place following losses to Legacy and Astralis and a win against BetBoom.

Previously, the team placed 12th-14th at XSE Pro League, 5th-8th in BLAST Premier Rising, 3rd at Stake Ranked Episode 3, 9th-16th in BLAST Bounty, and 3rd-4th in the closed qualifier for StarSeries Fall.

Snappi has been on Ninjas in Pyjamas' books since January 2025, when the organization rebuilt the squad around Artem "⁠r1nkle⁠" Moroz, who was recently sold to G2.

During his time at the helm, the veteran captain has led the Ninjas to the grand final of StarSeries Fall 2025, a Stage 2 appearance at the Budapest Major, and a top-12 finish at IEM Cologne 2025.

Ninjas in Pyjamas will return to action at Logitech G Play Connect, a $100,000 tournament in Warsaw from September 17-23. The team has also signed up for ROG Journey Autumn in early October.

Ninjas in Pyjamas declined to comment when contacted by HLTV.
