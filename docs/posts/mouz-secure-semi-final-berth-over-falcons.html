MOUZ secure semi-final berth over Falcons

MOUZ came out victorious from the Group B seeding match against Falcons 2-1 to secure a berth in the BLAST Open Porto semi-finals.

Falcons will start the playoffs, which will move from BLAST's studios in Copenhagen to Porto's Super Bock Arena from Friday, from the quarter-finals.

The series got off to a one-sided start with Falcons turning up to play on the opponents' pick of Nuke, breaking MOUZ's seven-map win streak there off the back of a dominant defense that saw the entire quintet firing on all cylinders.

A surprising Ancient pick came back to bite Falcons next, however, as their woes on the map continued after they had also lost it to Lynn Vision and Legacy at the same tournament.

MOUZ challenged Falcons' Mid takes and came out on top time and time again as no one stepped up to the plate on the other side to create any space, which led to a swift win for Dorian "⁠xertioN⁠" Berman's men.

And the deciding Inferno was much of the same. Once again starting on the CT side, MOUZ punished a struggling Nikola "⁠NiKo⁠" Kovač in Banana over and over with spot-on utility and timely Car retakes, while on the other side Ádám "⁠torzsi⁠" Torzsás kept shutting down attempts to gain some space in Mid and Brackets.

Oldřich "⁠PR⁠" Nový then shut down Falcons' last-ditch attempt to push down Middle in the second-half pistol as MOUZ raced to a 13-3 win.
