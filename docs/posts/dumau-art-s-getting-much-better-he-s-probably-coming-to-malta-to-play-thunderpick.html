Legacy are taking Pro League as it comes with their in-game leader's absence.

Legacy are flying high after their FISSURE Playground victory saw them rise to number two in the VRS.

They should be anyway, but their momentum has been dented by Andrei "⁠arT⁠" Piovezan contracting meningitis.

Eduardo "⁠dumau⁠" Wolkmer called the news a "big shock" and "tough" as he explained to HLTV about how an old teammate of his, Matheus "⁠bruttJ⁠" Queiroz had previously died from the same illness.

Fortunately, arT is on the mend, and Legacy can set their sights back on in-game matters for the Thunderpick World Championship later this month, with ESL Pro League something of a free hit with stand-in analyst Pedro "⁠bobz⁠" Rocha.
