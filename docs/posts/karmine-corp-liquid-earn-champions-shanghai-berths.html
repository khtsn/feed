The day began with the upper final between FUT and KC, who had both stormed through the upper bracket. While FUT had already qualified to Champions based on points, this series meant everything to KC—win, and you're in; lose twice in a row, you're out.

The series started on KC's pick of Lotus, where they were undefeated thus far in Stage 2. They quickly demonstrated why with an overwhelming 10-2 attack half, outdueling FUT and working their way into polished postplants. N4RRATE continued to dominate individually on Chamber, finding 17 kills and four first bloods in the first half alone. FUT strung together a few rounds off a bonus, but KC immediately answered back to take the map 13-6. N4RRATE ended with a 25/11/3 KDA and a 1.80 rating.

N4RRATE was a monster on Lotus.

Haven was a map where neither team had lost this stage, with FUT's dominance stretching even further back to Masters London. FUT narrowly led 7-5 after the first half, dropping the pistol but punishing KC with two unlikely eco round wins.

However, after equalizing with a pistol, KC began to pull away, proactively earning picks and converting advantaged retakes repeatedly to turn a 7-7 into 13-8 win. N4RRATE once again led the server with 21 kills, going 46/24/11 in the series overall with an impressive 1.65 rating and 193 ADR.

As KC claim a well-earned place in Shanghai, Avez , dos9 , and LewN are all set to make their first global appearances within the VCT circuit. KC moves on to the grand final and will await the winner of FUT versus Liquid on Sunday, Aug 30.

KC's win left one spot in Shanghai up for grabs. If Fire Flux were able to make it to the grand final, it would have been theirs, but Liquid took things into their own hands, eliminating Fire Flux and taking the spot for themselves.

FF went for a surprising map pick of Haven, which they themselves had banned in four out of their last five matches. The gambit found little purchase as Liquid shut down FF's Chamber-Vyse composition with a 10-2 defense, converting successful site holds and retakes on C in the majority of the rounds.

A round 14 eco win gave FF a way back in as they closed the gap to 11-9, punishing Liquid's slow and separated attacking side, but the comeback was not to be as Liquid eventually scraped together the rounds needed to win 13-10. trexx topped the board with 25 kills and 323 ACS on Sova.
