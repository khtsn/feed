BC.Game, BIG among first teams through to EWC open qualifier playoffs

Most favorites are through to playoffs in the massive EWC open qualifier, including Oleksandr "⁠s1mple⁠" Kostyliev's BC.Game.

The squad suffered defeat to BASEMENT BOYS on Nuke in their first series, but won 2-1 and then found life easier agaisnt the recently-signed 1win squad.

The qualifier, featuring 64 teams on LAN in Paris, got underway an hour late after technical issues but has run smoothly since. Another round of best-of-threes, featuring the likes of 100 Thieves and Liquid, kick off shortly in Groups 9-16.

Four spots are on offer at the $2m main event, with an extra $100,000 split between teams who place 5-16th in the qualifier.
