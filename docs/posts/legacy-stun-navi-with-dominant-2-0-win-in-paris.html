Legacy stun NAVI with dominant 2-0 win in Paris

n1ssim led the Ancient trouncing with an impressive individual performance

Legacy are through to the Esports World Cup's quarter-finals after a quick 2-0 victory against Natus Vincere. It started with a 10-2 lead on Inferno, which Natus Vincere managed to bring back to a 13-9, but another 10-2 on Ancient ensued, and this time Legacy shut the door on any comeback aspirations three rounds later, 13-2.

Up next for Legacy is Falcons, one of the tournament favorites, as the two are set to meet in the quarter-finals. The last time the two clashed was in May in the CS Asia Championships grand final, where the Brazilians were crowned champions 3-1.

The pace on Inferno, the opening map and Legacy's pick, was set by the AWPers, Santino "⁠try⁠" Rigal and Ihor "⁠w0nderful⁠" Zhdanov, although they often found big-swing rounds without their sniper rifles. One such round was an AK quad-kill by the Argentinean player that turned a 2v5 and extended Legacy's early lead on the T side to 1-6.

try quad turns 2v5

Having fallen behind 2-10, w0nderful aced with the Glock in the second pistol round to keep Natus Vincere alive, after which they started to mount a comeback by winning six of the following seven rounds.

During that comeback, w0nderful had another big round, this time with the big green, undoing a quick double by Andrei "⁠arT⁠" Piovezan in a 3v3 as he clutched against the final three Legacy players.

Ultimately, w0nderful's antics were not enough to bridge such a large gap despite his map-leading 1.61 rating, as Legacy closed out their pick 13-9.

w0nderful wins 1v3 with the AWP

If the AWPers were front and center on Inferno, Ancient had one name that stood above the rest: Vinicius "⁠n1ssim⁠" Pereira. It didn't take him long to make his mark, either, winning a 1v2 clutch with the USP in the pistol round to get underway.

n1ssim clutches 1v2 to kick off Ancient

After a 17-kill half, another big round of n1ssim's came again in pistols, this time on the attack, as he downed four players after a dominant 10-2 half to put Legacy well on their way to victory.

Three rounds later, the map was done, 13-3, and n1ssim atop the board with 23 kills, 100.0% KAST, 151.9 ADR, and +20.40% swing in a 2.91-rated map.
