Short news: Week 30

Liquid are a busy team at the moment, having headed straight from their playoff campaign at BLAST Bounty in Malta to Stockholm for Stake Pulse Beat.

They have made the playoffs of the $60,000 LAN event, needing only one game to do so as their opponents forfeited the opening match.

Betclic awaited Liquid in the group final and after losing the opener of Cache in overtime, strong performances by Keith "⁠NAF⁠" Markovic and Jonathan "⁠EliGE⁠" Jablonowski respectively ensured Nuke and Anubis went Liquid's way.

FACEIT has unveiled a new part of their anti-cheat system, dubbed "Human Input Detection."

The feature uses machine-learning to "detect cheaters by their in-game actions, not by the software they use," and will go live August 5.

FACEIT will make it possible for players to experience a blast from the past after 79% of the 106,647 players who voted on the platform did so in favor of the return of half-time voice chat.

This feature, which allows players from both teams to communicate with each other via voice chat during half-time, was enabled in the early days of CS:GO's matchmaking, from 2012 to 2015, before Valve removed it.

Half-time voice chat in FACEIT matches will return as a one-week test at the start of Season 9 on August 5-12.

FACEIT has announced that Vertigo will join its map pool as an optional map for Season 9, which begins on August 5, after getting the majority of votes in a community poll.

Train, Overpass, Fachwerk, and Boulder were the other maps up for voting, with Vertigo receiving 97,421 votes — well above Train's 64,744.

It is the second time the platform will include a map outside the Active Duty pool in its rotation after Cache was added in Season 8 before it was officially added to the pool after the IEM Cologne Major.
