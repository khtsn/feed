    Short: flashie gives update on Liquid's potential scheduling conflict

    In a post on X, Liquid coach Viktor "⁠flashie⁠" Tamás Bea explained how the team will handle the potential scheduling conflict between BLAST Bounty and Stake Pulse Beat.

    Liquid are expected to play their upcoming Bounty series on the 30th. If they win that match, they will drop out of Stake Pulse Beat. If they lose in Malta, they will travel to Stockholm on the 31st and will have a full day to prepare for their first game, on August 1.

    Short: FACEIT fixes spawn bug

    FACEIT has sprung into action after Martin "⁠STYKO⁠" Styk released a video on X showing how spawn locations would not be randomized after the knife round.

    This was particularly abusable if you started the knife round on T-side, but switched over to CT side for the pistol, as you would know the spawn locations of your opponent and could prepare for them making use of good spawns.

    Unfortunately for all the FACEIT tryhards, the bug has now been remedied.
