AwaykeN sets CS2 LAN kill record in a single map

The Bulgarian's 62-kill performance was not enough to carry INFURITY in a sextuple-overtime match at Urban Riga Open S7.

Slaven "⁠AwaykeN⁠" Lyubenov has set a new record for the most kills over a single map played on LAN in Counter-Strike 2.

The 23-year-old Bulgarian scored 62 kills in INFURITY's 29-31 loss to Inner Circle Academy in the group stage of Urban Riga Open Season 7 taking place in Riga, Latvia.

AwaykeN broke a record that had stood for almost exactly a year, jointly held by Alexander "⁠Altekz⁠" Givskov and Nils "⁠k1to⁠" Gruhne from an AM vs. Nexus match at Digital Crusade DraculaN Season 2, where the duo scored 60 kills apiece.

It is also the joint-third-highest kill record overall in CS2, behind Adrian "⁠xelex⁠" Vincze's 67 kills and Dmitry "⁠sstiNiX⁠" Khlebnikov's 63 kills.
