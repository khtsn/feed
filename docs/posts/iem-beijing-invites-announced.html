IEM Beijing invites announced

Falcons headline the team list for IEM Beijing 2026, with Vitality and Spirit both opting to skip the $1.25m event.

MOUZ and Natus Vincere are the next two highest-ranked teams in attendance, with FURIA — champions of the event's predecessor at IEM Chengdu in 2025 — also making the trip.

Legacy, BetBoom, G2, and Aurora have also accepted invitations which means that there were only two declines from the ten global VRS invites.

Instead of Vitality and Spirit, PARIVISION and FaZe have secured tickets. Finally, regional representation comes from Liquid (NA), 9z (SA), and The MongolZ (Asia).

The final three spots will be decided by qualifiers, with one spot in Asia and two in Europe.
