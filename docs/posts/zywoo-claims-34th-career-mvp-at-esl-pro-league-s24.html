ZywOo claims 34th career MVP at ESL Pro League S24

The Frenchman secures his sixth award of the year.

Mathieu "⁠ZywOo⁠" Herbaut has claimed the HLTV x 1xBET Most Valuable Player award at ESL Pro League Season 24 after leading Vitality's charge to their second consecutive trophy.

The French sniper averaged a tournament-high 1.43 rating across the event and topped the board in all three of Vitality's playoff games against PARIVISION (1.66), Aurora (1.44), and MOUZ (1.65).

Vitality's star also placed first on the leaderboards in terms of Kills per round (0.86) and Round swing (+4.33%), and placed second in Damage per round (85.0) and KAST (79.2%).

The award is ZywOo's sixth of the year and extends his all-time overall record to 34 medals.
