Media: paiN set to bench piriajr

paiN are reportedly set to make their second change after a last-place exit at BLAST Open Porto.

paiN are to make their second change in as many weeks by benching Guilherme "⁠piriajr⁠" Barbosa, according to a report by Dust2.com.br. The team are set to use academy player Lucas "⁠Tatu⁠" Santana in his stead, at least in the short term.

The report comes after paiN recently benched Vinicius "⁠vsm⁠" Moreira, who was replaced by another academy player Rickson "⁠Rkzinho⁠" Ribeiro Dias.

Replacement Tatu has spent the last two years with paiN Academy, the longest serving player on the roster.
