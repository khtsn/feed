tikuak joins Chinggis Warriors

The MongolZ have announced Zolbayar "⁠tikuak⁠" Chimedtseren's return to Chinggis Warriors, ending the 17-year-old's short stint with The MongolZ's main roster.

The news follows an ill-fated tenure with The MongolZ that saw the team place 9-16th at EWC and suffer a last-place exit at FISSURE Playground 3, amidst roster instability as Anarbileg "⁠cobrazera⁠" Uuganbayar and Sodbayar "⁠Techno⁠" Munkhbold were shuffled in and out of the starting five.

The MongolZ bench DarkMeister, tikuak

tikuak arrived in The MongolZ in July with at least some level of expectation, having cemented himself as one of the best prospects in the region whilst representing Chinggis Warriors. He was the team's star player and averaged a 1.15 rating during his tenure, resulting in multiple appearances on the HLTV Prospects ranking.

The youngster was unable to deliver on his promise, however, posting a 0.91 rating across 21 maps for The MongolZ as he struggled against a higher calibre of opposition, after initially showing some positive signs during the online portion of BLAST Bounty S2.

With this announcement Erdenedalai "⁠maaRaa⁠" Bayanbat and The MongolZ are left needing to quickly pull more diamonds from the rough, as The MongolZ are only sporting three members on the starting roster with their next appearance, at PGL Masters Bucharest, less than a month away.
