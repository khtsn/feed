NIP claim second consecutive title with ROG JOURNEY win

Ninjas in Pyjamas have claimed the ROG JOURNEY Autumn title after a 2-0 win against Eternal Fire in the final, taking home the $10,000 prize alongside 82 VRS points.

Karim "⁠Krimbo⁠" Moussa's troops placed second in their group after a best-of-one loss against Sangal, which meant they started their playoff run in the Round of 12, but only lost one map on the way to the trophy.

They beat Johnny Speeds, SINNERS and BBL to make the final, where a strong Inferno CT side netted them a comfortable 2-0 victory.

With the victory, Ninjas in Pyjamas accrued 82 VRS to rise 11 places in the rankings and increase their chances of making the Major to 63.7%, according to HLTV's invite simulation.

In terms of other Major hopefuls, the biggest movers were SINNERS (+10 spots), BC.Game (+10), and Voca (+34).
