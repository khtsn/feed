PARIVISION cast 100 Thieves out of EWC

Jame showed 100 Thieves there are levels to CS

PARIVISION's journey at Esports World Cup continues by virtue of a comfortable lower bracket victory over 100 Thieves, the Russian side crushing their opponents convincingly on Anubis and Dust2.

100 Thieves may have triumphed in a tough bracket in the qualifier to earn themselves a spot in the main event, but today they were shown the difference between tier-two or three and tier-one in brutal fashion.

Jame and FL1T combine to complete convincing shut down of 100 Thieves

Anubis was a close affair for the first handful of rounds, 100 Thieves finding some success with a proactive approach on the CT side, but once guns came up for PARIVISION their methodical defaults stopped the aggression and took control of the map, eventually sealing a straightforward victory.

Things were a little better for 100 Thieves across the first half of Dust2, Jack "⁠Gizmy⁠" von Spreckelsen's big-brain pushes finding great timings to scupper PARIVISION's offensive efforts, but once the sides switched PARIVISION were flawless, eliminating 100 Thieves with ease.
