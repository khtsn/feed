Teams and format

Falcons, FURIA, Legacy and Aurora headline the eight-team field at Thunderpick World Championship 2026, joined by BETBOOM, 9z, PARIVISION and Ninjas in Pyjamas.

Ninjas in Pyjamas were brought in to replace Virtus.pro, whose withdrawal followed visa issues affecting Aleksandr "⁠AquaRS⁠" Kovalev and health problems that forced Dias "⁠F0R3VER⁠" Kutubay to temporarily step away from the lineup.

All eight teams will begin their campaigns on Wednesday, October 14. The opening day will start with 9z facing Aurora, followed by Falcons taking on Ninjas in Pyjamas. For group B, Legacy vs. PARIVISION and FURIA vs. BETBOOM will complete the day's action.
