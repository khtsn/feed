Congratulations on the win. First of all, I just wanted to ask what's it like playing with your full roster again? Is it a bit of a relief?

Yeah, it's happy time for our roster. A lot of tournaments right now in like two months and the full lineup, so it's a good time to show what we can do.

Yeah, of course. We didn't play official matches for three weeks, and we have Swiss system here, and of course our goal is to go to playoffs, and it doesn't matter if it's 3-0, 3-1 or 3-2. We just want to feel the game and the competition. So that's all.

I think right now we're at the next step, we're playing not only for the Major, but we want to be like a tier-one, tier-two team who is reaching the playoffs more and more, and playing stability, and growing up to the tier-one level.
