FURIA eliminate MOUZ from StarSeries Fall

FURIA have eliminated MOUZ from StarLadder StarSeries Fall with a 2-1 victory (Cache 13-8, Mirage 16-19, Nuke 13-6), led by a 66-kill performance from Kaike "⁠KSCERATO⁠" Cerato.

Gabriel "⁠FalleN⁠" Toledo's side will continue their lower bracket campaign against MIBR later on Saturday, after their compatriots dispatched NRG earlier in the day.

MIBR dump NRG out of StarSeries Fall

It is a disappointing return for a team whose recent results pointed toward another title challenge. MOUZ had ended their trophy drought by beating Spirit at BLAST Bounty Season 2 and reached another grand final at BLAST Open Porto. MOUZ had a strong opportunity to build on that progress. Instead, an opening upset against NRG immediately put them on the back foot. A reverse sweep of Natus Vincere offered a reprieve, but their recovery stopped against FURIA, leaving them outside the final four.

The series opened on FURIA's pick of Cache, where MOUZ raced to a 4-0 lead on the defense. FURIA responded with seven consecutive rounds to take control, heading into the break 7-5 ahead before extending their advantage to 10-5. MOUZ could only muster three rounds on their offense as KSCERATO topped the board with a 1.51 rating in a 13-8 win.

Mirage initially followed a similar pattern. MOUZ won the opening three rounds but again trailed 5-7 at halftime, with FURIA stretching their lead to 10-5 after the switch. This time, MOUZ fought back, winning five straight rounds and eventually reaching map point at 12-11.

FURIA forced overtime and survived another map point in the first set, but MOUZ prevailed 19-16 in double overtime. Adrian "⁠xelex⁠" Vincze spearheaded the recovery with 34 kills and a 1.51 rating, while Dorian "⁠xertioN⁠" Berman added 30 kills.

Nuke was a tale of three uninterrupted runs. FURIA stormed to a 6-0 lead on the attack before their opponent answered with six rounds of their own to level the score at halftime. The comeback ended there, however, as FURIA produced a flawless seven-round CT side to close out the series. KSCERATO again led the way with 18 kills and a 1.57 rating.
