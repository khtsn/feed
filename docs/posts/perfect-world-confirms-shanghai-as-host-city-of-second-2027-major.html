Perfect World confirms Shanghai as host city of second 2027 Major

Perfect World has announced that it will host the second Major of 2027 in Shanghai, China, confirming a previous report by HLTV.

Sources: Buenos Aires and Shanghai to host Majors in 2027

It will be Perfect World's second Major after Shanghai 2024, which saw Spirit win their first Major trophy after beating FaZe in the grand final.

The first Major of 2027 will be hosted by FiReSPORTS in Buenos Aires, Argentina, followed by Perfect World at the end of the year. HLTV previously learned that BLAST and ESL won the bids for the Majors in 2028.

The Perfect World Shanghai Major is slated to run from November 22 to December 12 over four stages, barring changes to the Major format, and with an expected prize pool of $1.25 million.
