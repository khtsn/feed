SGE started fully in control on their map pick of Lotus. In the second match of the day, Fire Flux earned a hard-fought upset win over Vitality, moving forward into the top six of VCT EMEA and eliminating Vitality from playoffs.

The first map was Fire Flux's pick of Abyss. Fire Flux opted for a double-Duelist, double-Controller composition with Yoru, Waylay, Astra, and Viper, while Vitality adjusted their own Waylay to a Jett pick for Derke following a close 13-11 loss to Enterprise prior this week.

The first half was a close 7-5 affair; Vitality took an early 6-2 lead off of a pistol round and an eco round win in round five, while Fire Flux found answers with proactive fights on their defense to narrow the gap towards the end. Vitality surged in the second half as a 2v4 from Sayonara and Chronicle got them up to 12-6; Fire Flux made a solid comeback attempt but couldn't force overtime as Abyss went Vitality's way 13-11. Kachoww 's heroics were a big reason it got close as the Yoru ended with a server-high 27 kills, while Sayonara topped the board for Vitality with 23.

Sayonara and Chronicle invest their ultimates and convert an unlikely 2v4.

The second map was Ascent. Both teams went for fairly orthodox double-Duelist looks spearheaded by Jett, with Vitality putting Jamppi on Phoenix and Fire Flux putting DeadInk on Waylay. Despite winning the second round eco, Fire Flux struggled to respond to Vitality's attack in the buy rounds—a full defuse stick from hezacoil in round 12 scraped them their fourth of the half.

This time, Fire Flux made the comeback a reality, winning the pistol and breaking open sites with their double dash frontline to take it all the way to a 13-11 victory. The young superstar Kachoww again led the charge, finding 15 of his 25 total kills on the attack side and dishing out 205 ADR.

Kachoww collects his fourth ace in five VCT matches.

Split was the decider. The first half was a back-and-forth 6-6, but the second half was no contest; Kachoww went on an absolute tear on Fire Flux's attack with a 15/2 half as Neon, paving the way forward alongside DeadInk on Waylay to a 13-7 victory.

With this win, Fire Flux move forward to face BBL Esports tomorrow. Vitality can still qualify for Champions via championship points, currently holding the second most in EMEA behind FUT, but their fate is no longer in their hands; BBL and Liquid can both overtake them based on the playoff results, while FUT exiting before the top two would also take away one of the two available point spots.
