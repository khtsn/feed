Snappi: "Sometimes it's hard to know which NIP shows up"

Three days into the Esports World Cup, Ninjas in Pyjamas have stayed alive at the tournament after making up for a best-of-one loss to Legacy with a series victory over BetBoom.

It was an Kacper "⁠xKacpersky⁠" Gabara masterclass that got them over the line in the series, a stark improvement on the Pole's somewhat patchy form in recent months.

We caught up with Marco "⁠Snappi⁠" Pfeiffer after the match to ask him about the Pole's inconsistency of late, which the in-game leader put down mainly to the CT sides, where the Opener is still coming to grips with Martin "⁠stavn⁠" Lund as the new AWPer and new player in Roman "⁠n0te⁠" Hamze.

I hope we make the Major and make a run there because that is what decides the next season

Marco "⁠Snappi⁠" Pfeiffer

"He has like a 1.20 rating on T and then 1.00 on CT. So I think with more consistency and learning the players around him, he's a guy that's really good at understanding patterns of other players. With time, he will get more and more comfortable," Snappi said.

The Dane also commented on Ninjas in Pyjamas's up-and-down results and struggles to break through the glass ceiling through the many changes that happened on the squad. "It's hard to build consistency and actually build proper routines when there's constantly shifts. And I think that's been one of the bigger things," he said.

"A lot of the time it also sucks because then when we have had the roster change, we go into the most important tournament straight away," he went on. "I just hope now that we'll obviously do a good EWC. We had some time now, so we should be playing pretty good here."

Astralis coming up, they've had their own fair share of inconsistencies recently. Do you feel like they're beatable and you can make it to the playoffs?

Yeah, they're obviously beatable. I also think we are beatable. It's kind of hard sometimes to know which NIP shows up. I think we have a really high level, but we also sometimes tend to... we are really good when we play a little bit more free, where we follow kind of a plan.
