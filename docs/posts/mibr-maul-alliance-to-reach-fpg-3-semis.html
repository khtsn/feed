MIBR maul Alliance to reach FPG 3 semis

MIBR are through to the FISSURE Playground 3 semi-finals after beating Alliance 2-0 in the first series on stage at the SND Cultural & Sports Centre, with the team's Brazilian core all averaging 1.40+ ratings.

The series kicked off on Alliance's Inferno pick, and the Swedes took an early lead despite losing the first pistol round, 3-4, but the map took a turn as the Brazilian-majority squad put up five defenses to lead 8-4 at the half.

MIBR's Brazilian core dominated the map, with Lucas "⁠nqz⁠" Soares putting up a 1.90-rated performance followed by Felipe "⁠insani⁠" Yuji's 1.74, and Carlos "⁠venomzera⁠" Eduardo's 1.52, as they followed up the five-round streak before the half with another five rounds in a row on the attack to win out 13-4 and take the series lead.

Alliance won four on the trot to kick off Cache, but once again it was short lived, as a triple by insani in the middle of a seven-round streak put MIBR ahead 5-4.

The sides switched with 7-5 in favor of the South American squad, and the lead was extended after pistols. nqz then won a 1v1 to hit double digits, which insani followed up with a quad in a 3v5 situation.

After MIBR hit map point, 12-5, Alliance had a deathrattle, chaining four in a row on the attack to feint a comeback attempt, but were closed down by a Tomas "⁠tomaszin⁠" Corna double opener in the 22nd round to seal it 13-9.
