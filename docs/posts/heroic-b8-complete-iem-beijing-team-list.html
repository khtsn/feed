HEROIC, B8 complete IEM Beijing team list

The IEM Beijing closed qualifier came to an end with HEROIC and B8 securing the final two spots at the Chinese event.

B8 progressed by losing just one map across four matches, qualifying for the upper bracket final against Nuclear TigeRES.

They dispatched the Russian squad in a 2-0 series to send them to the lower bracket final, where they were once again bested and left without a spot at the tournament.

HEROIC, who were relegated to the lower bracket by Eternal Fire earlier in the event, cashed in their favorite status to win the match 2-0 and secure a spot in the main event thanks to a 1.38-rated performance by Ludvig "⁠Brollan⁠" Brolin.
