100T beat 1win to qualify for EWC group stage

100 Thieves became the first team to progress through the Esports World Cup open qualifier after a 2-0 win over 1win (Ancient 16-14, Anubis 13-6).

Jack "⁠Gizmy⁠" von Spreckelsen's troops were placed in the bracket of death that also included BC.Game and Liquid but bested Oleksandr "⁠s1mple⁠" Kostyliev and company, while 1win got the better of Liquid in the other Ro16 match.

In the match against Vladislav "⁠nafany⁠" Gorshkov's troops, 100 Thieves needed overtime to secure their opponent's Ancient pick, but they were far more comfortable on their own choice.

Alex "⁠poiii⁠" Nyholm Sundgren led the way with a 1.65-rated CT half that ended 9-3 in favor of the European squad despite 1win winning the pistol, which gave 100 Thieves the platform for a straightforward 13-6 victory.

100 Thieves' qualification marks the organization's first appearance at a tier-one LAN since they returned to Counter-Strike at the start of the year.

Their exploits in the open qualifier also mean they have risen to No. 33 in the VRS, and now have a chance to accrue even more points across the double-elimination group stage, set to begin on August 12.
