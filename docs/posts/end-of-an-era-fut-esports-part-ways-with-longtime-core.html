End of an era: FUT Esports part ways with longtime core

FUT Esports FUT Esports Europe Rank #5 s0pp Efe Tur xeus Doğan Gözgen yetujey Eray Budak KROSTALY Okan Alaçam sociablEE Volkan Yonal has released its longtime core of Ata "AtaKaptan" Tan, Doğukan "qRaxs" Balaban, Furkan "MrFaliN" Yeğen, and Baha "baha" Gözmen.

The original Turkish aimers had already lost their spots on the active roster well before being released. FUT benched baha in late January, just three days after FUT's 0-3 elimination from EMEA Kickoff, with qRaxs returning from a personal break to replace him.

AtaKaptan and MrFaliN followed him to the bench in the weeks after, with MrFaliN's move in March marking the exit of the last player from FUT's 2023 core. By April, FUT had already replaced its coaching staff and signed Volkan "sociablEE" Yonal to round out its new starting five.

MrFaliN joined FUT in 2022, having spent over four years with the organization. He helped FUT to a third-place finish in VCT EMEA League 2023, qualifying for Masters Tokyo and Champions Los Angeles. During his time, the squad's best result was a top-six finish at Masters Shanghai. FUT demoted the Turkish IGL to the bench ahead of VCT 2026: EMEA Stage 1.

qRaxs debuted with FUT in 2021, during the pre-partnership era, anchoring what became FUT's legendary core. Ahead of this season's Kickoff, the Initiator announced a personal break for the rest of the year and moved himself to FUT's inactive roster voluntarily. He briefly returned to the active roster after baha's benching.

Longtime Turkish mainstay AtaKaptan joined FUT in 2022, helping the roster qualify for Masters Tokyo and Shanghai, as well as Champions Los Angeles and Seoul. FUT later benched AtaKaptan, replacing him with the well-known Mehmet "cNed" Yağız İpek.
