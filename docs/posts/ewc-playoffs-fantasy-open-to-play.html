Player prices & pick stats 

  • The bracket draw produced a lopsided bracket with Vitality, Spirit, Natus Vincere, and Falcons all being drawn into the bottom side of it. Minor player price adjustments were made to account for that.

  • The most popular "golden card" pick is Ilya "⁠m0NESY⁠" Osipov who goes for $235k, the fourth most-picked overall and present roughly on every fifth team. His pick rate is double that of Mathieu "⁠ZywOo⁠" Herbaut, who goes for the same price, and almost double that of Danil "⁠donk⁠" Kryshkovets, who goes for $240k.

  • The second-most picked player is Yasin "⁠xfl0ud⁠" Koç. The Turkish rifler goes for $195k while his team is set to face magic in round of 16, the biggest underdog of the playoffs, and he has shown a strong performance at the event so far with a 1.42 rating. Notably, FUT are set to meet the winner of GamerLegion vs. MOUZ, the latter of which FUT bested in the group stage.
