Aurora advance to EPL semi-finals over 1win

Jimpphat had a great series, posting a 1.59 rating

Aurora beat 1win 2-0 in the opening ESL Pro League Season 24 playoff match at the Spodek Arena, stealing Dust2 13-9 before taking their Anubis map pick 13-8.

Artem "⁠ArtFr0st⁠" Kharitonov put 1win ahead with a triple in the first pistol round on Dust2, but Aurora hit back with Ali "⁠Wicadia⁠" Haydar Yalçın and Jimi "⁠Jimpphat⁠" Salo MP9 doubles in the second round and ran away with an 8-4 lead at the half.

1win won a crucial second pistol round and brought it back to within one on the defense, but failed to tie it up against deagles when down by one, opening the door for Aurora to run away with the map.

A last-second kill by Ismailcan "⁠XANTARES⁠" Dörtkardeş saved Aurora from botching an A hit to make it 11-8 before hitting map point. Petr "⁠fame⁠" Bolyshev then aced to keep his team alive, but a 1v2 by Özgür "⁠woxic⁠" Eker the following round sealed the deal, 13-9.

woxic ends Dust2 with 1v2

Anubis was much less of a contest as Aurora got off to an early lead with a dualies triple by Wicadia in the pistol round and allowed just two scattered rounds to take double digits at the half.

1win won the second pistol round and tried to build a case for a comeback, but two back-to-back rounds — one with woxic winning a 1v1 clutch despite getting knifed — put Aurora on match point.

1win won three more to continue on their monumental task of bringing it back from eight behind, but were stopped in their tracks by a quick A hit on a forcebuy round that put an end to the map, 13-8, allowing Aurora to advance to the next round of the playoffs.
