OG overhaul roster with Ex3rcice, nut nut, and Chucky signings

OG have announced the signings of Pierre "⁠Ex3rcice⁠" Bulinge, Martin "⁠nut nut⁠" Holm Vestergaard, and Jordan "⁠Chucky⁠" Molnar, signaling "a new dawn" for the organization with the trio joining Alexandre "⁠bodyy⁠" Pianaro and Olle "⁠spooke⁠" Grundström from the previous roster.

Casper "⁠cadiaN⁠" Møller and Luca "⁠pr1metapz⁠" Voigt have been moved to the bench to make way for the incoming players, with the lineup now back to full strength after the transfer of Adam "⁠adamb⁠" Ångström to Ninjas in Pyjamas in September.

"We are aware that the changes made to our CS2 team require a VRS reset," the organization said in its announcement. "And we believe this is the right timing to go that way. As a result, we will look to play as many open LAN events as possible to climb back the rankings."

bodyy will take over in-game leader duties from cadiaN, and reunites with Ex3rcice, whom he has played alongside several times in his career on 3DMAX, DBL PONEY, and LDLC.

nut nut joins OG after a period of inactivity following a move to ECSTATIC's transfer list in July. The Dane had averaged a 1.13 rating in 2026 over 98 maps, but hasn't played an official since April.

Chucky joins as the team's new AWPer after impressing with GenOne, averaging a 1.18 rating over 335 maps this year, primarily in online competition. However, he maintained that level in his few LAN appearances, averaging a 1.21 rating in 27 maps despite GenOne's best result coming in a third-place finish at HyperX Retake Season 12.

OG's Head of Esports, Tutouan Merloz, further explained the move on the organization's website.

"At the end of 2025 we chose a different road: getting to T1 fast, in one move, with cadiaN and FL4MUS," he said. "The results fell short of the ambitions we had set for the project.

"So we’re changing our lineup, and cadiaN is stepping out of the starting five. Bringing him in was my push. He’s a winner and a leader, and I still believe that. But we had two different visions of how you build a winning roster, and that wasn’t something we managed to fix. The call was mine, and so is the responsibility.

"What this organisation is actually good at – and has been for years, with Lambert and his staff leading it – is developing players. Chr1zN and adamb are both doing great, at Heroic and NIP. spooke is also a great example, and he is still with us.

"That’s our identity, and it always has been. So we’re going back to it: building players, and building a team, brick by brick, with the time that actually takes."

OG will debut with its new roster at Roman Imperium Cup IX, which is scheduled to run from October 7-10.
