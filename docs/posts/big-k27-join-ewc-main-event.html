BIG, K27 join EWC main event

BIG became the second team to progress to the Esports World Cup out of the open qualifier after beating Sashi 2-1.

Map two in this series was the only one the team dropped all event long, as they justified their #2 seed with Gleb "⁠gr1ks⁠" Gazin in super-saiyan form.

The Belarussian AWPer, a top prospect, was the best player on the server in six maps out of eleven played and ends the qualifier with a ridiculous 1.58 rating.

The Danes of Sashi have been on a climb in the VRS as of late and picked up four valuable series wins in Paris, including the elimination of Virtus.pro. Added to their first place at CCT 2026 Europe Series 5, they're now comfortably within the top 50 on VRS.

BIG, however, proved too tough a foe as Cache and Ancient went against Nicolai "⁠HUNDEN⁠" Petersen's side. That was much to Benjamin "⁠blameF⁠" Bremer's delight, who took issue with Sashi's trash talk and let them know about it: "Why are you yelling so much when you're fucking shit?"

Down the hall, K27 took on fnatic in a tight affair which they eventually converted to main event qualification.

Both sides have new players, Timur "⁠clax⁠" Sabirov for K27 plus Volodymyr "⁠mazay⁠" Iorhachov and Artem "⁠cairne⁠" Mushynskyi for fnatic, and are looking to improve on their top 40 VRS rankings.

But fnatic's core all had quiet series, below 1.00 ratings, and the efforts of their new recruits could not get them over the line.

That was even after a 1v3 by mazay got fnatic a series point on map two, only for them to lose a 5v3 at 12-11, 16-13 in overtime and end up 10-4 down on map three.

That charge to 10-4 was led by #4 and #6 prospects Aleksey "⁠xeedo⁠" Mikulich and Daniil "⁠qw1nk1⁠" Kabilov, who both posted 90 ADR on the decider as K27 earned a spot in only their second-ever Big Event.

At PGL Astana, inexperience hurt the young Russians (as did their use of a stand-in in the form of Petr "⁠fame⁠" Bolyshev) but they will have learned from the last-place finish and can now take aim at securing some giant killings in the 32-team main event.
