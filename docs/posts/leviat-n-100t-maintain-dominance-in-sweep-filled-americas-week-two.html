LEVIATÁN remains at the forefront of Group Alpha with a 3-0 record. The London champions were firmly in control of both of their matches this week, conceding only ten rounds total to EG and twelve total to ENVY. They also quickly redeemed their Sunset woes, earning a 13-5 over EG following a 13-1 loss to FURIA on the map last week.

blowz and Neon lock down the A site.

In second place in Group Alpha is NRG, who defeated FURIA 2-0 as the latter squad played their first match with C0M in the lineup. Breeze was a back-and-forth overtime affair, but NRG cleaned things up on Summit with another impenetrable 10-2 defensive half for a 13-7 win. mada had a strong performance with a cumulative 40/27/18 KDA.

On the side of Group Omega, 100T trounced KRÜ to keep their spot at the top of the group, starting with a brutal 13-0 on Split and ending with a 6-10 to 13-10 comeback on Ascent. Individually, the Duelist duo of Asuna and Timotino has continued to put up numbers; Asuna leads the league by ACS and ADR with 254 ACS and 172 ADR, while Timotino holds a third-highest 251 ACS.

Nipping at 100T's heels in Omega are Sentinels, who sit at a +22 round differential versus 100T's +26 after a convincing 2-0 over LOUD. In-game leader johnqt , back in the server after sitting out week one due to an emergency appendectomy, ended the match at the top of the server with a 37/20/11 KDA and was a key source of impact frags for Sentinels in lateround situations.

johnqt and cortezia pull off an impressive 2v4.

Lagging just behind Sentinels are G2, who handed Cloud9 a 13-1, 13-6 thrashing to seat the fellow North American squad at the bottom of the group.

Lastly, of the four teams with two matches this week, MIBR were the team besides LEV to come out with two wins, while ENVY and FURIA each suffered two losses. MIBR opened the week by dominating a struggling ENVY, who now languish at the bottom of the standings with a 0-6 map record, and closed the week with a closer win over FURIA that included another overtime map one.

Mazino dismantles ENVY in the 1v3.
