Vitality eliminate 9z from BLAST Open Porto

Vitality have kept their BLAST Open Porto campaign alive after dispatching 9z 2-0 in Group B's lower bracket, winning 13-2 on Dust2 and 13-6 on Nuke.

The result eliminates the South American side in 13th-16th place, while Vitality advance to face the loser of the match between Legacy and Falcons.

The victory provides some relief for Vitality , who began the tournament with a shock 0-2 loss to Inner Circle in Justinas "⁠jL⁠" Lekavicius's first appearance as a stand-in for William "⁠mezii⁠" Merriman.

Vitality went on to secure a commanding 10-2 advantage at halftime on Dust2. jL led the charge with 21 kills, six deaths and a 2.49 rating, while Mathieu "⁠ZywOo⁠" Herbaut posted a 1.84 rating of his own.

Nuke initially looked set to follow the same pattern as Vitality raced into a 4-0 lead, but 9z recovered and fought back to make it 6-6 at halftime.

That resistance disappeared after the side switch. Vitality won seven rounds to complete the 13-6 victory, with ZywOo recording 13 kills and just one death during the flawless closing half.

jL finished the series at the top of the scoreboard with 38 kills and a 1.96 rating, narrowly ahead of ZywOo, who averaged a 1.82 rating across the two maps.
