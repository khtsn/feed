Liquid and NIP eliminated as Logitech G Play Connect reaches playoffs

The round-robin BO1 group stage of Logitech G Play Connect has concluded, sending the two group winners through to the semi-finals while the runners-up and third-place teams advanced to the quarters.

The $100,000 LAN in Warsaw, Poland, saw B8 grab the first semi-final spot on offer, the Ukrainian side marching through group A without losing a single map, Luminosity the only team who managed to put up a double-digit score against them.

Luminosity and M80 were the other two teams to get through group A, picking up three wins and two losses apiece to earn places in the quarter-finals.

Group B was topped by Inner Circle, who enjoyed a winning run all the way through to their last game of the group stage, where they lost to EYEBALLERS with a playoff berth already secured.

Three teams tied for a 3-2 record in Group B, but EYEBALLERS missed out on the quarter-finals to BBL and 3DMAX as they lost to both in their head-to-head matchups.

Several notable names missed out on playoff spots, namely Ninjas in Pyjamas and Liquid. The former had a torrid time in the group stage, racking up four losses whilst posting a single win against Metizport.
