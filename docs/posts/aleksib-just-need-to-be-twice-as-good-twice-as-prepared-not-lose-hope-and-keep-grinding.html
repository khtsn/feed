Aleksib: "Just need to be twice as good, twice as prepared, not lose hope and keep grinding"

Natus Vincere have been eliminated from the Esports World Cup after a 0-2 loss to Legacy in a one-sided series controlled by the Brazilians from the get-go.

Speaking to HLTV after the series, Aleksi "⁠Aleksib⁠" Virolainen put the result down to slow starts, with his team having fallen behind 10-2 on both maps.

"It's almost impossible to win a game in this fashion against opponents like this," Aleksib said.

He added, "It's tough to say [what went wrong] before analyzing the game and letting your thoughts settle for a couple hours. But as of right now, I just think they played better and we weren't there as the NAVI that could contend for trophies."

B1ad3: "We were close to making some changes"

Natus Vincere will have no time to rest, with their next event less than a week away. BLAST Open Porto offers the team a chance to turn the page after a slow start to the season.

"This result is not what we expected and we're not happy about it, but there are multiple tournaments left and the next one is right around the corner, so we just need to try our best to reset and play better in the next tournament," he said. "There's nothing else to it."

A 2-0 defeat and you're out of the tournament. A disappointing loss, I'd say. Give me your thoughts on what happened.

I think that Inferno started a bit rough. We felt like we had a lot of good reads. Even in the opening gun round, we were already stacking three B, [still] lost that round. There were a lot of rounds where we predicted them to come fast A and we couldn't stop them, and then we had like a 5on2 as well, or something like that. We just started really slowly, and then we picked up the pace on the T side, but they had too many rounds. And yeah, ended up making a couple crucial mistakes there to not make the comeback.

So we felt like we were ready, ready for the game now. And we were actually shooting back, and now we're going to go into our map pick. And throughout the whole tournament we had felt pretty good on the T side. They ended up shutting us down, and I felt like n1ssim had a good game on Ancient and he put us in our place and we couldn't trade him in some crucial gun rounds where we could have perhaps brought the game back in our favor.

It was the exact same maps that you played against them back at the Major. You won back then 2-0, now 2-0 the other way. And it felt like Inferno could have been even more one-sided if w0nderful hadn't had those two great rounds, the 1v3 and the pistol ace. What do you think went wrong today in terms of preparation? Or do you think that they maybe had a good game plan coming into the series?

It's tough to say before analyzing the game and letting your thoughts settle for a couple hours. But as of right now, I just think they played better and we weren't there as the NAVI that could contend for trophies. So yeah, they played much better. I'm not sure if it's a game plan or something else, but it's going to be a better picture in my head after this evening when I can analyze.

The quarter-final [Round of 16] loss here is obviously not what you expected, especially considering that the Club championship is very important for NAVI, right? You were in third place, if I'm not mistaken. Where are the main takeaways from this event as a whole?

Not good. We practiced hard. We practiced on all the days we've been here, even though it's been only two games that we've been playing officials, so this was the third. All the other days we've been practicing and trying to get ready, trying to stay in shape and improve on all of the maps that are in our map pool. And yeah, this result is not what we expected and we're not happy about it, but there are multiple tournaments left and the next one is right around the corner, so we just need to try our best to reset and play better in the next tournament. There's nothing else to it.

And considering what happened here, what sort of goals can you set for the next tournament?

[smiles] Not go out in the quarter-finals [Round of 16], and reach the playoffs. And yeah, just try to be a stronger force. Today, on both of the maps, we started slowly. It's almost impossible to win a game in this fashion against opponents like this. If we couldn't do it now, it would have been even harder in the semis. Just need to be twice as good, twice as prepared, not lose hope and keep grinding. So yeah, that's about it.
