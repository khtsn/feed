Short news: Week 36

The organizers of Esports World Cup 2027 have announced more details for the massive open qualifier.

The tournament, which will award four spots at the main event, is once again open up to 128 teams on a first-come, first-serve basis, with more information about the registration set to be announced "soon."

You can read the entire announcement here.

FiReSPORTS confirmed on Tuesday that it will run an international open LAN in Buenos Aires, Argentina, for 16 teams with a $75,000 prize purse.

The event runs from October 29 to November 1, one day before the invite cutoff for PGL Major Singapore.

Registration for the event will open on September 22.
