Short news: Week 36

FiReSPORTS confirmed on Tuesday that it will run an international open LAN in Buenos Aires, Argentina, for 16 teams with a $75,000 prize purse.

The event runs from October 29 to November 1, one day before the invite cutoff for PGL Major Singapore.

Registration for the event will open on September 22.
