FACEIT has announced that it will be handing out longer bans "for cheating, smurfing, stream sniping, and repeated or targeted abuse" with the launch of Season 9 on August 5.

"We are applying a more contextual approach for occasional offences so the ban more closely matches the offence," the company said.

"There is more room for trash talk, banter, harmless backing of your country or favorite sports team, and some profanity, as long as it doesn't progress into abuse."
