latto after third MVP in China: "It's China magic, I think"

Legacy secured their third trophy in China with a victory over G2 in the grand final, with Bruno "⁠latto⁠" Rebelatto adding a third MVP medal on Chinese soil to his collection. Having previously described his performance in the semi-finals of CAC 2026 as one of his career’s top three, the rifler placed his latest display above them all.

"I think it's number one because it's a grand final,” he said. “So yeah, I think it's my best performance."

Speaking after the victory, latto credited the support of the Chinese crowd and reflected on the mental work that helped Legacy survive a difficult match against MIBR. He also discussed his longstanding partnership with Eduardo "⁠dumau⁠" Wolkmer and how their reunion with Santino "⁠try⁠" Rigal differs from their time together in 00NATION, highlighting the AWPer’s consistency as an important influence on the team.

With two MVP medals and two EVP awards this year, latto has his sights set on a first appearance in HLTV’s Top 20 after finishing in the Top 30 last year. For the rifler, reaching that milestone would fulfil a personal ambition, but he was quick to acknowledge his teammates’ contribution.

You can watch or read the interview in full below:

At CAC 2026, after the match against The MongolZ, you said it was one of the top three performances of your career. How would you rate your performance in today’s final against G2?

I think it's number one because it's a grand final. So yeah, I think it's my best performance.

This is Legacy’s third trophy in China and your third MVP medal here. China seems to bring out the best in both you and the team. Why do you think you’ve been so successful here?

I don't know. I think the crowd cheering for us is so exciting. Having everybody cheering for us is very good. I don't know; it's China magic, I think. It's really different. We play very well here.

I don't know, it's China magic, I think

Back in June, you mentioned that BobZ, adrrr, and arT had helped you a lot with the mental side of the game. How much has your mentality improved since then? Do you feel better equipped to handle high-pressure situations now?

Yeah. I think they've helped me a lot, and helped the team as well. We had a very tough game against MIBR, and if we hadn't worked on those things with them, maybe we would have lost the map and gone home.

So I think we've improved a lot, and they're still working on this with us. I think it's working.

You and dumau have played together since 2021. What has allowed that partnership to keep improving after so many years?

Yeah. I think me and dumau, I don't know, we just like playing together. He knows everything about me, and I know everything about him. It's the partnership.

We're building a very good history together; now we can show everybody we can do damage, and we can prove to everybody we can be at the top.

You, dumau, and try are together again after your time in 00NATION. Beyond having more experience, what is different about how the three of you understand and play the game now?

Yeah, I think when we played together — me, dumau, and try — we were kids. It's been about four years now, and try is a different guy. dumau is a different guy, and so am I.

We have more experience now. I think that's what has changed. We've learned and grown up over these years.

We can prove to everybody we can be at the top

You followed third place at EWC with a group-stage exit in Porto, and now you’ve won another title. What have those different results taught you about what this team needs to perform consistently?

I don't know. try joined the team, and I think that changed a lot of our game. He's so consistent in his job in the game. This has helped us become a more consistent team, and that's the key.

You finished No. [30] in HLTV’s ranking last year. What did you feel you needed to improve this year to make the list? With two MVP medals and two EVP awards already this year, are you confident you'll make HLTV's Top 20 this year?

Yeah. For me, it's a dream to be in the top 20 players in the world. Last year, it was a surprise for me to be in the top 30. I just told myself that I needed to keep the same mentality, keep growing in the game, and I would reach the top 20. That's it for me.

The team is helping me a lot, and if I can achieve this, it will be partly thanks to the team as well.
