NRG soar in the ranking with MOUZ upset in StarSeries Fall opener

MOUZ's hopes of lifting the StarLadder StarSeries Fall trophy have been dealt a huge blow with a shock 2-1 loss to bottom seed NRG.

The victory comes as a massive boost for the North American side, who move up 18 places in the global VRS to 33rd after netting 129 points. The loss costs MOUZ 31 points and sees them drop two places to fourth.

nitr0: "Any win here would be huge for us"

On media day Nick "⁠nitr0⁠" Cannella had talked up NRG's chances in the series by saying MOUZ were a "good matchup for us considering all of the tier-one teams." They were the better team for much of the game on Cache, though they left it late to clinch the map as MOUZ nearly pulled off a miraculous comeback from 6-11 down.

MOUZ responded with a 13-5 hammering on Inferno, extending their win streak on the map to four games.

Most would have expected them to carry that momentum into Dust2, but they looked sluggish and disjointed instead, managing just four rounds on the CT side, salvaged largely by a handful of crucial 1v1s.

A 6-0 run in the second half kept MOUZ's comeback hopes alive, but NRG tightened up and strung four rounds together at the death to pull off a massive upset.
