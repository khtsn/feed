MistR mutually parts ways with Sashi

Mikkel "⁠MistR⁠" Thomsen has announced that he has mutually parted ways with Sashi, the Dane saying that the move will allow him to "step away from this crazy situation and focus on what's next."

The crazy situation MistR is referring to is the recent region-swapping turmoil that Sashi found themselves embroiled in after benching both MistR and Casper "⁠Cabbi⁠" Jensen in favour of Australian duo Tynan "⁠TjP⁠" Purtell and Jordan "⁠Hatz⁠" Bajic.

Sashi's move to bring in the aforementioned Australians on short-term contracts was designed to allow the team to switch to the Asia VRS region, giving them a better shot at qualifying for PGL Major Singapore later this year.

The cynical nature of Sashi's approach led to Valve issuing a change to the Major Supplemental Rulebook a mere day later, preventing the region swap and leaving Sashi disqualified from Major contention.

"Thank you to Sashi and especially to my coaches and teammates," MistR's post to X read. "The past few days have been hard and it hurts to see what's happening at X. I'm happy Sashi and I mutually agreed to part ways, allowing me to step away from this crazy situation and focus on what's next."

According to Turkish X account Arhaval, MistR will join BBL, who expanded into CS in August with the signing of the Echo roster.
