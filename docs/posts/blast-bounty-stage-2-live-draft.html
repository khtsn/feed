BLAST Bounty Stage 2 live draft

After a huge number of upsets during Stage 1, which meant the likes of Falcons, Vitality, FURIA, and Aurora have already been eliminated, the competition has reached Stage 2.

One more round of online play is set to happen across Saturday and Sunday, with the winners of the eight matches receiving a ticket to the LAN finals in Malta.

Now, eight of the lowest seeds will pick their opponents for the qualification clashes, and Ninjas in Pyjamas are the first to pick.
