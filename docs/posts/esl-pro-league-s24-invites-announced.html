ESL Pro League S24 invites announced

ESL has announced the invites for their Pro League Season 24 tournament, set to be played in Katowice, Poland from October 3-11.

A total of 12 teams have received direct invitations, with all VRS-invited teams accepting spots at the event.

The current top five in the VRS, namely Spirit, Falcons, 9z, MOUZ, and Vitality, will all be in attendance, as will the likes of G2, FURIA, and the new-look Aurora.

The 12 invited squads will join TYLOO, M80, 1win, and ShindeN, who won their region's respective ESL Challenger League Finals to qualify for the event.

EPL S24 will begin with a Swiss group stage before an eight-team playoff bracket is set to be played in the Spodek Arena from October 9-11.
