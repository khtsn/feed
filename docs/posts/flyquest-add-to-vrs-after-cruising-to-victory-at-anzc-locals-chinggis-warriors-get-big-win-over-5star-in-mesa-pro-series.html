FlyQuest add to VRS after cruising to victory at ANZC Locals; Chinggis Warriors get big win over 5star in MESA Pro Series

FlyQuest eased to victory against local opposition

FlyQuest made quick work of the field at the ANZC Locals Brisbane Season 2 LAN and have added 149 points to their name in the VRS ranking, putting them in fifth place above Lynn Vision and 5star as the race for Major qualification starts to heat up.

The Alistair "⁠aliStair⁠" Johnston-led squad beat madlikewizards and Abyssal in the group stage, before taking out KZG and Ground Zero on the way to the final, where they once again beat Abyssal 2-0 to secure the tournament win in dominant fashion with no maps lost.

FlyQuest will attend two more regional events coming up before the Major's November 2 cutoff date, DFRAG Logitech G Pro Series and DFRAG Invitational, as they continue to contest for one of five Major invites in their region among a tightly packed group of seven teams comprising TYLOO, BC.Game, The MongolZ, Chinggis Warriors, FlyQuest, 5star, and Lynn Vision, all within less than 130 VRS points of each other.

Over at the MESA Pro Series Fall tournament in Ulaanbaatar, Mongolia, Chinggis Warriors beat 5star in a crucial grand final that allowed them to surpass their compatriots in the VRS ranking and become the second-highest team in Mongolia behind The MongolZ.

5star had beaten Chinggis Warriors in the opening round of the tournament, but the Sodbileg "⁠cool4st⁠" Batbaatar-led squad ran the gauntlet through the lower bracket to take revenge in the final and secure the $1,650 cash prize and the important VRS points.
