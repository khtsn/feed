Imperial beat paiN to win FERJEE In House S2

Imperial have taken home the FERJEE In House S2 trophy by breezing past paiN in the best-of-five final (Anubis 13-4, Cache 13-11, Mirage 13-6).

The two teams, which came into the tournament as the two highest-ranked squads, progressed to the grand final without losing a single map. In the final, however, paiN's new-look lineup was visibly second-best against Vinicius "⁠VINI⁠" Figueiredo's troops.

Their academy graduates Rickson "⁠Rkzinho⁠" Ribeiro Dias and Lucas "⁠Tatu⁠" Santana both had rough games, with only captain Rodrigo "⁠biguzera⁠" Bittencourt finishing the series with a positive rating (1.36), 0.55 ahead of his closest teammate.

On the Imperial side, sniper Guilherme "⁠saadzin⁠" Pacheco was their best player across the event (1.36) and also finished top of the board in the final.
