FaZe knocking on the door of top 15 with Roman Imperium Cup IX title

FaZe won the Roman Imperium Cup IX on Saturday after a 2-0 thrashing of NAVI Junior, moving up 14 places in the global VRS to 16th.

Russel "⁠Twistzz⁠" Van Dulken's charges now have a 71.8% chance of qualifying for PGL Major Singapore, according to HLTV's latest projections. They will attend two more LAN events before the November 2 invite cutoff: DraculaN Season 7 (October 19-23) in Bucharest, and RLG Tesla Cup (October 27-29) in Belgrade.

It was FaZe’s first LAN title since IEM Chengdu 2024, with David "⁠frozen⁠" Čerňanský the sole member left from that squad. Their previous LAN final, at HLC Belgrade PRO 2026, ended in a loss to BIG that cost them a place at the IEM Cologne Major.

FaZe capped their Roman Imperium Cup IX run with a one-sided series in which they conceded only four rounds. The campaign was far from straightforward, though, as they had to recover from a map down against Sangal in the opening round and again against NAVI Junior in the upper bracket final, where the decider went to overtime.
