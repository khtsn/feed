BELCHONOKK: "I feel much more comfortable now; in PARIVISION we had high expectations"

A support player in PARIVISION, Andrey "⁠BELCHONOKK⁠" Yasinskiy was given star opener roles in TDK and 1win and he is repaying their faith after top-fragging against G2 at Pro League.

The 22-year-old finished Mirage with a 2.09 rating, and the series with 52 frags. It was a show of firepower we rarely saw in a PARIVISION team that struggled under the weight of expectation placed upon them by their win at BLAST Bounty.

It's not just the roles; BELCHONOKK also pointed to the "much more comfortable" personalities of Konstantin "⁠groove⁠" Pikiner and Vladislav "⁠nafany⁠" Gorshkov, having played for years under Dzhami "⁠Jame⁠" Ali and Dastan "⁠dastan⁠" Akbayev's more disciplinarian style.

The team are still far away — BELCHONOKK says it will take at least a year to fully merge the way the different players see the game — but a win over G2 this early is proof they are heading in the right direction.
