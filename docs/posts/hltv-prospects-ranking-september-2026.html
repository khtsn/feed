HLTV Prospects ranking: September 2026

David "⁠Dawy⁠" Bibik has risen to take the top spot on the prospects list after the graduation of Adrian "⁠xelex⁠" Vincze.

He leapfrogged previous #2 Klimentii "⁠kl1m⁠" Krivosheev — the subject of our latest spotlight — after shining at the EWC open qualifier to complete a journey to #1 that started for him in the very first edition of Prospects last year.

HLTV Prospects spotlight: kl1m

Seven players have remained in the list throughout all 11 rankings, though that includes four of our top five, and Dawy leads the way as a veteran of the list. The Ukrainian rifler was as high as #2 in January before falling out of the top ten and now climbing all the way back to the top of the mountain.

Aleksey "⁠xeedo⁠" Mikulich of K27 is the only top-five player who joined the list late, breaking into it in January and embarking on a steady upwards journey to his new highest spot of #2.

Those two are followed by kl1m and the Swedish pairing of Liam "⁠MaiL09⁠" Tügel and Alex "⁠poiii⁠" Nyholm Sundgren.

It's notable that new Liquid sniper Georgi "⁠Jorko⁠" Mitev breaks into the top ten, having impressed thus far. Gleb "⁠gr1ks⁠" Gazin is also in the top ten, returning after a few months away, after averaging a 1.58 rating to send BIG into the EWC main event.

Zhassulan "⁠Botpa1⁠" Assembek (#22), highlighted last month for his form locally, is at it again too with a 1.39 rating en route to another trophy for Omega. Teammate Aldiyar "⁠Aldikon⁠" Alim (#29) was also on song this month with a 1.43 rating at a separate Kazakh LAN win.

Other big climbers include Zolbayar "⁠tikuak⁠" Chimedtseren, who is bedding in at The MongolZ, and Philipp "⁠tex1y⁠" Moskvitin after he recorded a 1.27 rating at Moscow Cyber Games.

Down under, Kalan "⁠sliimey⁠" Lorenzo continues to perform a head above the rest with a 1.36 rating in August and he breaks into the top 20 as a reward.

Egor "⁠ayuki⁠" Ershov of Nuclear TigeRES is making moves after he helped his side win NODWIN Clutch Series 10 and performed well on LAN over the last month in general.

Also keep an eye on new entrant Andrii "⁠tired73⁠" Hlushko, a 17-year-old Ukrainian with 4293 FACEIT ELO and a 1.26 rating in the last six months.

Graduates

Two of our top ten from August depart, in the form of xelex and Jason "⁠JBOEN⁠" Boe Nielsen.

We also will no longer be seeing Andrey "⁠AW⁠" Anisimov, Su "⁠Zero⁠" Jingshen, Vladislav "⁠lattykk⁠" Vydrin, Abdurakhim "⁠mo0N⁠" Issa, or João "⁠Ltz⁠" Bonetti.

Now that graduations happen each month, you are less likely to see our prospects at tier one events. These youngsters no longer have the luxury of bedding into tier one while remaining in the list, so it is less likely they come away with crazy ratings against the best teams in the world.

Instead, most of our highlights will be at smaller LANs, or events like EWC's humungous last chance qualifier. By keeping the list fresh, we are also reducing the quality at the top end.

Stat Attack

For this month's scatterplot, we're looking at some brand new metrics.

We now have access to round swing data per round, which means we can use stats like 'rounds with negative swing' or 'rounds with more than 20% swing' to see which players are consistently impactful.

We chose 20% because when a player ends the round above that mark, their team wins 90% of rounds. We chose 0% because, well, you can guess.

tired73 is the first name to jump out, after brilliant work for WBT — though he is yet to play a top 50 team in an official.

It's interesting to see players like Kristian "⁠kralle⁠" Jensen stand out in the explosive round-winning 20%+ metric, but also end more than 54% of rounds with a negative impact on the round outcome. It's no surprise plenty of orange aggressive riflers are in this quadrant, playing for high reward with plenty of risk too.

Our purple passive riflers skew left, with players like Daniel "⁠syph0⁠" Ageyev ending less than half of rounds with a negative swing while holding up as fairly explosive too.

AWPers tend to do well overall, with Vladimir "⁠b1st⁠" Krasikov and forgotten man Alimzhan "⁠Alkaren⁠" Bitimbay closest to tired73 in the top-right corner.

Top Picks

It's finally time to pick MaiL09 as a player ready to make a move. After being so good in Alliance, he's finding it a bit harder in Metizport but there are good signs from Challenger League that he is adapting to Peter "⁠stanislaw⁠" Jarguz's system. Another few months, and the FACEIT prodigy will be ready for some Big Events.

xeedo is one of many Russophone star openers in this list, but in a congested market he is flying his colors well and seems as sure a bet as any.

We've then gone for Alkaren, who had a dire time with HEROIC due to visa issues but has all the same potential he had when they signed him and is getting rid of his ring rust on loan with CYBERSHOKE.

For our maverick picks, we've turned to NA for Chance "⁠WUMBO⁠" Boyer, then back to Europe for ayuki as a closer and tired73 as a sniper.
