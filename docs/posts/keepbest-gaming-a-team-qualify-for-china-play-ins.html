Despite another sweep loss to KBG in the finals, the team now has a chance for Champs, but that begins with a series against Trace Esports. Trace was on a six-game losing streak before sweeping TEC in the seeding match for the play-ins, earning the chance to face the weaker of the two CN National teams. Malix, Dingwei, monk, and Eren will need to step up to defeat an experienced Trace team if the squad wants to make it to Shanghai.

As is standard for VCT China, the region begins its playoffs earlier than any other, but not as far ahead as before. The China play-ins begin on August 5, one day before the EMEA play-ins. Matches are as follows:

  • Trace Esports vs. A Team (Upper Round 1)
  • Titan Esports Club vs. KeepBest Gaming (Upper Round 1)

The victors move on in the upper bracket, but the losers continue in the lower bracket with a second chance for playoff qualification, and by extension, Champs qualification. A long run ahead for these new VCT squads, just like every other tier-two squad at a play-in this year, but not impossible.
