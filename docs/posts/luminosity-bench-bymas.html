Luminosity bench Bymas

The Lithuanian rifler moves to the inactive roster just a month after joining.

Luminosity have announced the benching of Aurimas "⁠Bymas⁠" Pipiras following two consecutive last-place finishes by the international squad.

The organization re-entered Counter-Strike in July by signing the ex-Monte quartet, who were joined by Lucas "⁠lux⁠" Meneghini as the team's IGL.

A leadership change took a toll on the team's results, however, as they finished last at XSE Pro League Guangzhou and Esports World Cup and were unable to replicate their form from the Cologne Major, where they were still under the tutelage of Jack "⁠Gizmy⁠" von Spreckelsen.

"I'm very grateful for the opportunity to represent such an organization and I really wish the guys nothing but the best going forward. These guys were like family to me and I genuinely enjoyed spending time with all of them," Bymas wrote on X.

"As for me, I'm disappointed that I couldn't perform at the level I wanted to in officials lately. I'm going to work hard to become the player I know I can be."

Luminosity are now:

Aleks "⁠Rainwaker⁠" Petrov

Aurimas "⁠Bymas⁠" Pipiras (benched)
