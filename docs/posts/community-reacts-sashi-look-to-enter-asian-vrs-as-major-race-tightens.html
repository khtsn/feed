Community reacts: Sashi look to enter Asian VRS as Major race tightens

Sashi announced on Monday the surprise additions of Australian duo Tynan "⁠TjP⁠" Purtell and Jordan "⁠Hatz⁠" Bajic to their roster as they look to maximize their chances of qualifying for the Singapore Major ahead of the November 2 invite cut-off.

The pair's arrival will see Sashi switch regions from Europe to Asia, as the Danish organization says Anton "⁠Anlelele⁠" Huynh has Vietnamese citizenship.

The move is expected to be temporary, with Sashi saying that Mikkel "⁠MistR⁠" Thomsen and Casper "⁠Cabbi⁠" Jensen "will be stepping out of the starting lineup for the coming month." Still, both players took to X to say they're unsure what the future holds.

It's not the first time that a team has swapped regions in the final stages of a Major race. Earlier this year, G2 and Liquid swapped Mario "⁠malbsMd⁠" Samayoa and Guy "⁠NertZ⁠" Iluz, allowing the latter team to return to an Americas core before the cut-off for the Cologne Major.

Last week, BC.Game entered Asia's qualification zone for the Singapore Major right after signing Tyson "⁠asap⁠" Paterson. And WW recently made headlines by signing Ermek "⁠enzero⁠" Kartanbaev and having Ivan "⁠StRoGo⁠" Shurpatov switch his nationality to Tajikistan.
