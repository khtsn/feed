VLDN to stand in for Liquid at Logitech G Play Connect

Liquid will field Vladan "⁠VLDN⁠" Radević in place of Mario "⁠malbsMd⁠" Samayoa for their Logitech G Play Connect campaign in Warsaw, announcing the Guatemalan rifler will miss the event due to personal reasons.

It will be Liquid's first LAN since their Round of 32 in the Open Qualifier for the Esports World Cup, and comes on the heels of a labored campaign through the PGL Masters Bucharest qualifier.

VLDN has spent recent months plying his trade for ex-Zero Tenacity, and has averaged a 1.10 rating in 2026.

He will debut for Liquid against INFINITE in a best-of-one on Friday, with four more best-of-ones to follow in the round-robin group stage.
