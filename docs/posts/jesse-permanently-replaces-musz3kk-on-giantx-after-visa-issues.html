Jesse permanently replaces musz3kk on GIANTX after visa issues

GIANTX GIANTX Europe Rank #23 westside Miłosz Duda Flickless Karel Maeckelbergh ara Hanceriuc Eduard-George Jesse Adam Čtvrtníček neT Michael Bernet has terminated its contract with Cihan "musz3kk" Göçer, and fully signed Adam "Jesse" Čtvrtníček who was previously a stand-in for musz3kk.

"It's really sad," musz3kk said. "I did everything I could, but it didn't work out. Also, despite our explanation of the situation, I thank the 'Turkish' esports federation for not providing the slightest support to resolve the issue :))"

Jesse was initially brought on as a stand-in following unexpected delays in musz3kk's visa process. GIANTX shared that musz3kk's visa situation remained unresolved, leading to this decision.

"Given the short duration of the VCT EMEA Stage and the team's need for stability, GIANTX and musz3kk have mutually agreed to terminate his contract," it said. "We thank musz3kk for his commitment, professionalism, and efforts throughout this process, and wish him the best in his future endeavors."

musz3kk leaves without ever donning the GX jersey in an official match. Jesse, who was brought in as his stand-in, will now occupy his position for the rest of the split.
