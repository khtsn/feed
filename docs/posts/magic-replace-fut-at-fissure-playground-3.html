magic replace FUT at FISSURE Playground 3

magic will take FUT's place at FISSURE Playground 3 after the Turkish club was forced to pull out due to visa issues.

FISSURE stated that the change will result in the match schedule being rearranged. The revised groups and opening matchups have yet to be announced.

The withdrawal comes just one week after FUT finished second at the Esports World Cup, where their run was ended by Spirit in the grand final.

Their replacement, magic, are currently ranked 19th on the HLTV ranking and will make their third LAN appearance of this year. The European side finished fourth at PGL Astana and reached the round of 16 at the Esports World Cup after recording victories over 3DMAX and 9z.

FISSURE Playground 3 moves to Suzhou
