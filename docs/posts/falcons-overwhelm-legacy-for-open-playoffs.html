Falcons overwhelm Legacy for Open playoffs

Falcons are the fourth team to secure a ticket to Porto for BLAST Open playoffs after beating Legacy 2-1.

It's a big win for the team after they lost both previous matches against the Brazilians, one in the CAC grand final and the other last week at the Esports World Cup. With Vitality waiting for the loser in the lower bracket, too, there was an extra dose of pressure for the teams on the server.

It also answers questions raised by Falcons' rusty performance against Lynn Vision yesterday as the Major champions secure a top-six finish at the very least.

The two 13-6 scorelines perhaps flatters Falcons, given how close the games had been early on. But later in the maps, the big moments went their way like when Ilya "⁠m0NESY⁠" Osipov's 1v3 clutch extinguished Legacy on Dust2.

"I just took an aggressive duel against him," m0NESY told Alex "⁠Mauisnake⁠" Ellenberg post-game. "I didn't want to respect."

Falcons were also helped by an off-day for star AWPer Santino "⁠try⁠" Rigal, who has been so important to Legacy's recent resurgence in form.

Down 4-1 on that map, having just lost a 5v3, Falcons needed Danny "⁠zonic⁠" Sørensen to call a timeout where he told his troops to "reset".

But they managed to do so, with m0NESY in top form and joined by Maksim "⁠kyousuke⁠" Lukin, who recovered from a 6-20 K-D on map two to post a 2.03 rating on map three. Legacy won just two more rounds in the series.

In the end, bad moments that could have derailed the series – they lost a 5v2 on map one too — did not cost the Falcons as they sailed into playoffs.
