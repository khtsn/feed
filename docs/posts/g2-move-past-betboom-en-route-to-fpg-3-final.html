G2 move past BETBOOM en route to FPG 3 final

G2 defeated BETBOOM in the semi-final of FISSURE Playground 3 with a 2-1 scoreline (Mirage 7-13, Anubis 16-13, Ancient 13-7) to secure their spot in the grand final.

Standing between Nemanja "⁠huNter-⁠" Kovač's troops and the trophy are Legacy, who have already won two Chinese events in the last 12 months.

BETBOOM got off to a better start in the series, racing away to a 7-2 lead before a couple of crucial multikills by Matúš "⁠MATYS⁠" Šimko reduced the deficit to just two rounds at the switch.

The Slovakian rifler kept up his impact on the CT side and finished the map on a 1.62 rating, 0.81 ahead of his closest teammate, but it was BETBOOM who took the map 13-7 after Kirill "⁠Magnojez⁠" Rodnov's 1vs3 clutch.

Anubis was a much closer affair, with G2 this time dominating the early rounds on their T side. They looked to be breezing to a comfortable victory before Aleksandr "⁠zorte⁠" Zagodyrenko clutched a crucial 1vs3 late in the second half, helping BETBOOM secure overtime.

That was when a clutch finally went the way of G2, as Guy "⁠NertZ⁠" Iluz fake-planted his way to a 1vs3 clutch and secured the Ancient decider.

NertZ, who had a fantastic map on Anubis (1.62 map rating), was also impactful on Ancient, where his 21 kills, 110.5 ADR, and a 1.54 rating helped G2 secure a spot in the grand final.
