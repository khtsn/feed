yxngstxr departs HEROIC

HEROIC have announced the departure of Simon "⁠yxngstxr⁠" Boije from the organization to set off on a "new journey."

The Swede joined HEROIC in 2025 and fulfilled a plethora of roles on the team, including returning from the bench at the start of 2026 following Alimzhan "⁠Alkaren⁠" Bitimbay's visa issues to fulfill the AWP role.

Since moving to the inactive roster, he stood in for EYEBALLERS BLAST Bounty 2026 Season 2 and turned out for the Raccoons mix-team across several online competitions.
