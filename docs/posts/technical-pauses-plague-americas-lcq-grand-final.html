Technical pauses plague Americas LCQ grand final

Author update: As of 10:06 p.m. EDT, the match's broadcast has started up again.

Author update: As of 10:28 p.m. EDT, the match has resumed.

The battle for the final ticket to the Americas Play-Ins between Shopify Rebellion Black Shopify Rebellion Black North America Rank #6 zander Alexander Dituri satellite exa Kim Lee-hwan (김이환) Smoke NaturE Nicholas Garrison and 2Game Esports 2Game Esports Brazil Rank #3 Brinks João Victor gobera Vitor Gobo qck Gabriel Lima xenom Eduardo Soeiro frz Leandro Gomes was on-and-off for over six hours. LIGA Ace, the tournament organizer, released a statement at 8:40 p.m. EDT clarifying that, after discussions with both teams, the technical team had been granted “a little longer” to resolve the issue before any other decisions were made.

The best-of-five match kicked off at around 3:08 p.m. as SRB and 2G met after a day of Round Robin play. While 2G's first pick of Ascent was completed in full in a 13-10 victory for the Brazilian representative, technical issues stalled the commencement of the second map of play, Split.

After nearly 90 minutes of delay, the teams began map two. However, disaster struck again in round 12 when players and production alike experienced major lag spikes that assisted SRB in converting a 2-man deficit into a round win.

Brinks shared his point of view on X.

An additional 52 minutes went by before the second half of Split resumed. However, one round passed before the lag spikes returned in round 14 and viewers were sent back to another 52 minutes of technical pause before the broadcast streams went offline.

After 92 minutes of downtime, the broadcast streams were booted up once again at 10:06 p.m. and the second half of Split resumed 22 minutes later.
