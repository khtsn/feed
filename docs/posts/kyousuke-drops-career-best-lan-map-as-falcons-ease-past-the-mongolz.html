kyousuke drops career-best LAN map as Falcons ease past The MongolZ

Striker

20-8-2026 14:42

The 18-year-old Opener stole the show on Inferno with a 26-7 score and 2.54 rating.

Falcons passed their first playoffs match in Paris with flying colors

Falcons have advanced to the quarter-finals at Esports World Cup off the back of a convincing 2-0 victory over The MongolZ in the round of 16.

Maksim "⁠kyousuke⁠" Lukin stole the show on Inferno, where Falcons' game plan was clear: take the fight to The MongolZ with aggression from all sides on the CT side.

The 18-year-old dropped his career-best map on LAN with a 2.54 rating and 26-7 scoreline, farming multi-kill after multi-kill in Banana pushes that had Finn "⁠karrigan⁠" Andersen laughing as he had to hold off his utility to avoid raining it down on his red-hot teammate.

Dust2 was more closely-contested at the start, until Nikola "⁠NiKo⁠" Kovač swayed the first half in Falcons' favor, calmly tapping away for a 1v3 clutch at the close.

And once Falcons' had weaponry in the second half, they were in cruise control on the CT side to run down the map 13-9 and the series with a quick 2-0.

The IEM Cologne Major champions will next face off against Legacy after the Brazilians took Natus Vincere by surprise in their round of 16 affair.
