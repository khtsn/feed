Luminosity sign yxngstxr to replace Bymas

Luminosity has announced the signing of Simon "⁠yxngstxr⁠" Boije from HEROIC, bringing its active roster back up to full strength after moving Aurimas "⁠Bymas⁠" Pipiras to the bench on Monday.

Luminosity bench Bymas

yxngstxr will be happy to find a permanent home after spending the first half of 2026 in limbo, having been moved to HEROIC's bench at the start of the year before returning to the roster as an AWPer while the organization worked to resolve Alimzhan "⁠Alkaren⁠" Bitimbay's visa issues.

As their interim sniper, the Swede averaged a 1.00 rating, helped HEROIC qualify for Stage 1 of the IEM Cologne Major, and secured title victories at DraculaN Season 5 and Stake Ranked Episode 3.

"After 2 rough seasons, it's finally time to go and start the real rifle grind," yxngstxr said in a post on X.

He joins the roster as a replacement for Bymas, who was moved to the bench after the former Monte core with Lucas "⁠lux⁠" Meneghini debuted under Luminosity's banner with consecutive last-place finishes at XSE Pro League Guangzhou and Esports World Cup.

yxngstxr will debut for Luminosity at Roman Imperium Cup VIII, scheduled to run from September 10-13.
