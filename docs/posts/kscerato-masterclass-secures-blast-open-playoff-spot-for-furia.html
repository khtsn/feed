KSCERATO masterclass secures BLAST Open playoff spot for FURIA

FURIA became the second team to qualify for the BLAST Open Porto playoffs after defeating M80 in a two-map series (Cache 16-13, Dust2 13-5).

The majority-Brazilian squad has their talisman Kaike "⁠KSCERATO⁠" Cerato to thank for the victory. Their long-standing member put up a 1.86-rated performance on the Cache opener, racking up 32 kills, 99.9 ADR, and a +6.51% round swing in the overtime victory, while being 0.79 ahead of his closest teammate Mareks "⁠YEKINDAR⁠" Gaļinskis.

"I just changed my crosshair," KSCERATO said about his performance on a broadcast interview. "I'm definitely keeping this one for Porto."

Their pick of Dust2 was much more comfortable for KSCERATO and company, with the Brazilian once again leading the way for his team. Gabriel "⁠FalleN⁠" Toledo found a crucial 1vs2 that put FURIA on the path to success early on, but KSCERATO once again finished the game as the highest-rated player on the server.

A 2.27-rated T-side, during which he won all five of his openers and found four multikills, set the scene for a one-sided win for FURIA, who waltzed into the playoffs with a 13-5 victory.
