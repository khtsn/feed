ESL Pro League S24 grand final as it happened

Shahar "⁠flameZ⁠" Shushan, speaking on the desk after the match:

On what he has to say about the game: We like to play the MOUZ boys because... I don't know, we like to beat them because of Spinx leaving us, because of many reasons, we always want to play them, and I'm happy we played them in the final now. [apEX: 'Sounds personal'] A bit, a bit (laughs) ... but we knew today there was no chance we lose this. We never want to give trophies to anybody, but once we're in the grand final, we want to win, and we're continuing what we know to do: bringing trophies to Vitality.

Dan "⁠apEX⁠" Madesclaire, speaking on the desk after the match:

On their record versus MOUZ: They used to have some good wins in 2025 as well, they beat Spirit, et cetera, but when we play like that, they just can't beat us. I heard one of them say that it was the same with jL, but of course it's not the same when you play with your full lineup or you play with a stand-in. They deserved the win last time, but we played better today and we were the better team.

On missing the next tournament: Thanks to my partner and the baby that didn't get out too early. I could win another trophy at least. Thanks to her and him to have waited for me, because I'm happy that i could go for a break with happiness."
