Spinx: "They have mezii now[...] but that doesn't mean it's a much stronger version. Maybe it is, maybe it's not, we'll see on the server"

vs.

Lotan "⁠Spinx⁠" Giladi spoke to Freya "⁠Freya⁠" Spiers on the broadcast ahead of the grand final, and he spoke on the change in Dorian "⁠xertioN⁠" Berman since becoming IGL and his thoughts on facing a full strength Vitality.

On xertioN: A big shift especially in the attitude and his mentality. I think in the past he could get tilted a bit, like 'what the fuck are we doing, this flash should have blinded him,' stuff like this.

He will still get frustrated, but the reset he has right now... you can feel he's tilted, but for exactly one second and it's instantly reset next round. It helps us a lot.

On facing the full strength Vitality: They will be better for sure compared to the last time we faced them. They have mezii now, who is a strong player and he really suits the team and the roles, which was probably a bit harder for jL.

But still, that doesn't mean it's a much stronger version. Maybe it is, maybe it's not, we'll see today on the server. We are confident and we are happy to be here.

Vitality seek back-to-back trophies for the first time in nearly six months

vs.

Six months ago we were firmly in the Vitality era as Dan "⁠apEX⁠" Madesclaire and co. had just completed a streak of five event wins in a row, were looking nigh unbeatable, and seemed a lock to secure another Major title in the coming months.

Fast forward to now and things look very different. A combination of some breaks from practice and the absence of William "⁠mezii⁠" Merriman for BLAST Open Porto resulted in the breaking of Vitality's momentum, as they went five tournaments in a row without a trophy.

With the full lineup back in action at StarSeries Fall Vitality got back to winning ways, but that tournament did not feature their upcoming opponents, MOUZ. Can Vitality bag another trophy to go back-to-back once again, and reassert themselves as the era-defining squad?
