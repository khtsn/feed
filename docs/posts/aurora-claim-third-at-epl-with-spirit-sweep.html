Aurora claim third at EPL with Spirit sweep

Aurora have taken third place at ESL Pro League 24 with a comfortable 2-0 beating of Spirit, securing their victory with wins on Mirage (13-9) and Anubis (13-6).

The match certainly meant more to Aurora than Spirit, with the newly-minted international lineup hoping to legitimize their recent improvements and get closer to a Stage 3 berth for Singapore Major.

Those facts bore out on the server, with Aurora seeming sharper in every department as Spirit fell to a lacklustre defeat, frustration evident on their player cams throughout much of the series.

Wicadia Dualies ace kicks off third-place decider

Aurora should have secured Mirage more quickly than they did, as the European combine raced out to a 6-1 lead off the back of a pistol win and a couple of very convincing gun rounds. Spirit, however, were able to break Aurora's momentum with some low-buy wins and secure five rounds by the half.

Another strong start to the second half kept Aurora in the driver's seat, and although Andrey "⁠tN1R⁠" Tatarinovich and Dmitry "⁠sh1ro⁠" Sokolov offered a bit of resistance, Aurora duly marched to a victory on their opponent's map pick.

Aurora outplay Spirit in the mid round despite insane sh1ro MP9 spray transfer

Anubis was even more one-sided, the frustration apparent on Spirit's player cams as they simply couldn't play themselves into the game. Aurora were better in the mid rounds, got more firepower out of their individuals, and eventually sealed a comfortable 13-6 win to close the series.
