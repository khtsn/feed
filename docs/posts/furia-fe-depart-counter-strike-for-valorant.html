FURIA fe depart Counter-Strike for VALORANT

FURIA has announced that its women's Counter-Strike team, FURIA fe, will completely transition to VALORANT.

FURIA fe won several titles in South America during their six-year tenure with the organization, and notably finished as the runners-up at three ESL Impact League Finals before finally claiming the trophy in Season 7.

The news comes as a severe blow to the already fractured women's scene, which saw several organizations part ways with their lineups after the ESL Impact circuit was put on an indefinite hold at the end of 2025.

Mayline-Joy "⁠ASTRA⁠" Champliaud, who was named the best women's player of 2025, also announced her transition to VALORANT in early August, citing "female CS falling apart and the lack of tournaments coming up."
