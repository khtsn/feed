BIT: "If we make it back to the playoffs consistently, I'm sure at some point we'll win another tournament"

9z delivered the first upset of the Thunderpick World Championship in Malta, beating World No.5 Aurora in two maps and sealing the win with a near-flawless performance on Nuke.

It was a much-needed victory for the South American side after a rough patch of form. Since the title run at XSE Pro League Guangzhou in July, the team had won just four out of 13 matches, dropping down to 17th in the global VRS.

"It was important, mainly because we had started to doubt a lot of things that we had been doing, rounds, some things, some calls, and that starts to make a difference when you're not on the same page while you're playing the match," Bruno "⁠BIT⁠" Fukuda Lima told HLTV after the series.

"Even though we won some matches in EPL, it had been a while since we'd beaten a really tier-1 team. We beat BETBOOM, a strong team, and we beat TYLOO, too. Those were two important wins, but I think since July we hadn't beaten a tier-1 team. So it was an important match. I'm optimistic now that we'll be able to get more wins like this one."

HLTV's latest projections put 9z as having a 60% chance of booking a spot in Stage 2 of the Singapore Major. BIT said he is more worried about finding consistency in the team's game and that "things will fall into place for the Major."

"We had a pretty interesting run of several playoffs at some point in the first half of the year," he said. "Now, obviously we go into every tournament to win, but we have to go step by step. If we can make it back to the playoffs consistently, I'm sure that at some point we'll win another tournament."

Congratulations on the win. First of all, I wanted to get your opinion on this match against a team you lost to very recently in Pro League.

It was a good match on our part today. We tried a different approach this time. The last two times we played against them, we picked Dust2 and we didn't have much of a chance. We ended up switching to Inferno, which gave us a chance to start winning the series.

Starting off winning a series also gives a bit more confidence, which we've been needing. They also ended up changing their pick; they usually pick Mirage against us, and today they played Nuke. We know Nuke is hard when you start on the T side and things aren't clicking. I think we managed to impose our rhythm on them.

Individually, we were very sharp today too: HUASOPEEK in the pistols, luchov… Everyone had good moments, good reads, and that makes all the difference.

As you said, you needed a confidence boost after some not-so-positive results. Aurora was coming off a very good stretch, with a third place at EPL. How important was this result and this performance for the team's morale?

It was important, mainly because we had started to doubt a lot of things that we had been doing — rounds, some things, some calls — and that starts to make a difference when you're not on the same page while you're playing the match. Even though we won some matches in EPL, it had been a while since we'd beaten a really tier-one team.

We beat BETBOOM, a strong team, and we beat TYLOO, too. Those were two important wins, but I think since July we hadn't beaten a tier-1 team. So it was an important match.  I'm optimistic now that we'll be able to get more wins like this one.

On Inferno, in the second half, there were some rounds you gave away, including a 5v2. Do you think the nerves were getting to the players?

Yeah, there were a lot of rounds there where we really overcommitted too much; we had a lot of advantage, played in very all-in positions, and that ended up giving them the chance to win those rounds. I don't know if it's because we were doing really well in the match and then you really get a bit more confident, you know? It's hard to have that balance, because to beat these teams you also have to have a lot of confidence, you have to have a strong aim, you have to get the openings and kills. But at the same time, you also have to know, especially in these moments, how to use a bit of the team to play together, use a bit of information. I think it's a bit of both.

We got a little anxious at some points, but luckily we managed to keep our cool. At no point did we get frustrated, at no point did we complain to each other about how we died, about having lost a round we'd given away. And I think that was important.

Let's talk a little bit about this start of the season for 9z. After that win in China, a series of less positive results, EWC, BLAST Porto, FISSURE, and Pro League. How did the team react to these results? What have you been focusing on to stay confident and, at the end of the day, keep believing in your work?

I've already been part of many teams, and I know each team has a different mindset. There are a lot of teams that fight, there are a lot of teams that manage to turn things around one way or another. We're a team that manages to keep our feet on the ground, stay calm, try to look for new solutions, swap positions, think differently.

We're a team that works hard, that tries to look for new solutions all the time, and we have to see how far we can get by changing things, and we'll reach the moment when things go right. We have a lot of faith that that's going to happen.

Looking at this final stretch of the Major race, you have this tournament and then Bucharest. What are your goals for this tournament? I'd assume that getting a spot in Stage 2, at this point, is your main goal, right?

I, as the coach, am involved in practically everything regarding planning; I'm keeping track of this Major situation. We've already reached, at least at this point, a stage where we're not thinking so much about the Major invite. We're thinking about the tournaments we're playing, we're thinking about making the playoffs here, we're thinking about making the playoffs in Bucharest and going further and further.

Obviously, I don't know if we still have much of a chance to reach Stage 3, but we're still going for a Stage 2 spot. I don't think we spend that much time these days thinking about the Major. We're focused on each tournament we play and on doing well in the tournaments we're currently playing. As a result, things will fall into place for the Major.

So for this tournament, getting to the semi-finals is the big goal, would you say?

We want to get back to the playoffs. We had a pretty interesting run of several playoffs at some point in the first half of the year. Now, obviously we go into every tournament to win, but we have to go step by step. If we can make it back to the playoffs consistently, I'm sure that at some point we'll win another tournament.
