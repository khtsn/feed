Without a Roof officially seek roof

WaR trio autimatic, Stewie2k and CeRq with EG at the PGL Antwerp Major RMR in 2022

The 2022 Evil Geniuses quartet of Timothy "⁠autimatic⁠" Ta, Jake "⁠Stewie2K⁠" Yip, Tsvetelin "⁠CeRq⁠" Dimitrov and Vincent "⁠Brehze⁠" Cayonte, with Justin "⁠FaNg⁠" Coakley as a fifth, who have been playing under the Without a Roof tag, have announced that they are looking for an organization to represent. Another NA veteran, Joshua "⁠steel⁠" Nissan, is the team's coach.

Without a Roof reunited before FRAG St. Louis and finished the LAN in third place after losses to LAG and SportsBetExpert. They then finished the online NSTLGA League Season 2 in second place, again behind SportsBetExpert.

After failing to qualify for PGL Bucharest, Without a Roof claimed their first victory, the ESEA Season 58 North America Finals, getting revenge on SportsBetExpert in the title match.

Without a Roof, 98th in the global VRS and 21st in the Americas, are currently playing CROSSFIRE Season 6 and confirmed to Dust2.us that they plan on attending the FRAG University LAN in Syracuse, NY, on October 9-11.
