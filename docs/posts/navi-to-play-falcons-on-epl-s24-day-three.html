NAVI to play Falcons on EPL S24 day three

Following the second day of action of ESL Pro League Season 24, during which Vitality and MOUZ secured spots in the Spodek arena, ESL has announced the matchups for day three.

Every match will be played for either progression or elimination on Tuesday, with the standout clash being Falcons vs. Natus Vincere in the 2-1 pool.

Aleksi "⁠Aleksib⁠" Virolainen's troops, who already met G2, Vitality, and 9z, thus continue a nightmare bracket in Katowice as they look to secure a playoff spot.

Elsewhere in the 2-1 pool, FURIA meet Aurora, and Spirit play 1win in a domestic derby.

G2 vs. PARIVISION is the standout game of the elimination pool, with a loss for Dzhami "⁠Jame⁠" Ali's troops putting their Singapore Major qualification in jeopardy.
