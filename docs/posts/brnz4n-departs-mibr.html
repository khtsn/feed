brnz4n departs MIBR

MIBR have announced the departure of Breno "⁠brnz4n⁠" Poletto, who was benched from the starting lineup in August to make way for Tomas "⁠tomaszin⁠" Corna.

The news brings to an end a long association between MIBR and brnz4n, the 23-year-old having joined the organization in 2021 as part of the academy setup.

After impressing on the academy squad brnz4n was promoted to the main team to replace outgoing AWPer Adriano "⁠WOOD7⁠" Cerato mid-way through 2022, and became a versatile member of the squad through many iterations.

brnz4n was briefly benched at the end of 2024 in favor of Lucas "⁠Lucaozy⁠" Neves, but returned to the lineup early the next year and continued his time with the squad until his recent replacement by tomaszin.

brnz4n won numerous regional titles during his time with MIBR and even enjoyed modest international success, winning ESL Challenger Melbourne 2024. He also attended three Majors with MIBR, his best finish coming at BLAST.tv Austin Major, when MIBR knocked out Falcons and BETBOOM before narrowly missing out on a Stage 3 berth.
