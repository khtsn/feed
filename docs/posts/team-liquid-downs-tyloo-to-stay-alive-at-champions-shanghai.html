The series was played out on TL's Split, TYL's Summit, and the decider map of Lotus. In the map ban veto, Liquid banned Sunset and Ascent, while TYLOO banned Haven and Abyss. Liquid won Split 13-8, TYLOO won Summit 13-7, and Liquid won Lotus 13-2.

Liquid's pick of Split seemed to highlight its most recent roster changes best. With GSR on Yoru and kamo on Raze, the team was able to implement a double-Duelist look that played into its strengths. However, it wasn't all easy all the time. After getting out to a 4-0 lead, TYLOO snatched seven straight rounds with its own double-Duelist, Jett-Raze comp, but that momentum wasn't held up.

kamo got out to a blistering start on Split, going 16/8 in the first half on Split.

Liquid's attack side, led by the aforementioned pair of Duelists, was utterly dominant. The pair combined for 25 kills on that side alone, dropping just one round en route to what ended up being a smooth 13-8 win.

On its pick of Summit, TYLOO rolled out its Phoenix-Waylay two Duelist comp, but Liquid rolled out an all-new comp, one that was seemingly made possible thanks to the GSR signing. With yet another Yoru-Raze look, the team adopted a solo Harbor comp with in-game leader nAts on Vyse. On defense, Liquid's comp put in work. Behind GSR and kamo once again, Liquid won the early engagements, entering halftime up 7-5, but the second half was a different story.

To this point, China had been winless in Shanghai as a region, both in matches and in maps. That changed with TYLOO's attack, led by slowly 's Waylay. The Duelist player had 10 kills in the half, with great retake protocols shutting down Liquid's attack, with eight straight rounds closing out a 13-7 win.
