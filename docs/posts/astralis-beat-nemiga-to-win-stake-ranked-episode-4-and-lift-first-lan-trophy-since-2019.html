Astralis beat Nemiga to win Stake Ranked Episode 4 and lift first LAN trophy since 2019

Staehr posted a series-high 1.41 rating in the final

Astralis beat Nemiga 3-1 in the grand final of Stake Ranked Episode 4 on Sunday, capping a remarkable lower bracket run after losing their opening match in Barcelona to Alliance.

It's the first LAN title for the Danish organization since the BLAST Pro Series Global Final in 2019 in Bahrain with their iconic lineup. (In January 2024, a different Astralis roster won Copenhagen Gaming Week, but it was a showmatch event.)

Tonight's victory was decorated by a great performance from Victor "⁠Staehr⁠" Staehr, who played a key role in each of his team's map wins. He leaves Barcelona with a tournament-high 1.27 rating.

The Barcelona run sees Astralis enter the top 10 for the first time since June. Despite a sour finish, Nemiga have plenty of positives to take from their Spanish trip: they are now ranked 15th in the world, with a projected 68.1% chance of qualifying for the Singapore Major.

Jakob "⁠jabbi⁠" Nygaard was the hero on Mirage, posting a 2.30 rating as Astralis cruised to an easy win to open up the series.

Nemiga hit back on Ancient with a strong CT showing, but Astralis put the series to rest with standout performances on Nuke and Cache, with Staehr posting a 2.02 rating on the former before picking up an ace clutch on the latter.

"I had to wait a little bit for that reward," said Filip "⁠NEO⁠" Kubski, who took over as coach in the summer. "It's a different culture. Different people, different vibes. It's very nice, I like it here. The guys are working very hard. We can see some progress here and there.

"There are always some bad moments. But we keep on working. We stay as a team. It feels nice to finally lift a trophy."
