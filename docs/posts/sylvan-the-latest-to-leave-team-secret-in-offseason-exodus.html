Sylvan the latest to leave Team Secret in offseason exodus

The Team Secret Team Secret Asia-Pacific Rank #37 natz Jonathan Adiputra Zeus Jhian Vega offseason exodus continues, with Controller player Go "Sylvan" Young-sup (고영섭) the latest member of the team to announce their departure from the team.

Sylvan shared that while he is still under contract with the org, Team Secret has allowed him to explore his options going into the 2027 season. He also said that he is looking for opportunities as a Controller, and is open to all regions. VCT's Global Contract Database details that Sylvan's deal with Secret was set to expire this offseason.

Sylvan on stage with Team Secret in Stage 2. (Photo by Riot Games)

The last two years have been a whirlwind for Sylvan. He landed with T1 T1 Korea Rank #2 stax Kim Gu-taek (김구택) Meteor Kim Tae-oh (김태오) BuZz Yu Byeong-cheol (유병철) DH Kang Dong-ho (강동호) iZu Ham Woo-ju (함우주) Munchkin Byeon Sang-beom (변상범) in the offseason as the team's go-to Controller player, but was benched after one match in Pacific Kickoff for veteran carpe . Sylvan returned at Masters Bangkok, where he played his part in the org lifting its first-ever VCT title and Pacific's second-ever international trophy. T1 returned to regional play but failed to live up to the standards set in Bangkok, finishing Stage 1 in 7th-8th. During the midseason break, Sylvan was benched, and seemingly for the rest of the year.

Sylvan was out of action until the offseason, when he landed with Team Secret as a free agent as part of a multinational rebuild for the org. TS's roster to start the year had five players of three different Asian nationalities, a trend that remained across three different rosters fielded through the three regional splits of the year. While Secret ended the year ranked 13th-16th in Pacific with three wins in VCT play, Sylvan had a strong individual year. He finished Stage 1 as Secret's highest-rated player and Stage 2 as the team's second-highest-rated player.

Team Secret's offseason outlook appears dim with Sylvan the latest to leave. In recent weeks, head coach Spin , analyst skye , in-game leader STYRON , and kellyS have all been allowed to explore their options.
