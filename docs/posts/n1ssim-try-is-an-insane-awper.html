Following the derby, Vinicius "⁠n1ssim⁠" Pereira took some time to talk to HLTV about his time on the bench, beating FURIA, playing with AWPer Santino "⁠try⁠" Rigal, and how Legacy — the CS Asia Championships 2025 and 2026 champions — shine in China.

Congrats on making the semi-finals over FURIA. How happy are you with your run so far?

I think we're playing really good in this tournament. It's like a tier two, three tournament-ish, but I think we're being pretty good.

And you won over FURIA for the second time this year. Is there anything that makes a game against them special for you?

For me, no. But I think the Brazilian scene has a flame to the game when the top two Brazilian teams play. But I think it's a normal game, and I just want to beat them.

Do you have that aspiration of being the top Brazilian team?

Not really. I mean, I want us to be the best team in the world. I don't care about the top one Americas or top one Brazilian team. I just want to be top one.

try has been playing with you for a couple of months now. What do you think of what he has brought to the team?

I think try is an insane AWPer; he has an insane game sense as well, and he has insane mid calls. So I think he brings solid work for AWP.

You had this situation mid-season when you got benched, and then you were back on the team. Can you tell what happened?

Yeah. I benched myself in the middle of the year because I was expecting some changes, and the changes didn't happen. And then I was expecting some offers, and I got some offers, but not good ones. And then the Legacy CEO asked me to come back, and I was doing nothing at home, so I just came back.

How happy are you that it turned out like this, considering you as Legacy now seem to be pretty strong?

Yeah. We've been really pretty good. I think when I came back, I came back with another mindset about my gameplay. So I think I've been playing much better, and I'm helping the team. And with the try addition, I think it helps me too. So I think, yeah, we are a solid team right now.

Did it help you specifically that you stepped out and then stepped back as if it's like a new fresh stint?

Yeah, yeah. For me, I think it's [the case] as well. I think when I stepped out and stepped back in again, I came back with another mindset.

So you won two events in China, which is crazy. And now you're just two matches away from winning another one. Do you think your good China luck is still there with you now?

Yeah, I think we still have the China luck. I don't know why, to be honest. I don't know if it's the crowd or if it's hotel rooms; I have no idea. So I think that does make a really good thing for us as well. China good luck, I guess.

Who do you think would be the toughest opponent to meet in the grand final considering you won over FURIA, the highest-ranked team at the event?

To be really honest with you, I don't know what teams are playing in the tournament because, since I've been in China, I just sleep all day because of jet lag. I think I saw G2 here. I think it's FURIA, G2, Astralis... Oh, they're eliminated, okay. G2 is still in the tournament? Yeah, G2 or FURIA.
