Short: Riot introduces new rifle Warden in patch 13.06

The competitive style of play may soon change with the introduction of a new rifle, the Warden, which will be released on September 22 through patch 13.06.

Warden Statistics

  • Weapon Class: Rifle
  • Price: 2900 credits
  • Projectile type: Bullet
  • Attachments: Scope (2x magnification)
  • Bullets per magazine: 18
  • Damage Per Bullet: 50 at all ranges | 200 headshot | 42 leg
  • Fire Rate: 6.5
  • Ammo Reserve: 36
  • Reload Timing: 2.5
  • Equip Timing: Normal: 1.0 | Fast: .6 | Instant: .2
  • Wall Penetration: Medium

“Joining the Vandal and Phantom, VALORANT is introducing the Warden to its list of automatic rifles,” stated Riot. “The Warden aims to expand player choice by offering another viable primary weapon, with the rifle designed with a stronger Aim Down Sights (ADS) option and high-damage output.”

The Warden (Image: Riot Games)

Additional Features

Additional features arriving in the future are:

  • Gauntlet: Glitched game mode

  • Competitive Updates (competitive profile improvements, evolving "Competitive Flex")
  • New Agent (planned post-Champions)
