BLAST Open Porto playoff bracket set

Falcons and G2 will face off in a rematch of the teams' Stage 3 opener in Cologne.

The group stage of BLAST Open Porto has concluded with six teams moving on from BLAST's studios in Copenhagen to the Super Bock Arena in Portugal.

Spirit and MOUZ advanced directly to the semi-finals after beating FURIA and Falcons in their respective group winners' matches, while Vitality and G2 recovered in the lower bracket to clinch the final quarter-final spots.

As a result, the first playoff match will see Nikola "⁠NiKo⁠" Kovač and Nemanja "⁠huNter-⁠" Kovač face off in a rematch of Falcons and G2's Stage 3 opener at the IEM Cologne Major, while FURIA and Vitality will lock horns for the first time since IEM Rio.

A potential Cologne Major semi-final rematch is also in the cards if Falcons advance as they loss in the group winners' match has set them on the same side of the bracket as Spirit.
