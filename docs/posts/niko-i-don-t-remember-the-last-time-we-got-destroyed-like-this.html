NiKo: "I don't remember the last time we got destroyed like this"

NiKo had a match to forget

Falcons have been eliminated from the Esports World Cup after suffering a stunning 0-2 loss to Legacy.

Shortly after the match, Nikola "⁠NiKo⁠" Kovač stopped by HLTV's microphone and discussed the team's off performance, describing it as "a shock" after their campaign in Paris had previously gone without a hitch.

"We just looked like a completely different team to the previous three games," he said. "Just a very poor performance today from us. We had higher ambitions for this event, so it's super disappointing to go out like this. I don't remember the last time we got destroyed like this."

Very frustrating with how I played today

Like everyone on the team other than Ilya "⁠m0NESY⁠" Osipov, NiKo had a match to forget, recording a 0.76 rating over the two maps.

"I couldn't really play my game for some reason," the Bosnian explained. "I couldn't dictate the tempo, take the duels on my own terms. So yeah, very frustrating with how I played today as well. I tried to fight back a little bit on Dust2 CT, but perhaps a bit too late."

NiKo, commiserations, a tough loss to Legacy. I guess a big shock to the team because so far the tournament had gone pretty well. Can you tell me about it?

Yeah, it's definitely a shock. Just looked like a completely different team to the previous three games that we had played. Props to Legacy for playing amazing. Even though we knew what to expect, we prepared, maybe the game plan didn't work out as we had expected, I would say. And yeah, just a very poor performance today from us. We expected better. We had higher ambitions for this event, and... just super disappointing to go out like this. I don't remember the last time we got destroyed like this. So it's a tough one.

What do you really put it down to? Because obviously only m0NESY was also on individually. Do you feel like it was just an off match from you guys individually, or was there something not clicking? What was happening really?

I think it was a combination of everything. As you mentioned, Ilya was on point really, especially on Mirage to start with. Us playing, maybe we never managed to get on the level that we usually are. We were very slow in the rounds, maybe a bit too hesitant, perhaps a bit too scared as well.

Maybe we focused too much on their play style and how to counter them instead of just playing to our strengths a little bit. Very weird feeling, very weird feeling, something that we usually don't have, so I really just want to take a look at the game before making further assumptions on what went wrong.

Can you at least tell me about what Legacy did that made it so difficult? Because it felt like even I was watching from your point of view, a lot of the times you didn't even have a chance in the duels. Was it just that they were so on point individually, and try had a really good game as well?

As I said earlier, props to them. I think they were on point with both individually with the game plan and the attitude as well. They didn't really care who they went up against. They just stuck to their game style and they just fully executed the game plan. Even though on Mirage, for example, we knew what they were going to do, we punished them, they just kept going harder and harder. So they were on point.

It just felt like, personally, I couldn't really play my game for some reason. I couldn't dictate the tempo, take the duels on my own terms. So yeah, very frustrating with how I played today as well. I tried to fight back a little bit on Dust2 CT, but perhaps a bit too late.
