FUT confirm SunPayus signing

FUT have announced that they have completed the signing of G2 benched AWPer Álvaro "⁠SunPayus⁠" García, confirming a September 16 report by HLTV. The 27-year-old will replace Nikita "⁠cmtry⁠" Samolotov, ending the Ukrainian's long tenure with the roster.

SunPayus has been out of action since the Cologne Major, with G2 benching him in the summer break — a move that paved the way for the arrival of Artem "⁠r1nkle⁠" Moroz from Ninjas in Pyjamas.

The Spaniard brings a wealth of experience to a young team that had an average age of 20.3 years. He should act as a secondary caller, bringing a considerable upgrade over cmtry with the AWP, albeit at the cost of significantly weaker rifling production.
