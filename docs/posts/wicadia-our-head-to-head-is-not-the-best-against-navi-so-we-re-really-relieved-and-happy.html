Wicadia: "Our head-to-head is not the best against NAVI, so we're really relieved and happy"

Aurora kicked off StarSeries Fall with a victory against Natus Vincere, a team that has gotten the best of them four times this year, albeit with the full Turkish roster.

"We played some good CS sometimes," Ali "⁠Wicadia⁠" Haydar Yalçın told HLTV about Aurora's first months with the international roster, "but we couldn't finish it. We were almost winning against FURIA and the G2 games."

Up next for Aurora in the tournament's upper bracket semi-final is NRG. "I don't think we should underestimate them," the 21-year-old warns, "because they just beat MOUZ, and I think they're a great team."

Listen to the interview here.

So we're here at StarSeries Fall with Wicadia. Aurora just got maybe the first big win against NAVI. Give me the feeling of being able to beat a team like NAVI.

I think after our roster changes, we played some good CS sometimes, but we couldn't finish it. We were almost winning against FURIA and the G2 games and stuff. So it was important for us to come to this event and get the first win, and it's against NAVI. Our head-to-head is not the best against them, so we are really relieved and happy.

The last time you beat NAVI I think it was under Eternal Fire, it wasn't even Aurora. So how is it not only the international roster getting this win, but also getting it against NAVI?

Every single important final or quarters, or whatever, we lost against them, so it's feeling really good.

You've had two, three months to kind of feel out the new roster. How is it going? How is the evolution?

I think we are getting used to each other day by day. Four or five different people from different countries, so it's going to take time to be at our best shape, but I think every day we are getting better.

I think we are getting used to each other day by day

And what have you noticed? How are you getting better? What's improving?

We are just playing the game and keep fixing the mistakes and working on them, I can say.

And so where do you see the potential in this team, or how good do you think you guys can be?

I don't want to talk big. I think we can be one of the contenders if we keep working hard.

How has it been, also maybe with the big changes since leaving the full Turkish lineup to now? How has it been adapting to maybe having to struggle a little bit more and not having the same momentum as you used to have?

I think recently I talked about it and people misunderstood me. Everyone on the team has good English, and we can communicate daily. But we are playing for... only me for four years, in a Turkish team, and the others for more. So it's hard to sometimes call some stuff in the mid-rounds. But every day, as I said, it's getting better.

It's hard to sometimes call some stuff in the mid-rounds

Yeah, I was listening to XANTARES. He's come a long way, it seems, with the English as well. He was very vocal. So how's the communication going? How have you noticed the improvements?

I think for us it's also important to get these wins for our confidence and stuff, so we keep improving. But I can say everyone has good English, and it's going to be better for sure.

And I know Jimpphat, in one of the previous interviews he did with us, he was talking about how sometimes you guys lose against yourselves, and he was talking about improving the mental aspect of the game. Is that something that you guys have been working on?

Yeah, of course. It was always our kind of weak side, but I think right now we are improving it as well.

And what do you think is improving? Or how are you improving it?

We are talking about it. I don't know. In-game, we are trying to stay more calm and stuff. I think most of the stuff we do is by ourselves. We work on ourselves, every single one.

In-game, we are trying to stay more calm

So everybody has to kind of take individual responsibility?

Yeah, of course. Everybody has a different mental, and they should work on it, I think.

Now you got this first big win here against NAVI. What do you see as some next objectives that Aurora can conquer? What would you like to achieve?

We are going to play NRG next. I think everybody is strong, and we need to take each one by one. So we will just prepare for the NRG game and see what happens next.

How do you see that match-up against NRG? How do you think you match up against them?

Tough. I don't think we should underestimate them, because they just beat MOUZ, and I think they're a great team.
