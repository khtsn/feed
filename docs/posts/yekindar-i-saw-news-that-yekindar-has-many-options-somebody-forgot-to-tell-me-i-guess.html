YEKINDAR: "I saw news that 'YEKINDAR has many options.' Somebody forgot to tell me, I guess"

FURIA are through to the semi-finals at the Esports World Cup after beating G2, once again denying an opponent an attempt at an Ancient punish pick after doing the same to Aurora on the previous day.

After the match, we caught up with Mareks "⁠YEKINDAR⁠" Gaļinskis to ask him about the team's decision to start playing Ancient, which had been their permaban, as well as rumors that are floating around the team as Gabriel "⁠FalleN⁠" Toledo's retirement date is drawing nearer and nearer.

The Latvian addressed the rumors, which suggest he and Danil "⁠molodoy⁠" Golubenko could be out of the team, head-on.

It's completely logical that everybody's looking at options

"If things are not going to be working out, there's no good options [to replace FalleN] or whatever, it's completely normal for people to look for other perspectives," YEKINDAR began.

"We are working on it. Hopefully we're going to stay in four and we're going to find a good player to replace FalleN. But at the same time, it's completely logical that everybody's looking at options," he repeated.

"Though I saw news that 'Oh, YEKINDAR has many options.' I don't see the... somebody forgot to tell me, I guess, or something," he smirked.

You can watch or read the interview, in which YEKINDAR also spoke about taking over in-game leading on some maps in the new season and whether that could be his and FURIA's future, below:

Congratulations on making it through to the semi-finals here at EWC. First of all, I wanted to ask if you guys are an Ancient team now, or what's going on? Last two matches, both times they tried to punish pick you, and it didn't work.

Well, the thing is, after kind of the death of one of the strongest maps of the last season, which was Overpass, we knew we have to experiment a little bit with the map pool. If you just remove Overpass and we play the same other maps, our map pool is just not going to be strong enough, because Overpass was one of the best maps we had.

We had to experiment and we had to try something new, and I feel like when we practiced Ancient at the start of the season, even though maybe it was hard early in the season to showcase our map pool and play the strongest maps, which showed in Bounty as well, 'cause you had to test out how Cache is going to be feeling and whatever. I'm glad that it finally showcased that, even though we're not probably playing against the best Ancient teams, since Aurora is kind of permabanning Ancient as well, and then G2 has one of the lowest percentages on Ancient, but I think it's still a very good confidence booster for us.

But at the same time, we're still not in the state where we have a permaban. Right now we're kind of floating the things, trying to understand which map can work against which team, and that's it.

It was a logical step for us to try a little bit of me IGLing on some certain maps

We saw that T sides went pretty well, but you were missing a couple smokes, I think the red smoke that you guys were missing consistently for the first game. Did you feel any of that shakiness was coming through in these matches, or did you feel comfortable enough?

No, I feel all the situations that we had on Ancient were very good for us. I feel we always knew what was happening on the map. We always knew what's the next step to take, because we practiced many Ancients this season. And to a degree where all the rounds that made hard situations for us against Aurora, where we lost them, we were at the right place with the right amount of people. It just was more about the mechanical side of things. I had a very rough series against Aurora. It was very hard for me to perform, and if I would get an extra kill somewhere where I had to get a kill, then the score could've been completely different. So yeah.

FalleN was saying yesterday that you were calling the T side on Ancient. Can you tell me a little bit about that, and what went into that decision?

From the start of the season, we started experimenting a little bit, in a way where we don't necessarily know what the future is going to hold, and it was a logical step for us to try a little bit of me IGL-ing on some certain maps. I feel there's maps where mine and FalleN's vision kind of divide maybe a little bit in some certain situations, so it was interesting to try different perspectives. And specifically on Ancient, we agreed that I would call that game.

I don't think it's going to be continuing on that, because honestly, it's too much cortisol. Being an IGL, it's just such an ungrateful thing. I truly envy all the people who are IGLs and they can cope with that. But I don't know if this is going to be my future or FURIA's future, but we're trying to test out to understand in what direction we have to go after FalleN retires, and we're trying a lot of things right now, so we're probably not in our best shape.

Now that you're mentioning the future of FURIA, there's been a lot of rumors floating around, one of them to do with you and molodoy, and what's going to happen past this year with all this uncertainty going on around the team. How much has that affected the team in terms of the atmosphere? As you get closer to the date where FalleN's not going to be in the team, how much more difficult has that been to deal with?

We are kind of joking about it, because FalleN's such a big figure in the team, and FalleN retiring does leave a huge amount of space that somebody has to pick up, and it's impossible to replace him one for one, and you need to understand what direction you want to go into. And at some point, you're going to have to make a decision, what is that direction, who is that last player.

Hopefully we're going to stay in four and we're going to find a good player to replace FalleN

And of course, if things are not going to be working out, if there's no good options or whatever, it's completely normal for people to look for other perspectives and whatever, and I don't think anybody's going to be mad at anybody if something happens and the lineup doesn't go through.

But at the same time, we are working on it. We're trying to figure out what's our future, and hopefully we're going to stay in four and we're going to find a good player to replace FalleN. But at the same time, it's completely logical that everybody's looking at options and kind of scouting around. Though I saw news that 'Oh, YEKINDAR has many options.' I don't see the... somebody forgot to tell me, I guess, or something.

You landed on the side of the bracket where the top teams have not, but still MOUZ and FUT have looked pretty strong this season, reinvigorated with new lineups. What do you think is going to happen in that game, and is there any sort of preference or expectation of who you're going to face?

Well, I think right now MOUZ is a little bit better probably than FUT, and we would probably expect to play against them. But at the same time, I feel now they see us, it's more about momentum and who has a better day. I feel everybody in top 10 can beat everybody most of the time, depending on who's going to have a bad day, who's going to have a good day.

But we probably expect MOUZ. And the bracket is, well, as people are always saying, 'Oh, this is the easy bracket.' We do not control what's going to be the bracket. And we just play the game that's in front of us, and well, if the favorites fucked up, then that's the bracket, you know?
