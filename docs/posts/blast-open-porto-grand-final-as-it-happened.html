xertioN: "Everybody today had a moment where they checked out"

vs.

Speaking to BanKs: "Nuke came down to the fact that we weren't ready to play again, mentally. They had good ideas on CT to annoy us, and they were successful. I trolled that CT side, I'm super disappointed at how I played Nuke."

On the mental: "I think everybody today had a moment where they checked out. You saw xelex, but all of us had this moment, for me it was on Nuke, everything I did went wrong. There's a lot to review, Mirage was a good comeback, but we'll come back stronger next time."

zont1x talks about aiming for a "ropz-style" legacy

vs.

Speaking to the desk after the final, about MOUZ: "They just didn't connect to the server, in my opinion. [At Bounty] we had no energy, it was a terrible game by us.

On his career goals: "ropz style, I would say, to have a big impact but nobody talks about you unless you're winning everything for two years straight. With my game intelligence I am not able to be top1, top5, I might not even hit top 20, but I think I am doing my impact in other ways even if I am not the best mechanically. I want to win as much as I can."

On being a secondary caller: "Yes, I'm the second caller. I think this would be obvious to anyone who listens to a single TeamSpeak of our game. I've never called as much as I do now, I'm a strong second voice. The team functions differently to a year ago with chopper, before S0tF1k. Now everyone can implicate something in a game plan or inside the game, everyone can do whatever they want."
