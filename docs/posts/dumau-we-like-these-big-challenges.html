dumau: "We like these big challenges"

Legacy arrived in Copenhagen for the group stage of BLAST Open Porto fresh off a third-place finish at Esports World Cup, a campaign that saw them pick up fantastic victories over Natus Vincere, Falcons and FURIA, heralding the promise of a new era with Santino "⁠try⁠" Rigal among their number.

Unfortunately for the Brazilian side they were unable to build on that performance in Denmark, falling to Falcons and then Vitality to exit the tournament prior to the playoffs.

Vitality fend off Legacy to stay alive at BLAST

"I think the first map we let it slip away from our hands," Eduardo "⁠dumau⁠" Wolkmer hold HLTV when reflecting on his team's defeat at the hands of Vitality. "We had a good control of what was happening, how they're rotating and everything, I think it was good, but we lost some rounds that were 4v2. They deserve it, of course, too, they played well, ZywOo hit amazing shots. But we still got the opportunity to finish the game."

Despite the early tournament exit, dumau felt Legacy would only grow from the challenges they faced. "I think it's about overcoming challenges and being able to deal with pressure, deal with hard moments. I think this makes all the team, all the individuals, better as a man, you know? And I think it's very important."

What's your first reaction after that series?

I think the first map we let it slip away from our hands. We had a good control of what was happening, how they're rotating and everything, I think it was good, but we lost some rounds that were 4v2. They deserve it, of course, too, they played well, ZywOo hit amazing shots. But we still got the opportunity to finish the game.

I think as a team, as we are improving, it's a part of our process. We cannot rush it, but it's something we just have to look for, and we have to talk about these moments like, 'Oh, this is happening, how can I help you?' Or maybe how can we give each other more clarity.

Sometimes I think after these moments happen, we get more tilted than what we see, when actually we are getting rounds [where] we can see, okay, they are a bit lost here, a bit lost there, and we can use from these parts. Sometimes we are just tilted, oh, why didn't we peek or why didn't you call, these things. So I think we have to keep finding solutions in these moments to win it, and I think it's just a matter of closing the games now.

When [Vitality] get momentum back, it's hard, you know?

To be honest, this game, if we won Nuke, it would be 2-0, you know? Because in Mirage we had the control, we played very good as a team, and I think we got a very good opportunity in this game. Like in Nuke we started by winning a lot of rounds, and it was very controllable for us, and we felt that they were a bit tilted, so we got more momentum. But I think teams like Vitality, of course, they're the best. When they get momentum back, it's hard, you know?

Do you think it's something that just comes with experience? Obviously Vitality have a lot of experience in games against the best teams where they need to dig deep. Do you think maybe that will just come with time for you guys?

Yeah, because I think we got the momentum, but it's just a matter of closing now. We got momentum, we got experience because for me and latto or try, we are 22 or 21 years old, 23 years old, but we played a lot of events already, we played a lot of things. So when you play against good players, good teams, we kind of know what's going to happen, and we have this clarity. But sometimes it's just a matter of closing the rounds, like 4v2 or something like this, and it's hard because when you can't close it, you just give economy and momentum for the other team. Like it's easy to kill ZywOo with Five-SeveN sometimes, but with AWP it's much harder, you know?

If you don't have a big challenge, I think the trophy is not a big matter

You've obviously had a really good start to the season at Esports World Cup 2026. You beat some amazing teams. Because you had such a good start and the teams you lost to here are Falcons and Vitality, two of the best teams in the world, is it maybe easier to not get so tilted over this tournament?

Yeah of course, because I think all tournaments we are playing, we get a big challenge, you know? And I think this is important, to be honest, because to win big trophies, you have to win big teams, because if you don't have a big challenge, I think the trophy is not a big matter, you know? I think it's about overcoming challenges and being able to deal with pressure, deal with hard moments. I think this makes all the team, all the individuals better as a man, you know? And I think it's very important.

We played against Falcons yesterday. It was a tough match. I think the results lied a little bit because of economy, you know? Falcons played better yesterday, so they deserve it. Today we got a lot of momentum, we got a lot of pace in the rounds. We knew what we were doing, but again, this time was harder than yesterday, because today we played much better than yesterday. But today we lost when we had to close the rounds or close the game, maybe 11-4 or 11 something.

So yeah, I think it's a matter of time now and about we should maybe control ourselves a little bit better, and I think it will happen at some point. But we like these big challenges. I think it's important for us as a character, not only as players, because if you go to playoffs, win against these teams, it's much better, you know?
