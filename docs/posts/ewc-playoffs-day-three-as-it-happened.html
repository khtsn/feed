    "We've not looked our best," he added, "and they haven't played a flawless tournament either."

    Nikola "⁠NiKo⁠" Kovač spoke to Tres "⁠stunna⁠" Saranthus ahead of his team's quarter-final bout with Legacy, and he gave this thoughts on Falcons' run at EWC thus far.

    "We obviously wanted to do better than at BLAST Bounty, we had a very bad start to the season and we knew that we had to get it going here. EWC is quite a big event for us and our organiztion, so there was pressure all the time before we enter our match, but I think we have handled it pretty well during the game.

    "We have played some good CS so far and I would say that we were in control in the majority of our games so far. Hopefully it continues today as well."
