Spirit have not been able to beat Vitality since 2024, when Vitality were playing with Audric "⁠JACKZ⁠" Jug as a stand-in at the BLAST Premier World Final. They've played six times since, with only one of those being in 2026, at IEM Rio.

"I think stylistically we match up pretty well, and I think we prepare the game really well, as well," William "⁠mezii⁠" Merriman told Freya "⁠Freya⁠" Spiers in a pre-match interview on the broadcast. "donk is always good, but I think over time he hasn't been the usual donk against us."

He added that Vitality has had other teams' numbers in the past and you can never know, however, as every game brings with it something new and could change the dynamic between the two teams.

"We've not looked our best," he added, "and they haven't played a flawless tournament either."
