ODDIK pause esports operations

ODDIK have announced that they have suspended esports operations indefinitely and with immediate effect, which also brings an end to its Counter-Strike division.

The Brazilian organization entered CS in June 2022 and was a mainstay in South American domestic competitions, peaking at No. 28 in the HLTV rankings after an appearance at PGL Astana 2025, where they narrowly missed out on a playoff spot.

The announcement also states that Diogo "⁠diozera⁠" Oliveira Nunes and Gustavo "⁠nardes⁠" Henrique have been moved to the organization's bench, while the remaining members, namely Vitor "⁠Ceruttera⁠" Cerutti, Gabriel "⁠NEKIZ⁠" Schenato, Sebastian "⁠righi⁠" Righi, and Joaquin "⁠lokomotioN⁠" Abasolo, have been released as free agents.
