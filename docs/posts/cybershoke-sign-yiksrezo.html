CYBERSHOKE sign yiksrezo

CYBERSHOKE have announced the signing of Vladimir "⁠yiksrezo⁠" Mitrofanov, bringing the former GUN5 and Nemesis player onboard to replace Vladislav "⁠H4SAN4TOR⁠" Tuchin. The organization also announced that Demid "⁠dEMA⁠" Bukovsky would join as the team's coach for their upcoming events.

The CYBERSHOKE roster has been in flux since August, when Aleksandr "⁠glowiing⁠" Matsievich and David "⁠bl1x1⁠" Stepanyants were benched to make way for Emil "⁠nota⁠" Moskvitin and H4SAN4TOR. The latter did not last long, however, and was transferred to a new team after a handful HLTV-recorded matches.
