Inner Circle send meziiless Vitality to BLAST Open Porto lower bracket

cptkurtka023 topped the board with a 1.58 rating in the series

Inner Circle beat Vitality 2-0 in their BLAST Open Porto Group B opening match on Anubis (13-8) and Cache (16-13) to advance to the next round of the group stage through the upper bracket.

The series was Justinas "⁠jL⁠" Lekavicius's first match as a stand-in for Vitality in a stint that is set to extend beyond the BLAST event, where he is taking the place of William "⁠mezii⁠" Merriman, and into PGL Masters Bucharest, where he will substitute Dan "⁠apEX⁠" Madesclaire.

"It was our prime game," said player of the match Arseniy "⁠cptkurtka023⁠" Derevinskiy in his post-game interview on the BLAST broadcast, "we did everything very good."

Inner Circle kicked off on their Anubis map pick as a very confident side, dominating to a 7-1 lead on the attack before Vitality brought the deficit to two at the switch.

The Ukrainians didn't slow down in the second half, winning five of the first six rounds to hit map point, and converted on it just three rounds later to secure their pick, 13-8, with a 1.64-rated performance by cptkurtka023 leading the way.

The series turned to Vitality's Cache pick, where they hoped to even out the series, and it was smooth sailing in the first six rounds on the attack before Inner Circle won a six-round streak of their own to keep the score tied at the half.

Vitality retook the reins on the CT side, winning six of the first seven rounds to hit map point with an ample cushion to tie the series up, but they crumbled as Inner Circle chipped away at the lead one round at a time all the way to 12-12.

Two quick entries on the A site by Eduard "⁠zeRRoFIX⁠" Petrovskyi put Inner Circle in the lead immediately in extra rounds, and the Ukrainians never looked back, taking two of three in the first half before ending it cleanly on the defense, 16-13.

Mathieu "⁠ZywOo⁠" Herbaut, who had the slowest start to a match he's had on CS2, going 0-7 on Anubis, struggled throughout and posted his second-worst-rated series in CS2, landing at the bottom of the scoreboard with a 0.78.
