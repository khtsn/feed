paiN continue through EWC lower bracket after eliminating Wildcard

Having lost their opening EWC match to 9z, paiN met Wildcard in the Group D lower bracket. Both teams secured their map picks, but paiN proved superior on Mirage, turning an 11-1 lead after the CT side into a 13-7 victory.

paiN stormed out to a 6‑0 lead on Inferno to kick off the series before Wildcard staged a turnaround, surging to 10‑6. Unable to lock down their advantage, Wildcard were dragged into overtime after multiple back‑and‑forth rounds, but the NA team's CT‑side performance proved superior as they took their pick 16-12.

saffee turns 2v3 with three AWP kills
