Wildcard sign fr3nd on loan to replace mhL

Wildcard have announced the signing of Sebastian "⁠fr3nd⁠" Kuśmierz on loan from PURE to replace Miłosz "⁠mhL⁠" Knasiak, who has been moved to the bench.

It will be fr3nd's second stint with Wildcard after he played for the organization's academy team in the first half of 2025 before briefly getting called up to the main roster from July to September.

The Polish sniper has since plied his trade for KOLESIE, PURE, and stood in for Betclic, averaging a 1.05 rating in 2026 across 146 maps and notably leading the latter team to a title victory at Stake Pulse Beat I over Liquid.

He replaces mhL, who averaged a 1.13 rating since being promoted from Wildcard's academy roster at the start of the year. fr3nd's debut will come at iBUYPOWER Masters FML on September 25, where Wildcard will face NRG in their opening match.
