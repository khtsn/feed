rain on moving away from IGLing: "I think my personality is maybe a little bit too chill"

Håvard "⁠rain⁠" Nygaard joined 100 Thieves as the IGL of the organization's new European project, but has found himself out of the role after just over six months at the helm, with the team adding Jack "⁠Gizmy⁠" von Spreckelsen to their ranks in the offseason.

"I had a discussion with Lukas [gla1ve] midway through the season where he kind of asked me, like, 'Is this what you really want to do?' And I said I would think about it," rain told HLTV about the move away from the role.

"But then I'm like, 'I can go back to playing my own roles. I can do what I really enjoy doing.' So I'm very happy now, and I'm happy that I can add to the team in my old role."

He also touched on 100 Thieves's games in the open qualifier, "perfectionist" Gizmy, and his expectations for the EWC main event.

Congratulations on making it through into the main event here at the Esports World Cup. First of all, three back-to-back series, how are you guys feeling after all that?

I feel pretty good, actually. I think some energy drinks are fueling me right now. I might have a little crash later on, but it feels pretty good.

Especially the second game against BC.Game, I guess that would've been the one that got the closest. It was very close to you guys going out early.

I actually thought it was over on Nuke on that clutch that Jack [Gizmy] had. I thought it was one on four, and then suddenly I look up, it's, 'Oh, he's the only one alive.' 'Cause I already like, 'Oh, fuck. I'm going home.' So it feels really good, yeah.

I actually thought it was over on Nuke on that clutch that Jack [Gizmy] had

Does it fulfill expectations? When you saw that draw, obviously it's a lot of big value names, a lot of the people that maybe we thought might make it through to the main event. How difficult was it at the end of it?

It was pretty difficult. BC.Game put up a really hard fight, and then they smoked us on Inferno, and basically every map was close from then on. So it was very hard. I think the last game actually was the most comfortable one, so it was good.

How would you evaluate the whole qualifier from your side? You've already been at this level for a little while, so you know what the Open LANs are like and all that stuff. So this experience was maybe not so new to you, but what do you think about how the team handled this kind of barrage of matches?

No, I think we handled it very well. I think everybody gave everything. There was a lot of energy. Of course, it's understandable, in the end now, we ran out of energy. Everybody's been screaming. The voices get a bit like, 'Uh, uh, uh.' So overall, the pressure, we handled very well. And we listened well to Jack, and he did amazing calls, amazing clutches, so it was great.

How are you guys feeling going into the main event? Obviously a step up in competition, here was all the top 30-ish teams. From here, it's going to get tougher, and you're going to be one of the lower seeds, so obviously straight from the bat, you're probably going to be facing off against somebody up there.

Yeah, I prefer playing tier-one teams. That's what I've been playing my entire career now, so for me, you kind of know what's happening. The flow of the game is more familiar to me compared to these tier-two teams where sometimes it's just full random and chaos. So for me, I'm feeling pretty confident going in.

Do you think you guys are ready already? Obviously it's kind of still early days with Jack especially. How are you guys feeling in terms of how quick the development has been in terms of getting a new in-game leader in?

Well, we've been doing, like, three, four hours theory sessions every day. And it's been a lot of information to process, getting in Jack's system. He has a lot of things that he wants to implement, and we're basically playing his system now, and I think it suits all the players very well. So it's been a lot of work. I don't think we're at our top yet. We definitely aren't the peak of where we can be with this team, so it's going to take some time, but we have to wait and see the next few months.

We definitely aren't the peak of where we can be with this team, so it's going to take some time

For you, obviously, a change back to the kind of player that you used to be. Can you tell me about, let's say, what went wrong in your in-game leadership stint, or what you felt like you were lacking, to maybe be in this role a bit more permanently?

Yeah, I had a discussion with Lukas [gla1ve] midway through the season where he kind of asked me, like, 'Is this what you really want to do?' And I said I would think about it. But then the ball starts rolling, and I'm like, 'I can go back to playing my own roles. I can do what I really enjoy doing.' So I'm very happy now, and I'm happy that I can add to the team in my old role, so it's fine.

What do you think you were missing in that first season, being the captain?

I think it's just my personality maybe. They wanted a more fiery IGL. I'm very laid back and calm, so I'm not going to tell... If people do something wrong, I'm like, 'Ah, it's fine, bro. Don't worry about it.' So I think my personality is maybe a little bit too chill.

Jack will tell you if you fuck up a nade, that's for sure

How is Jack different from that?

Jack will tell you if you fuck up a nade, that's for sure. So he's very straightforward and very hungry to win and wants everything to be perfect, so he's a perfectionist, you could say.
