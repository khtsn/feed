Short news: Week 39

VCT Americas is proper back.

With a 2-0 showing, LOUD LOUD Brazil Rank #1 lukxo Lucca Travaioli DaviH David Cruz Darker Sebastián Castro Cicuamia erde Roberto Lobos tkzin Enzo Zimiani loan added another patch of green to its record, sending Global Esports Global Esports Asia-Pacific Rank #3 PatMen Patrick Mendoza UdoTan Go Kyung-won (고경원) Kr1stal Savva Fedorov xavi8k Xavier Juan Autumn Kale Dunne home to make it four Americas teams in the top eight.

The series was played out on GE's Split and LOUD's Sunset. LOUD won Split 13-8 and Sunset 13-9. On both maps, extended runs by the South American squad saw the team run away with the result. An eight-round streak to end Split and a seven-round streak to start Sunset were paramount to the team closing out the win on both maps.

In-game leader Roberto "erde" Lobos was the driving force in the series, finishing with series-high marks with 46 kills and a 1.56 rating.

The series began on GE's pick of Split, which was noteworthy on its own. LOUD had banned the map nine times through Americas Stage 2, but elected to ban Ascent instead. LOUD rolled out a comp similar to what it had run previously, switching out Astra for Omen and placing lukxo on Yoru. The teams had mirror comps on Split, with GE moving UdoTan moving off of Raze and onto Neon.

Early on, it looked like GE had things figured out. The team looked sharp on its attack side, with PatMen particularly hard to stop in that half. He paced all players in the half with 14 kills and just seven deaths, leading GE to a 7-5 halftime lead. Things seemed on schedule for GE, with the team claiming the second-half pistol to threaten a clean win of its map pick. However, an eco round lost to four Classics and one Bandit derailed any momentum that GE had.

erde puts four to the ground to tie things up.

That anti-eco round was the beginning of the end for GE. A smooth run of eight straight rounds, led by a combined 27 kills from erde and lukxo, helped LOUD transform what was a tight map into a runaway win, 13-8.

Attention then turned to LOUD's pick of Sunset. The South American squad owned a 5-1 record on the map in Stage 2, while GE had some well-documented struggles on the map. The team had a 1-3 record on the map in Pacific Stage 2, and seemingly had issues with identity. Against LOUD, the team fielded its third unique comp on the map, going for a Neon-Yoru-Chamber look. Meanwhile, LOUD went with a comp similar to what the global meta on the map has evened out to, with a Sage-Chamber pairing complemented by tkzin 's Neon as the lone Duelist.

Any momentum held at the end of Split was sustained heading into Sunset. The team got off to a red-hot start: a pair of erde triple kills, a lukxo 4K, a tkzin 4K, and an assortment of multi kills. Seven straight rounds were claimed to start the map, and it was clear that GE were even winning in the fine margins. Three 1v1 clutches were converted as part of a 9-3 attack side, putting GE in danger.

GE started its attack with its third pistol of the series, converting the anti-eco this time, but struggled to follow it up. Within four rounds, LOUD had reached match point, up 12-6. GE didn't go down without a fight, clawing back three straight rounds, but ran out of gas before it could even up the score.

Darker and erde pull off the duo ace to close out Sunset.

Darker, erde, and tkzin finished Sunset with 20 kills, mitigating a 23-kill map from PatMen to help LOUD claim one of the last two remaining playoff spots.
