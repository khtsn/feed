Short news: Week 39

FISSURE has announced that its October 2028 event has been moved from October 3-15 to October 16-22.

The announcement also included a change to its February 2028 event, but for that the organizer needed a Valve exception, as the 22-month Announcement Date deadline had already passed.

Upon inquiry, the TO said it did not receive the exception, and that only the October event would be moved.

HLTV's new VRS invite simulator has received a significant post-launch upgrade, including a strength model built from map results and rosters rather than raw VRS points, adjustments for teams signed up to multiple clashing events, and an upgrade from 500 simulations to 1000.

The most notable example of a team to see a shift is Natus Vincere, whose predicted chance of qualifying for the Singapore Major has increased to 91.2% on our simulator page.
