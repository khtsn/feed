Short: Nordic Partners withdraw from DraculaN

In a post on X, Nordic Partners announced that they will not be participating in DraculaN Season 7, stating that the team is "currently not at the level we want to be."

The decision comes after a 13-16th place at ROG JOURNEY Autumn, where they were soundly beaten by Eternal Fire, SINNERS and 100 Thieves in the group stage.

"We will figure out in the next days what are our next steps," Nordic Partners wrote. "We have been not were we want to be."

Short: FISSURE updates dates for 2028 event

FISSURE has announced that its October 2028 event has been moved from October 3-15 to October 16-22.

The announcement also included a change to its February 2028 event, but for that the organizer needed a Valve exception, as the 22-month Announcement Date deadline had already passed.

Upon inquiry, the TO said it did not receive the exception, and that only the October event would be moved.
