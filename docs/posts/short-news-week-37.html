Short news: Week 37

Nikola "⁠NiKo⁠" Kovač recently hopped on the LINKED podcast, touching on a range of topics from across his career.

One interesting revelation was that NiKo personally worked with performance coach Lars Robl after his team's loss to Legacy in the CAC grand final, and that he credits the cooperation with helping him to win his Major title.

NiKo also touched on why Damjan "⁠kyxsan⁠" Stoilkovski was replaced in Falcons, focusing on the differences between Finn "⁠karrigan⁠" Andersen and how kyxsan didn't take the right approach to dealing with feedback from his teammates.

The organizers of Esports World Cup 2027 have announced more details for the massive open qualifier.

The tournament, which will award four spots at the main event, is once again open up to 128 teams on a first-come, first-serve basis, with more information about the registration set to be announced "soon."
