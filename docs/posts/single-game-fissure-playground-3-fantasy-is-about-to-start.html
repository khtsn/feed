FISSURE Playground 3

Format

  • You can create your teams on the Fantasy page here.

  • Two eight-team double-elimination groups.

  • Six-team single-elimination playoffs.

  • Quarter-finals and semi-finals are BO3, grand final is BO5.

Teams

Things to note 

  • Teams advancing to upper finals get one round of padding. Teams winning the upper finals get seeded into the semi-finals, guaranteeing themselves one round of padding for skipping quarter-finals.

  • As the third place decider is not added to HLTV, it will not be included into the game.

  • BO5 grand final will deliver 25% rating points than regular BO3 games.

Player prices & pick stats

The Polish player had a considerable improvement in his rating in the past few months while his price is still affected by his previous underperformance, making him a hot pick at relatively low price.
