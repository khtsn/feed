BLAST Open Porto teams, format, schedule, prizes, talent, fantasy

BLAST Open Porto will feature 16 teams competing across Copenhagen and Porto from August 26-September 6 for a combined prize and club share pool of $1.1 million.

Teams and format

Twelve tournament spots were initially awarded through the Global VRS standings at the July 6 cutoff, while four more were secured their spots by winning the regional qualifiers.

The two eight-team groups will compete through double-elimination, with every match played as a best-of-three. The group winners will advance directly to the playoff semi-finals, while the second and third-placed teams will enter the quarter-finals. The quarter-finals and semi-finals will remain best-of-three, with a best-of-five series deciding the champion.

Schedule and opening matchups

The group stage will take place at BLAST's Copenhagen studio from August 26-31. Group A will open the tournament on August 26, followed by Group B one day later.
