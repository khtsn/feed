Legacy sweep G2 to lift FISSURE Playground 3 trophy

Legacy swept G2 in the FISSURE Playground 3 grand final in Suzhou, 3-0, and lifted their third title on Chinese soil after claiming the CAC 2025 and 2026 trophies.

The series kicked off on Legacy's Ancient pick, but that's where G2 looked strongest in the series despite trailing 2-5 early on the CT side, as they won five rounds in a row to take the lead at the switch.

G2 then extended their lead to three in the second pistol round, but couldn't run away as Legacy won the ensuing forcebuy and won five in a row to take a 10-8 lead.

Finally, the European squad hit back, but Legacy's hero on Ancient, Bruno "⁠latto⁠" Rebelatto, got a double in a 3v3 in the 20th round to keep his team ahead, 11-9, after which the Brazilians went on to win out the map to take the series lead.

Santino "⁠try⁠" Rigal then kicked off Mirage with a USP quad-kill, and a few rounds later an AWP triple on short as Legacy got off to a 5-0 start on the defense. Artem "⁠r1nkle⁠" Moroz, who was 1-11, then won a 1v2 in the final round before the half to make it 3-9.

As the map went on, try fell to the background and latto once again put himself at the fore, leading Legacy on the scoreboard. Nemanja "⁠huNter-⁠" Kovač had a moment in the 17th round, getting an ace as G2 tried to claw their way back into the map, but the Brazilians fought back going back-and-forth five rounds in a row to go from 11-8 to 13-10.

Having won the first two maps and going into their second pick, Dust2, the wind was in Legacy's sails, and they didn't let up, starting 5-0 on the attack and cruising through the half to an 8-4 lead.

latto finished atop the scoreboard yet again in Legacy's 13-4 victory, posting his best map of the series with a 2.25 rating, 131.6 ADR, and a +11.50% round swing.

The 23-year-old took a close MVP race with Vinicius "⁠n1ssim⁠" Pereira and ran away with it in the final, topping the scoreboard on all three maps and dominating the final with a 1.67 rating, +5.59% round swing, 102.9 ADR and 80.6% KAST. This makes him a three-time MVP in all of Legacy's wins in China.

On the other side, Guy "⁠NertZ⁠" Iluz was a bright light for G2 as he posted a 1.23 rating — and the highest ADR in the series, 107.7 — despite the team crumbling around him, while r1nkle struggled with the AWP as the lowest-rated player in the final with a 0.63 rating and just 26 kills across three maps.
