FaZe sign Neityu in permanent deal

11-8-2026 21:00

The Frenchman joins the team as a permanent fixture after a four-month stand-in stint.

FaZe have announced the signing of Ryan "⁠Neityu⁠" Aubry after his loan from ENCE was made permanent.

The French rifler joined the organization as a replacement for the outgoing Finn "⁠karrigan⁠" Andersen in April as a stand-in, and represented the team at five tournaments.

He helped FaZe claim four semi-final finishes at BLAST Rivals S1, Stake Ranked Episode 2, BLAST Bounty S2, and XSE Pro League Guangzhou 2026, playing his best event at the latter with a 1.05 average rating.

The Frenchman will play his first game as a fully-fledged FaZe member on Wednesday, as his team has been matched up against BetBoom in their Esports World Cup opener.
