Boo ends four year run with Team Heretics

Team Heretics Team Heretics Europe Rank #9 Wo0t Mert Alkan RieNs Enes Ecirli benjyfishy Benjamin David Fish has let Boo , their in-game leader since the start of partnership, explore options outside of the organization.

Boo joined Heretics starting in 2023 at the beginning of the partnership era. His first year leading the team was largely unsuccessful, but in 2024, Heretics became an EMEA powerhouse. They reached all three international events that year, and placed infamously placing second at two of them. At both Masters Shanghai and Champions Seoul, they fell short 2-3 in the grand final.

The roster stuck together in 2025 and continued to be a strong team in EMEA. They placed second in Stage 1, but had a disappointing 0-2 finish at Masters Toronto. They then finished outside of the top four of the Stage 2 playoffs, but still made it to Champions off of championships. At Champs, they had a resurgence in form, taking down a number of strong teams including G2 Esports G2 Esports North America Rank #4 jawgemo Alexander Mor BABYBAY Andrej Francisty trent Trent Cairns valyn Jacob Batio leaf Nathan Orf . In the end they finished in the top six, still chasing a VCT trophy.

In 2026, Boo would split with his brother MiniBoo, who left the team for new pursuits. Heretics initially had trouble filling his shoes, with their initial choice of ComeBack not panning out. However, once koshmaras entered the roster in Stage 1, they rediscovered their form, and went on to win their first domestic trophy. However, they went 0-2 in Masters London, and failed to make the playoffs in Stage 2. They were then eliminated by tier two teams in the play-ins, and their accrued Championship points were not enough to qualify.

With their season over, Heretics is preparing for the future, and the historic core is being dissolved.
