ZywOo claims uncontested StarSeries Fall MVP

ZywOo poses with his 33rd MVP medal

Mathieu "⁠ZywOo⁠" Herbaut has claimed the HLTV x 1xBET Most Valuable Player award at StarSeries Fall after a dominant run that left no doubt about who the tournament's best player was.

The Frenchman averaged a 1.50 rating at the event, his highest since a similarly unambiguous IEM Krakow MVP early in the year and a staggering 0.24 clear of the next best player (Kaike "⁠KSCERATO⁠" Cerato at 1.26).

With a 1.51-rated grand final performance against Aurora, ZywOo left his closest competition to the award in the dust in Jimi "⁠Jimpphat⁠" Salo, who was very much in the race leading up to the grand final but went missing in the best-of-five.

Off the back of that finish, Vitality's superstar dominates the tournament leaderboards with a 92.2 ADR (#1), 0.89 KPR (#1), 0.57 DPR (#1) and +6.13% Round Swing (#1).

The award extends ZywOo's all-time overall record to 33 medals, and the Big Event record to 26, just two away from doubling Oleksandr "⁠s1mple⁠" Kostyliev's tally.
