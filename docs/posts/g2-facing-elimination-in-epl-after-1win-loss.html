G2 facing elimination in EPL after 1win loss

G2 are staring down elimination at ESL Pro League 24 after a 1-2 loss to 1win, which drops them into the 0-2 pool.

It's a third consecutive loss for G2, following the 0-3 sweep by Legacy in the FISSURE Playground 3 final and the 0-2 hammering by Natus Vincere on Friday. They slip one place to eighth in the global VRS, while 1win climb to 33rd in the world after a five-place jump.

"They're definitely a good team, but from what I see, they still have a lot of struggles with their map pool," 1win captain Vladislav "⁠nafany⁠" Gorshkov said of G2 after the match.

"They have a good Ancient, but if we talk, let's say about Dust2, it's their great weakness. It's a matter of time before they fix it. For now, we were better, I believe."

It was a vintage Sergey "⁠Ax1Le⁠" Rykhtorov performance on Dust2, as he posted a 1.72 rating to power 1win to a close 13-11 victory, inflicting G2's sixth defeat on the map in their last seven games.

G2 responded with a 13-1 thrashing on Ancient but were to carry that momentum into the decider map of Mirage, where Andrey "⁠BELCHONOKK⁠" Yasinskiy produced a fantastic performance (2.09 rating, seven multi-kills, and a +11.83% round swing) as 1win ran out convincing 13-8 winners.

In his post-match interview, an emotional nafany gave a special shout-out to an HLTV user, Rancheros.

"He supports Ax1Le in every match," nafany said. "If you check HLTV, every match since TDK, he has posted the first comment in every match of ours and has been supporting us a lot.

"I want to say big thanks to such people because it's really important."
