Luminosity confirm kakafu signing

Luminosity have confirmed the signing of Alexander "⁠kakafu⁠" Szymanczyk as the team's head coach.

The Austrian tactician already stood behind the team at XSE Pro League Guangzhou and the Esports World Cup, and has now been officially announced as a member of the team.

kakafu was part of the Monte roster that attended the IEM Cologne Major before departing for Luminosity without their IGL Jack "⁠Gizmy⁠" von Spreckelsen, and now officially reunites with his former teammates.

The news comes shortly after Luminosity benched Aurimas "⁠Bymas⁠" Pipiras less than two months after signing the ex-Monte roster.
