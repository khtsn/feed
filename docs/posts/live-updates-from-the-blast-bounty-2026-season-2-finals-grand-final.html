Live updates from the BLAST Bounty 2026 Season 2 Finals grand final

Danil "⁠donk⁠" Kryshkovets is back in the limelight after posting a 1.55 rating in the online portion of the tournament and leading Spirit in Malta at the Bounty Finals with a 1.23 rating in his five maps played.

The LAN's stats leader, however, is MOUZ's Ádám "⁠torzsi⁠" Torzsás, who posted 1.38 and 1.29 ratings to help put 3DMAX and Astralis to the sword across the six maps, and holds a tournament-leading 1.32 rating.

The question now is whether the Hungarian AWPer will be able to continue playing at this standard being the underdog rather than the favorite, as MOUZ will go from facing teams outside the top 10 to a team in the top three.

The AWPer torzsi will be up against, Dmitry "⁠sh1ro⁠" Sokolov, has been no slouch in Malta, averaging a 1.17 rating, including topping the board against FaZe in the semis with a 1.24.
