Spirit snap six-match losing streak vs Vitality to reach EWC semis

Spirit are through to the semi-final stage at the Esports World Cup with a 2-0 victory against Vitality, ending a six-match losing streak that stretched back to October 2024.

Anubis and Nuke were closely fought, with both teams edging out their respective map picks by the slimmest of margins. The series then came down to Mirage, where Spirit ran away with the game, consigning Vitality to their third consecutive quarter-final loss on LAN.

Vitality fought back from an early deficit on Anubis with an eco win and used that momentum to string together some rounds. However, Spirit never let the game get out of hand and went into the break with a respectable six rounds thanks to some stellar fragging from Danil "⁠donk⁠" Kryshkovets, who boasted a 10.55% round swing.

Spirit took over the game on the T side and quickly got within touching distance of victory after a 5-0 run. Vitality refused to let up and kept the score close thanks to individual heroics from Mathieu "⁠ZywOo⁠" Herbaut and William "⁠mezii⁠" Merriman, but then Spirit closed the game out in the final round of regulation on the back of a triple kill from Andrey "⁠tN1R⁠" Tatarinovich.

Vitality came out swinging on the T side of Nuke and romped to a 7-1 lead before Spirit answered back with four decisive rounds — a streak so dominant they gave up only five kills.

ZywOo added to his team's lead with a clean 4K in the pistol round. Spirit answered with a streak of three rounds once they had money to work with and then fought back from four map points down to send the game to overtime.

Vitality did enough on the CT side to carve out a narrow lead and then put the game past Spirit in the second half following a 1v3 clutch from Shahar "⁠flameZ⁠" Shushan and a bit of a grenade blunder from Boris "⁠magixx⁠" Vorobiev in the last round that cost a low HP donk his life.

The Russian prodigy laughed it off and took over the game on Mirage, going 20-5 in the first half to power Spirit to a 9-3 lead. More misery was to come for Vitality in the second half as Spirit quickly reached series point and then finished the game off in a 3v5 situation.
