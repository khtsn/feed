It's the first time that Les Bleus can call themselves the kings of EMEA since Kickoff in 2024, which was the last time the org won a VCT event, albeit without a trophy.

It's one of the most remarkable mid-season turnarounds that VCT has ever seen, as KC went from 0-5 and 11th-12th in Stage 1 to now being crowned the Stage 2 champions. It was pure dominance for KC, who finished the group stage at the top of Group Omega with a 4-1 record before going a perfect 3-0 in the playoffs. Now, Karmine Corp will head to Champions Shanghai as EMEA's top seed.

KC made the most of its double-map ban advantage, taking Split and Abyss out of the equation. The maps could be seen as two of Liquid's stronger maps in the current pool, but also two maps KC has been hesitant to play in Stage 2. The series itself was played out on KC's pick of Sunset, TL's Ascent, and KC's Lotus. KC won Sunset 13-7, Ascent 13-6, Liquid won Lotus 13-7, and Summit 13-10.

On Sunset, KC went with its usual Omen-Viper comp with N4RRATE and LewN on their usual picks of Chamber and Neon, respectively. Meanwhile, Liquid was set to play the map for the first time since 2025. Immediately, KC looked much more comfortable on Sunset. KC worked the map wonderfully, piling up an early 6-1 lead which threatened a blowout. From there, though, Liquid seemingly seized the narrative, winning out the half to tie the score 6-6.
