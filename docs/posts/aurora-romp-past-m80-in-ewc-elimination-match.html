There were still moments like those that ash will likely want to review, but they were few and far between amidst a dominant series from Ismailcan "⁠XANTARES⁠" Dörtkardeş. The Turkish rifler was unstoppable from the outset, drumming up a 7-3 opening duels record on Dust2, 23 frags, seven multi-kills, and a 1.67 rating.

That, combined with a 5-0 opening duel record from Özgür "⁠woxic⁠" Eker and a 4-1 record from Jimpphat, ensured Aurora eventually closed out their pick from an 11-5 lead despite a 1v3 AWP clutch from Elias "⁠s1n⁠" Stein helping close the gap in the second half.

The German in-game leader then struggled immensely on Inferno, where XANTARES again ran rampant with eight multi-kills, a 24-12 K-D, and 1.68 rating as Aurora eased to an 8-4 lead at the break. A pistol win extended that lead, and a 1v2 by woxic set his team on match point before another last-ditch recovery effort by M80 was put to bed.
