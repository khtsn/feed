LOUD silences EDward Gaming with sweep in Shanghai

The kings of Sao Paulo aren't slowing down, not even against the crowd buff. LOUD LOUD Brazil Rank #1 lukxo Lucca Travaioli DaviH David Cruz Darker Sebastián Castro Cicuamia erde Roberto Lobos tkzin Enzo Zimiani loan defeated EDward Gaming EDward Gaming China Rank #4 ZmjjKK Zheng Yongkang (郑永康) nobody Wang Senxu (王森旭) CHICHOO Wan Shunzhi (万顺治) Smoggy Zhang Zhao (张钊) Jieni7 Zhang Juntai (张君泰) stew Park Young-chan (박영찬) 2-0, sweeping the third seed from China and continuing the home region's struggles at Champions Shanghai.

EDG picked Lotus to start the series, with LOUD following up with Summit. Sunset was the deciding map if the series went to map three.

Lotus began with an ideal start for EDG, taking the pistol to pump up the active crowd in Shanghai. Losing the bonus still kept the team ahead, but one player from LOUD turned up to shut down EDG. The Neon from tkzin earned not one but two aces, clutching multiple rounds on defense and ending the first half with a 22/5/1 stat line. LOUD entered the half up 9-3.

ZmjjKK defended well on Chamber with a comfortable pistol win, which CHICHOO followed up on the antieco. EDG losing the bonus round put LOUD up 10-5 and pulled a Chinese team to its best map result yet at Shanghai, but LOUD closed Lotus out 13-8.

CHICHOO gets caught in his ult, LOUD win Lotus.

The most surprising thing for Summit was the composition from EDG to try and counter the Sage and Chamber from LOUD. New pickup stew moved to Breach, ZmjjKK hopped on Waylay, and nobody picked Tejo. LOUD took the first pistol off two nice shots from tkzin, but ZmjjKK entered, nailed the entry kill, and stopped the bonus conversion. The Breach started to gain value when EDG had the rifles, as the map reached a 4-4 tie.

While EDG's A site hits never fought through the spam from LOUD, the B site hits looked much better. The half ended with a third tkzin ace, and LOUD up 8-4. LOUD earned the second pistol to jump ahead to double digits, and even converted the bonus to fully silence the Shanghai crowd. The Breach went from alright on the attack to pointless on the defense side, as LOUD secured Summit 13-5 and the series 2-0.

Champions Shanghai will continue tomorrow with Group A action, with the return Group C and Group D games on Tuesday.
