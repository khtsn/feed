M80 stun NAVI at BLAST Open

NAVI's early-season woes continue as M80 secure their biggest scalp since March.

JBa helped M80 secure a flawless Inferno T side with a 1v3 clutch

M80 have opened BLAST Open Porto with a bang, securing an upset victory over Natus Vincere in a 2-1 series.

The team around Elias "⁠s1n⁠" Stein turned it around from a disastrous Anubis opener, where Natus Vincere controlled the tempo with a dominant T side, picking up convincing wins on Mirage and Inferno.

Fritz "⁠slaxz-⁠" Dietrich starred for his team on the two map wins. M80 got eight on the Mirage defense in large part thanks to the German AWPer, before he pulled off a crucial quad-kill at the close of the defensive half of Inferno.

Then, Josh "⁠JBa⁠" Barutt helped round out the perfect Terrorist side with a 1v3 clutch for match point:

It is a massive scalp for the North American side, who entered the match as heavy underdogs after slipping out of the top 30 off the back of two early exits at BLAST Bounty and Esports World Cup to start the second season of 2026.

In fact, it marks M80's first win over a top-10 side since March 31, when they beat Aurora in Digital Crusade DraculaN Season 6.

On the flipside, Natus Vincere's woes at the start of the season continue after they went out in 9th-16th place at Esports World Cup off the back of a landslide loss to Legacy. They now need three wins in a row in the Group A lower bracket to make the comeback run to playoffs.
