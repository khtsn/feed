Stake Pulse Beat II reaches playoffs

A pool of 16 teams at Stake Pulse Beat II has been whittled down to eight after the conclusion of a GSL group stage that was topped by Astralis, HEROIC, 100 Thieves, and JiJieHao.

Sangal, BBL, fnatic, and Betclic secured the final quarter-final spots as their group runner-ups, while Virtus.pro and FOKUS were among the names to falter and miss out.

A 1.67-rated series from Gytis "⁠ryu⁠" Glušauskas helped Astralis recover from an opening overtime map loss to Sangal before Victor "⁠Staehr⁠" Staehr took over to sweep FOKUS and make playoffs. Sangal then recovered from a map down and a 4-8 deficit to edge past FOKUS in the Group A decider.

HEROIC romped through Group B with ease, while Virtus.pro's recent charge up the VRS and morale took a hit after crushing 0-13 and 5-13 losses to BBL. The Danes then dominated Johnny Speeds in the decider match to advance, while Group C saw 100 Thieves beat EAC and Phantom, the latter of whom lost in a rematch to fnatic in the decider.
