onic on beating Vitality: "Today we were mentally much, much stronger"

Inner Circle have been in good form in recent months, winning Super DraculaN prior to the player break, and they have taken that to the next level by opening their campaign at BLAST Open Porto with a 2-0 upset victory over Vitality.

"It's just incredible," Viktor "⁠onic⁠" Babii said in the wake of his team's win. "Of course everybody knows they're better than us, and it's normal, but today we were just mentally much, much stronger. I'm just proud of my guys."

So first, congratulations. They might have had a stand-in, but I'm sure it still means a lot to beat Vitality.

Yeah. Thank you, thank you so much.

What are your feelings after that game? What are your first thoughts?

Oh, bro, this... I dunno, it's just incredible, because of course everybody knows they're better than us, and it's normal, but today we were just mentally much, much stronger. I'm just proud of my guys. As I said to my guys before the games, it's CS and maybe they will have a bad day, maybe we will have a good day, and anyone can win. We showed it, that it's really possible, and we did it. I dunno, the emotions are crazy.

How did you manage to come back on that Cache? Because you were 7-12 down at one point, and I think a lot of teams would've just said, "Oh, it's fine. We'll try on Dust2."

To be honest, it was a kind of funny situation, because I really forgot that we used all our tactical [pauses]. I typed 'tactical' in chat twice in the last second, trying to take a tac. I guess it worked out for us because they would wait for a slow round, seeing we had no time to take a tac, and we didn't realize we didn't have one. But we were already running, freeze time ended, and I called a really fast round. They were not expecting it.

When we did this round and we won, we all felt this energy that we really can easily come back. Because even in our last tournament that we won, we were really mentally strong in this situation. We kind of like to do comebacks, so we were believing it a lot. So yeah, we just believed, and yeah, we did.

What kind of confidence does this give you for the tournament going forward now? You'll play either 9z or MOUZ in the next round.

To be honest, I don't want to change something in our mentality. I just want my boys to appreciate the chance to be here because it's a really good chance, but we also don't need to be afraid to lose or something like this. We just need to appreciate and gain as much experience as we can because this is the most important for us right now, because we are a young team. I'm a young IGL, we are all young, it's the first tier one [event] with such big names for us. So I'm just proud we're here, and I want the guys to appreciate the moment and just try to do our best every game, and no matter we lose or win, I just want to fight 'til the end.

With this roster we can be top 10 easy

Like you say, this is a really good chance for you, not just to get some experience, but also for your VRS. One more win here is a massive boost to your VRS. Do you think about that at all?

Of course it's very important, the VRS, because we already took like 80 points from this win, and also from the prize pool we will get some, so it's doing our chances for the Major way, way better. It's good.

We don't want to have kind of big expectations from this tournament because as I said, it's the first tournament with, like, top 12 teams. We just want to again show our best to gain this experience. And yeah, even if we lose the next games, like, 0-2, 0-2, I guess it's already good experience, because to beat Vitality on LAN is very good.

It feels like your team is hitting some pretty good form in the last couple of months. Like you say, you won that Super DraculaN, you've got like an 80% win rate or something over the last three months. How excited are you for the future of this team? Because like you say, you're a young team.

Yeah. We are young, and we are kind of not a full new roster, but I think we still don't feel each other 100%, so we can have more time and we will play even better, I would say. I believe in this team so much, nobody thinks about changes or something like this. We just know that if we still keep doing what we do right now, because we are really trying a lot, we are all hoping that we can be like top 10 VRS, stable. We just need to be more disciplined as tier one players. So yeah, we just need kind of time to get this discipline and experience, and with this roster we can be top 10 easy, I guess.
