Spinx earns career-first MVP at BLAST Bounty Season 2 Finals

Lotan "⁠Spinx⁠" Giladi has earned his first-ever HLTV x 1XBET Most Valuable Player (MVP) award after leading MOUZ to the trophy at BLAST Bounty.

The Israeli lurker was crucial in the final against Spirit with 78 kills over four maps, three Player of the Map awards, and a 1.54 rating.

That allowed him to overtake Ádám "⁠torzsi⁠" Torzsás — who led the way against 3DMAX and Astralis — after the AWPer ended the final on a 1.05 rating.

The two were hard to separate overall, with a 0.02 rating gap on LAN (1.22 torzsi, 1.20 Spinx) and in map wins (1.29, 1.27), which meant the medal had to go towards the player who contributed more deeper into the tournament.

Reminder

Online matches do not count towards MVPs or EVPs from 2026 onwards

Despite being a four-time Top 20 player and 17-time EVP, Spinx had never won an HLTV MVP having been a teammate to Mathieu "⁠ZywOo⁠" Herbaut and then part of a team effort when MOUZ won PGL Cluj-Napoca 2025.

He also had a fairly quiet start 2026, with no EVPs since IEM Krakow and a 1.01 rating in the last three months of the season.

But with the arrival of Oldřich "⁠PR⁠" Nový and a new season, Spinx has managed to reach a height he had never before seen.
