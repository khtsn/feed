Live updates from EWC semi-finals

FUT have some very obvious strengths in their map pool, namely Ancient, Anubis and Mirage. They pick Ancient and Anubis almost exclusively, and at least one will get through, so expect them to take the series there.

FURIA meanwhile have a little more depth to their pool, having added Ancient into the mix recently, and they looked incredibly proficient on that map against G2. Considering their recent difficulties on Anubis, it seems likely they will let Ancient through.

Dust2 seems a good pick for FURIA, as FUT are likely to ban Inferno first rotation, so the interesting question in this series is what the decider will be. They could handshake a Mirage, or FURIA could try to test FUT by banning Mirage out second rotation, likely sending the series to Cache. FURIA have shown a willingness to take a gamble in the veto, too.

The veto seems tough for Legacy in their bout with Spirit. Legacy's best map will be banned out by Spirit, although at least Legacy perma-ban a solid option for Spirit in Anubis.

Legacy could go for a risk and pick Cache, as Spirit have looked a little shaky on it, because beyond that there doesn't seem to be any great options for Legacy.

You'd think Spirit will pick Ancient, a map they will be favoured on, and unless Legacy risk Cache, Spirit will probably be happy with whatever the other two maps are.
