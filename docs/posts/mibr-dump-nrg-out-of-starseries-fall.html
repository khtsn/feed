MIBR dump NRG out of StarSeries Fall

MIBR have eliminated NRG from StarLadder StarSeries Fall after defeating Nick "⁠nitr0⁠" Cannella's troops with a 2-0 scoreline (Cache 13-7, Inferno 13-2).

Linus "⁠LNZ⁠" Holtäng and company thus continue their lower bracket run, with their next test coming against the winner of FURIA vs. MOUZ later on Saturday.

For NRG, this is a disappointing end to a tournament that began with an upset win against MOUZ. The North American squad could have secured 70 VRS points with the victory, which would have propelled them a further eight spots up the rankings, but still leave Barcelona with a 130-point haul.

The series began on NRG's pick of Cache, where the Håkon "⁠hallzerk⁠" Fjærli and company put together a solid 7-5 T-sided half despite losing the pistol.

Their CT side left much to be desired, however, as MIBR won eight straight rounds to secure a comfortable victory thanks to LNZ topping the board with a 1.70 rating.

Inferno was a similar affair, with NRG's first CT-sided round coming at 1-9. That gave them far too much ground to make up on their offense, as they petered out in a 2-13 loss to suffer a disappointing elimination.
