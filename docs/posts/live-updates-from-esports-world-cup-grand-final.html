MVP race update

13:55

Needless to say, a lot would have to go wrong for Danil "⁠donk⁠" Kryshkovets not to come out with the MVP at the end of the grand final today, win or lose.

As usual, he's been the tip of Spirit's spearhead, racking up a 1.55 event rating, only rivaled by Ilya "⁠m0NESY⁠" Osipov, and a staggering 1.60 in the playoffs.

Way behind him in the race are Dmitry "⁠sh1ro⁠" Sokolov, who has been his usual consistent self but has not hit anywhere near the same peaks, and FUT's Yasin "⁠xfl0ud⁠" Koç, Dmytro "⁠dem0n⁠" Myroshnychenko and Nikita "⁠cmtry⁠" Samolotov, who have shared the load in the playoffs so far.
