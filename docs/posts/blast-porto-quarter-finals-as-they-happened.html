molodoy on facing Vitality with jL standing in: "I cannot say if it's easier or harder"

vs.

Danil "⁠molodoy⁠" Golubenko answered some questions about the series against Vitality in the first round of the playoffs at the Super Bock Arena.

You have Vitality coming up, how are you feeling about that game?

They are playing with jL but they are still playing good. They beat good teams, they beat FUT who are very hard for us to play against. But yeah, they are playing very good still, it's good.

Do you think them having jL as a stand-in helps you, because maybe they are not as good as with mezii, or is it harder because they are going to play a little bit differently?

I cannot say if it's easier or harder, but jL has a Major. He's a good player and they have good teamplay, you see how they are screaming, the emotions they have when they are winning rounds. I think this helps them a lot. I'll say jL is a good player, and they have good communication, good emotions with the team. Who knows who is going to win; we will see.

Playing in the arena in Porto, we would expect a lot of the fans are going to be behind FURIA. You seem to be a player that steps up and plays really well in these arenas with the crowd. Do you feel like that?

Yeah, I think for me, I really like to play with crowd because I can do some emotions, I can do some things and with the crowd… I'm very happy when I play with the crowd. When you play with a crowd every time, it's hard to play without a crowd, I think it's always like this. Some players understand me.
