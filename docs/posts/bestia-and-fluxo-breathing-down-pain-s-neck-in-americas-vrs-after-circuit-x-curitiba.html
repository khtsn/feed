BESTIA and Fluxo breathing down paiN's neck in Americas VRS after Circuit X Curitiba

BESTIA won Circuit X Curitiba over Bounty Hunters

BESTIA and Fluxo are now joint-tenth in the Americas VRS following the conclusion of Circuit X Curitiba on Sunday, just 14 Valve points from the qualification zone for the Singapore Major.

Playing their first LAN event since adding André "⁠drop⁠" Abreu and Kaue "⁠kauez⁠" Kaschuk, BESTIA lost just one match — a 0-2 defeat to Galorys in the group stage — en route to the title. They picked up 204 points from their winning run, with drop named the MVP of the tournament by Dust2 Brasil.
