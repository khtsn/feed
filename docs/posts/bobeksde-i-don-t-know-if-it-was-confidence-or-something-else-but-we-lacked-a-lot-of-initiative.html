bobeksde: "I don't know if it was confidence or something else, but we lacked a lot of initiative"

Alliance were eliminated by MIBR in the first match of the FISSURE Playground 3 quarter-finals, a rather one-sided affair that went the way of the South American squad in two maps.

MIBR maul Alliance to reach FPG 3 semis

The team's newest addition, Jonatan "⁠bobeksde⁠" Persson, says they didn't feel ready to play, as the more experienced MIBR dominated the series and made Alliance look overwhelmed on the arena stage in Suzhou.

The Swedes can be happy to have made it out of the group stage, however, amassing some valuable VRS points after victories over FaZe, TYLOO and magic that set up their bid in the race to the Singapore Major.

What do you think you lacked in the match against MIBR?

I don't know if it was confidence or something else, but we lacked a lot of initiative, and I think it felt like we were just not ready to play.

You have already been in the semi-finals at a similar event. Did that put your sort of expectations higher of yourself here?

I think our goal is the Major. So we came here hoping for a win, and then everything else was a bonus pretty much. I think overall it was a good event, but obviously it sucks now.

It has brought you up in the VRS rankings. You are now 15th, quite high in the predictions as well. Do you feel like you’re in a good position to fight your way to the Major?

Definitely, I think we have a good shot. I think obviously it's our goal, but I don't think we should be focusing on it too much. I think we should take each tournament as seriously as that one. But yeah, I think we have a good shot at it.

You have a few smaller LAN events coming up. Do you feel like you have a good chance of actually winning one of them? Do you have the confidence for it?

Definitely, I think everything we've gotten from experience and stuff like that in both our China events, I think we've come pretty far, and I think we should have a pretty good shot at winning at least one of them.

So you've joined this team quite recently. What are your impressions of it?

I think it's a very good team with stability and a good supporting team around it. I think we get some good help from the outside as well, not only in CS. But overall, I think in-game as well, we have a really good AWPer, a really good IGL, really creative, and we're just a strong rifling core.

Speaking of your supporting staff, it's very unusual that, as you have a coach, during the games it's your performance coach or general manager standing behind you. What is the reason for this sort of arrangement?

In general, Hawk is doing some more analytical stuff, and he's been with us during the matches just because... It's just how it is.

If Pontus is not as much on the analytical side, what is his role during the games?

He mainly calms us down during the matches, and in the pauses, maybe doesn't say much about the game. Obviously, he knows some stuff about what we do and what happens, but I think it's mainly to calm us down during the game and make us focus on the now, so to speak.
