Astralis, HEROIC, 100 Thieves among teams confirmed at Stake Pulse Beat II

Stake has announced the 14 teams confirmed via open registration to attend Pulse Beat II, scheduled to take place from September 21-26 in Stockholm, Sweden, with a $60,000 prize pool.

The 14 highest-ranked teams, based on the August 3 VRS update, were selected from the registration process, with 100 Thieves, HEROIC, Astralis, fnatic, Virtus.pro, and EYEBALLERS among the list of notable names.

Two final spots at the event remain up for grabs through single-elimination open qualifiers scheduled for August 20 and August 21, which will see best-of-ones utilized until the best-of-three grand final.

Winners will receive a tournament package — which cost registered teams $3,000 — free of charge, "including accommodation, practice facilities, meals, local transportation, and full player access throughout the event."

The main event will see teams divided into four, best-of-three GSL groups, with the top two from each advancing to a single-elimination playoff bracket. Valuable VRS points will be up for grabs for teams looking to climb the standings ahead of the November 2 cut-off for PGL Major Singapore.
