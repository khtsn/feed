5star round out FPG 3 team list with Asia Closed Qualifier victory

The Mongolian squad will travel to China to play the LAN in Suzhou.

5star beat Chinggis Warriors 2-0 in the FISSURE Playground 3 Asia Closed Qualifier grand final to punch their ticket to the $1,000,000 event in Suzhou, China.

The Mongolians started out their run in the closed qualifier the same way they ended it, with a 2-0 victory in the opening round over Chinggis Warriors. They then knocked NEXVOID and Rare Atom to the lower bracket before doubling down against their countrymen from the opening round in the grand final.

Mongolian fans will have much to watch out for at the tournament in Suzhou, as 5star are the second squad from the Land of Blue Sky to reach the event. The MongolZ were invited via VRS and recently announced that Anarbileg "⁠cobrazera⁠" Uuganbayar will be taking the place of Sodbayar "⁠Techno⁠" Munkhbold in the starting five.
