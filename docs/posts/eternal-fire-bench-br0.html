Eternal Fire bench br0

Eternal Fire has announced that Alexander "⁠br0⁠" Bro has been moved to the bench. The Dane had joined the team on July 5 from NRG, where he had been benched in June after elimination from the IEM Cologne Major's first stage.

Official: Eternal Fire complete roster with br0

br0 played only eight matches since joining Eternal Fire, starting with a European Pro League Series 8 run, which the team won. They then finished in 9th-16th place at NODWIN Clutch Series 10, and had an unsuccessful bid at the Esports World Cup 2026 Open Qualifier, which ended with a defeat at the hands of BIG.

br0, who had near-average ratings during his time in Monte (1.02) and Astralis (1.01), and an above-average rating during his 18 months with NRG (1.13), did not click in his short stint with EF, posting only a 0.93 in 21 maps before leaving the active lineup.
