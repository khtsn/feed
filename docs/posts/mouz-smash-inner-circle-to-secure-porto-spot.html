MOUZ smash Inner Circle to secure Porto spot

MOUZ have only lost five rounds per map on average at BLAST Open so far

MOUZ have secured the third spot in the BLAST Open Porto playoffs taking place next week at the Super Bock Arena after making light work of Inner Circle.

The team around Dorian "⁠xertioN⁠" Berman still has a match to play in the Group B upper bracket final against the winner of Legacy vs. Falcons for a chance to earn a direct berth in the semi-finals.

Meanwhile, the Ukrainian side falls to the lower bracket, where they will face off against the winner of FUT vs. Lynn Vision.

Arseniy "⁠cptkurtka023⁠" Derevinskiy's team looked far removed from the one that stunned Vitality with a 2-0 win on the second day of the tournament, facing a MOUZ side that was firing on all cylinders.

They struggled to break through a stalwart MOUZ defense on Ancient led by Lotan "⁠Spinx⁠" Giladi, only winning the opening pistol round and a 1v2 clutch from Eduard "⁠zeRRoFIX⁠" Petrovskyi.

And Inner Circle weren't much more successful on their own defensive side of Mirage as MOUZ ran over them without letting them string rounds together all map long in a solid team performance.
