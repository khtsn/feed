FUT eliminate LVG despite Starry's best efforts

Lynn Vision face the familiar feeling of a last-place exit from a Big Event after a 2-1 defeat at the hands of FUT.

A June victory over Ninjas in Pyjamas remains Lynn Vision's only win at a notable event against international opposition in a best-of-three this year, though heads can be held high from a good effort in Copenhagen.

The Chinese side showed promise when they took a map off Falcons, though they ultimately failed to convert a 9-2 start on the decider. They were able to win a map here, too, after a 13-10 win on Dust2 that showed off the firepower of Lizhi "⁠Starry⁠" Ye and Sike "⁠z4KR⁠" Zhang.

But on Anubis, the gap between LVG and FUT was stark as the Europeans backed up their top-ten credentials with a dominant performance.

Ancient began in a similar vain before Starry inspired a fightback, but once the going got tough FUT flexed their muscles while LVG misplayed 2v2 retakes and struggled to keep up tactically.

Down 7-11, LVG were 14-4 up in opening duels but repeeks and coming out worse in trade battles meant only four of those 5v4s became 5v3s. That illustrated the difference in discipline, and ability of the likes of Dmytro "⁠dem0n⁠" Myroshnychenko to match Starry mechanically and claw back rounds for his side.

FUT will now take on Inner Circle to decide which team makes it to the group's lower final.
