Esports World Cup 2027 and 2028 dates revealed

The organizers of the Esports World Cup have announced more details about the Counter-Strike 2 portion of the 2027 and 2028 editions of the Saudi Arabian festival.

The 2027 event, set to be played from July 20-August 1, will keep this year's expanded format, with the main part of the competition still featuring 32 teams and a $2 million prizepool.

The gargantuan open qualifier also remains on the calendar and is set to be played from July 16-18 at the Esports Boulevard in Riyadh, Saudi Arabia, and award four spots at the main event.

The organizers have also announced the slot distribution for the event, which looks as follows:

  • 20 Global VRS invites

  • Two North American VRS invites

  • Two South American VRS invites

  • Two Asian VRS invites

  • Two Wildcard invites

  • Four teams from the Riyadh Open Qualifier

The 2028 edition's details will be announced at a later date, with the tournament currently "tentatively scheduled" to take place from July 14-30.
