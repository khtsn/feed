Live updates from the race for PGL Major Singapore

The first tier two event of the Major run-in is Stake Pulse Beat III, which features a hefty $80,000 prizepool and a raft of Major-chasing squads in attendance.

Envy, Ninjas in Pyjamas, Inner Circle, HEROIC, JiJieHao, 100 Thieves, fnatic, BIG, and HOTU are the teams in attendance who could see their Major hopes significantly bolstered by deep runs and important victories.

Astralis, MIBR, Liquid and BC.Game are also in attendance, but all have their Major spots either clinched or virtually certain. Rather than fighting for qualification themselves, they could offer a boost to any team that managed to beat them.

Some of the scenarios facing teams are:

  • Ninjas in Pyjamas - First place takes their qualification chances up to 97%, while last place leaves them at 48%.

  • Inner Circle - First place takes their qualification chances up to 99%, while last place leaves them at 26%.

  • HEROIC - First place would virtually guarantee their qualification, while last place leaves them at 71%.

  • Envy - First place takes their qualification chances up to 77%, while last place leaves them at 16%.
