Media: jL to stand in for Vitality

Vitality are set to be without their full roster for two separate events.

Justinas "⁠jL⁠" Lekavicius is set to serve as a stand-in for Vitality at BLAST Open Porto and PGL Masters Bucharest, according to a post to X by Sebastien "⁠KRL⁠" Perez.

The Lithuanian rifler would reportedly replace William "⁠mezii⁠" Merriman for BLAST Open Porto at the end of August and then Dan "⁠apEX⁠" Madesclaire at PGL Masters Bucharest in October, as both players will be away at those times for the birth of their children.

The news comes amidst a period of tough results for Vitality who, after winning five events on the bounce earlier this year, have now gone three events in a row without a top-four finish.

Vitality exited their most recent event, BLAST Bounty Season 2, at the hands of Liquid without even making the LAN finals.

jL has been without a team since departing Natus Vincere in July, having stepped down from the starting roster a year earlier to take a break from competition. His most-recent tier-one matches came on loan at MOUZ, with whom he achieved third-place finishes at PGL Astana and CS Asia Championships earlier this year.

Should the new come to pass, Vitality would field the following rosters at BLAST Open Porto and PGL Masters Bucharest:

BLAST Open Porto:

Dan "⁠apEX⁠" Madesclaire

PGL Masters Bucharest:

William "⁠mezii⁠" Merriman
