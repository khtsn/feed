donk earns Esports World Cup MVP

The Russian phenom is back on top after posting a 1.50 rating.

Danil "⁠donk⁠" Kryshkovets has won his second HLTV x 1XBET MVP of 2026 by dominating proceedings at the Esports World Cup.

There was little doubt about where this particular MVP was heading after donk posted a 1.50 rating, 1.32 KPRW, and no maps below the 1.00 mark.

donk was player of the map eight times, with a playoff rating of 1.50 and a 1.46 in the crucial quarter-final against Vitality.

It's donk's 12th career medal, and ninth at Big Events which draws him level with Nikola "⁠NiKo⁠" Kovač in fourth place on the all-time leaderboard.

After poor elimination series at IEM Cologne and in BLAST Bounty's grand final, it was a perfect way to end any doubt over his individual starpower as donk posted a 1.87 rating on map four of the grand final.

donk has never finished outside of the top two in the year-end top 20, and his medal here at the $2,000,000 event will help him as he battles against now usual rivals Mathieu "⁠ZywOo⁠" Herbaut and Ilya "⁠m0NESY⁠" Osipov to maintain that record.
