s1n: "Honestly I'm really proud of the team"

M80 have enjoyed a promising start to their campaign at BLAST Open Porto, taking an upset win over Natus Vincere and running FURIA all the way to OT on Cache, before eventually losing the latter series in 0-2 fashion.

"I feel proud of the way we performed today," Elias "⁠s1n⁠" Stein said of his team's performance on Cache. "We had a good T side, and a good start on CT, but we lost some crucial rounds. I think it was 5-3, after we had 12 already or 11, something like this, and after that we kind of fell apart in a sense. We definitely should have closed this game."

With a positive start to the event behind their backs, s1n is hoping that his M80 squad can push forwards in the lower bracket and "get revenge" on Aurora for their earlier defeat at EWC. "With some pistols under our belt and new-won confidence, I think we can be chin up into this game, just go very proud into this game and beat them."

So first, commiserations, obviously not the result you wanted. Talk me through that series from your point of view.

Our map pick, Cache, I feel it was such a fun game. We had some great rounds and honestly I'm really proud of the team. It was our first real Cache official also, and I feel proud of the way we performed today. We had a good T side, and a good start on CT, but we lost some crucial rounds. I think it was 5-3, after we had 12 already, or 11, something like this, and after that we kind of fell apart in a sense. We definitely should have closed this game, and I think this was kind of the round to pinpoint. We lost a huge advantage, and mentally we can learn from what happened after this: re-watch the voice comms, re-listen to them, and just see what went wrong. Honestly, I'm really proud of how we played the first map especially.

Going into Dust2 was kind of a deja vu moment. I think we've had similar games versus FURIA where they have so much chaos, and I think also YEKINDAR is kind of taking charge in the spots, just creating this chaos even more, and yeah, we couldn't handle it today. But nonetheless, I'm especially proud of the Cache game from our team.

I wanted to ask about that Cache pick, because you played it a lot in the Esports Nations Cup qualifiers with Germany, and BIG are playing a lot of Cache. Is it the German connection there? Germans just like Cache?

I think yeah, as German players, I remember playing [Cache] in ESEA Main lots a long time ago. I've always been a fan of Cache. I'm just happy that it's back and now we finally pulled it out one time in an official. Let's see how many more Cache games we can get in our bag.

Obviously a disappointing result today, but beating NAVI was a massive, massive win for you guys. Tell me a little bit about how the team was feeling after that one.

Yeah, the NAVI win was huge. We haven't won a game in a long time, especially a meaningful game. After yesterday we felt that maybe an era is starting [laughs]. Now, of course we gained a ton of confidence, and I think it just shows us again and again that we have what it takes to be a tier one team. Just in these crucial moments we gotta step up more, and I think today was a good example.

Tier one players, like FURIA, they are the best players in the world, they have them on their team, and they will find a way to win if shit's not working. So I think this is where we as individuals, and also me myself of course, we gotta take charge. I think if we learn from that and improve ourselves, we have definitely what it takes, macro-wise.

We can get revenge on Aurora

You must feel you're taking steps in the right direction, for sure, especially running FURIA so close today and beating NAVI. It's gotta feel you're getting there slowly.

Yeah, honestly I felt that way since before Esports World Cup, because we had a really meaningful bootcamp and we also brought in support for our coaching staff in Munstur, who was doing a really good job with dephh together. So yeah, I'm just happy how it is right now. Looking forward we have another game coming up on here. We can get revenge on Aurora, who I think we're playing.

Yeah, it's Aurora. So how are you feeling about the Aurora game?

Yeah, we played them at EWC. Honestly we played I think 13-9, 13-9, in Paris, and a little bit disappointed how that game went from our terms. We didn't perform on the first day at that tournament. But yeah, especially with some pistols under our belt and new-won confidence, I think we can be chin up into this game and just go very proud into this game and beat them.
