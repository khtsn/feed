A few familiar faces are set to return to Berlin as four proven Challengers representatives secured tickets to the EMEA Stage 2 Play-Ins. Beginning August 6, they will battle against partnered VCT teams in hopes of advancing all the way to Champions Shanghai.

Unlike the other three Challengers teams at Play-Ins, Eintracht Frankfurt didn't qualify through Stage 3. The German organization won 12 straight matches across three forms of play in the EMEA Last Chance Qualifier to snatch the final ticket to Berlin.

Prior to its run in the LCQ, SGE cemented itself in the upper echelons of DACH Challengers by never finishing below the top four. Despite this, SGE only ever finished among the top 12 at Stage 1 of EMEA Challengers.

With only enough points to qualify to LCQ Play-Ins, SGE tore through competition without a loss and advanced to the Group Stage.
