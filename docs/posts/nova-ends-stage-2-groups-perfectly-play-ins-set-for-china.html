China, like all other regions, has two double-elimination tournaments heading toward Champions 2026. Play-ins include two tier-two Chinese teams competing against the lower regional seeds while the top seeds wait in the playoff tournament.

The final seeding matches at the end of week three fully seeded the entire league, determining each of the opening matchups.
