FOKUS bench kuben

FOKUS have announced that head coach Jakub "⁠kuben⁠" Gurczyński has been placed on the bench, citing the decision to "move forward with a new direction for the team."

The Polish coach had been in charge of FOKUS since February, when the German organization entered Counter-Strike with a newly built international lineup captained by Erik "⁠ztr⁠" Gustafsson.

Currently ranked 55th in the global VRS, FOKUS have had a mixed start to the season following the summer departures of Georgi "⁠Jorko⁠" Mitev and Sebastian "⁠volt⁠" Maloș and the arrivals of Paavo "⁠podi⁠" Heiskanen and Jacob "⁠jocab⁠" Nerheden. They notably finished 9th-16th in BLAST Bounty, 17th-32nd in the EWC open qualifier, and 9th-12th at Stake Pulse Beat II.

ztr's team has been invited to the playoff stage of NODWIN Clutch Series 12 and signed up for ROG Journey Autumn, a $20,000 event in Skövde, Sweden.

With kuben's benching, FOKUS are now:

Matias "⁠Banjo⁠" Kivistö
