The Lithuanian has taken aim at his former organization over late and unfulfilled payments.

Aurimas "⁠Bymas⁠" Pipiras has taken to X to accuse his former organization, Monte, of unfulfilled payment obligations and a history of late payments.

The Lithuanian, who left Monte after a year-long service in early July to join Luminosity, claims he is owed "tens of thousands of dollars" from missed salary, prize money and bonus payments, in addition to a share of the team sticker from the still-ongoing IEM Cologne Major sale.

"Multiple former players and the coach are also still owed substantial amounts, to my knowledge," he added.

The X post came just minutes after Monte released a YouTube video in which its CEO, Dmytro Vovk, called out Bymas, Aurélien "⁠afro⁠" Drapier and Oscar "⁠AZUWU⁠" Bell and their agents for the manner in which the trio left the organization to join Luminosity.

The Ukrainian implied the players kept the organization and teammate Jack "⁠Gizmy⁠" von Spreckelsen in the dark about having signed with Luminosity prior to the IEM Cologne Major, while negotiations over contract extensions with Monte were ongoing.

The video made no mention of unfulfilled payments or other obligations to the players, but the organization replied to Bymas' post, saying: "Good text. You can tell it was approved by the agent. A two-week delay in salary payments is, of course, incredibly critical and painful."

HLTV has reached out to Monte for a comment.

In his post, Bymas also spoke about his motivations behind leaving Monte, noting that payment delays were "a recurring issue" that remained unchanged, which he says led to a breakdown of trust.

"Re-signing would have meant committing to an organization we no longer trusted to consistently meet its financial obligations, with no guarantee that the same roster would even stay together long term," the 23-year-old said.
