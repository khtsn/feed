sidde: "We can't only base the tournaments we attend on our schedule, or on what's convenient"

FURIA enter FISSURE Playground 3 as the heavy favorite to win the trophy and retain their title from FPG 2, one that would be their first of 2026.

All is not rosey, however. Gabriel "⁠FalleN⁠" Toledo's impending retirement hangs over every result and every interview, while Danil "⁠molodoy⁠" Golubenko's eye issues and a hectic schedule of back-to-back events are making it even more difficult.

This event is their third of four in a row, with StarSeries Fall next on the docket. A journey that started for the Esports World Cup halfway through August will not end until September 21.

But Sid "⁠sidde⁠" Macedo insists the team know what they are doing. "We have many reasons to be playing here," he tells HLTV in China.

"I know it's not only me deciding the schedule, so the organization also has their reasons for us to attend events. We know even though it was a difficult location, in other areas it was important for us to be here."

A tough game against GamerLegion even though they had a stand-in. How did it happen that you were close to letting the game end on map two?

We had a slow start on both maps, Mirage and Cache. We lost crucial rounds that made the T half a lot [more] difficult. We lost two anti-ecos, which is something that has been haunting us for some time now, on Mirage especially. After that, they took control of the economy, and we were kind of behind them in terms of game history, and also with how many spare rounds we had to play. So that was really hard after those two rounds. We got a bit steamrolled by them, and we had to come back CT side.

We have a good feeling towards this tournament because we won it last time

What was the role of your hectic travel schedule with you only recently returning from BLAST? Can you tell me that and also more specifics: when did you fly in, how much time did you have for your rest?

We left Portugal on Sunday, very early in the morning, 6:00 AM. We got here the next day at 7:00 AM. We traveled for 17 hours or something like that. We arrived at the hotel at 12, and then we rested for the day. At the end of the afternoon we did some media things, and we only focused on resting, and some of us did prep.

Today we woke up ready for the game. We got lucky that we had the last game of the day because with this schedule, we could rest a bit more throughout the day because we're still a bit jet lagged. We also had people with medical problems as a result, of course, of the difficult traveling that we had. But actually it wasn't that bad. We've done it in the past, arriving to a very different place in terms of time zone and playing on the same day. That was very bad. This one was okay, it was manageable.

So would you not say that it really affected you in this game?

No, I think it did. I think it affected us, especially in how we started Cache. We did not have the perfect conditions to play if you consider the travel time and everything, because it's a seven-hour difference from Europe, and you only have one day to adapt. It's not enough. But yeah, we needed to warm up a bit. We were very sleepy in the beginning of the game, of course, due to how tired we felt, but after we got some warm-up, we felt better to play.

You are the only established big name team that attends this event, and you are the favorite as well. What was different in your calculation in terms of choosing to attend or skip this event?

We have many reasons to be playing here. I know it's not only me deciding the schedule, so the organization also has their reasons for us to attend events. We know even though it was a difficult location, in other areas it was important for us to be here. We cannot only base the tournaments we attend on our schedule, or on what's sometimes convenient in terms of having an easy semester or easy travel days. Sometimes we have to do some efforts like we're doing here. So yeah, I cannot [reveal the reason] here, but yeah, there are many reasons for us to be playing.

Could it be that one of the reasons your team would like to play this event is that you would want more opportunities to lift trophies with FalleN?

I wouldn't say that. Of course, we have a good feeling towards this tournament because we won it last time, and we look at events that we won as an opportunity for us to repeat what we did. This can also maybe propel us into the higher flights in the semester. But it's not that we want to accumulate trophies or anything.

Tomorrow you're facing PARIVISION for a spot in playoffs. Do you expect that opponent to be more challenging and a tougher game for you in general, all things considered?

Yeah, of course, if you consider that GamerLegion had to play with a stand-in. Unfortunately, Snax couldn't be here. That goes against them a lot. It's a bit difficult to play in that situation.

So we're going to be playing against a team that's going to be on full force, and they have been resting for some time and preparing a lot of things, and I know how much they work, and they also had this new addition with FL1T. And he has good chemistry with Jame and dastan, and it's going to be interesting to play against them. It's been a while since we haven't faced them. Last time I think was in PGL Cluj, earlier this year or something like that. And we're going to do the best we can, and it's going to be a great match, huh?

Do you think you'll have enough time to recharge for that game?

Hopefully. I think they're going to give us the last game of the day as well, so with that, we can rest a bit, we can prepare, and yeah, we'll be ready.
