BC.Game CEO asks for fresh start: "Take all our points away"

BC.Game was affected by the recent change in the CS Major Supplemental Rulebook

Following a Valve adjustment to the CS Major Supplemental Rulebook affecting regional assignement after roster changes, BC.Game's CEO, Ali "Ako" Muhanned, has made a statement asking for his team to be given a chance to start in the Asia VRS from scratch.

"We understand why Valve introduced stricter regional rules. The integrity of the system needs to be protected, and we respect that," the missive starts. "But it's difficult for us to accept that our situation is being treated the same as every other case.

"Our current team has three Asian players (asap, mzinho and Senzu), three players who have competed at the highest level of Asian Counter-Strike. This is our core. These are the players we believe in."

Muhanned then goes on to ask for the team's VRS points to be set back to zero in order to let the team try and qualify based on their merits with the new roster.

"No previous ranking. No inherited points. No advantage," the letter continues." Only results earned by these five players from that moment forward should count. This is exactly how we would be treated if a completely new organization entered Counter-Strike with these five players today. We're not asking to be treated better than anyone else. We're asking not to be treated worse."

The CEO then explains that the team doesn't want to "remove another player simply to find a technical solution," once again pleading to let the players earn their way. "We're willing to give away everything we have earned," Muhanned ends. "Just give our five players the chance to earn it again. Start us from zero. Let us play. Let the server decide. 0 points. 5 players. 3 weeks."
