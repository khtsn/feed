magixx: "I told S0tF1k there's a reason I'm dropping HEs for outside players on Nuke"

With a win over Vitality in the Esports World Cup quarter-finals, Spirit have broken a long streak of losses to their nemesis after nearly two years and six matches.

The team easily brushed off an overtime loss on Nuke, where they fell just short of a comeback after Boris "⁠magixx⁠" Vorobiev killed off a low-HP Danil "⁠donk⁠" Kryshkovets with an unfortunate HE grenade, sweeping the floor with Vitality on the deciding Mirage.

When asked about what was different today, magixx opined Spirit took advantage of the pressure that was on Vitality.

"I feel like the pressure got to them because I've read some interview from apEX where he was talking about the Paris Major; it was hard for him to play in front of a home crowd because it's extra pressure, and I feel like that helped us win," he said.

I feel like the pressure got to Vitality

He brushed off the unfortunate ending to Nuke with a joke: "I just went back to our practice room and I told S0tF1k that, 'Did you see the reason why I'm dropping my HEs on Nuke for outside players?' So yeah, it was just a weird thing. But it didn't affect me in any way."

Spirit are facing Legacy for the very first time next in the semi-final, a matchup magixx clearly respects after seeing the Brazilians pull off several upsets since the Austin Major.

"We have seen them ending Vitality's win streak in Austin, we have seen them dominating Falcons today and in CAC. We have seen a game against MOUZ where they just owned them," he began.

"If you're not really prepared and you are not ready, and you're not ready to expect some things that they are doing, they might catch you easily."

You can read the interview in full below:

I wanted to ask you about the victory today and snapping a long streak of losses to Vitality a couple of years back, six games I think it was. What was different about today?

I feel like the pressure kind of got to them because I've read some interview from apEX where he was talking about Paris Major. There was something like it was hard for him to play in front of a home crowd, because it's extra pressure, and I feel like this was something that kind of helped us to win.

Also besides that, they are not that dominant as they were a few months ago. We managed to play better than we had in group stage. So we had a three-day break. My only wish for that three days was just to step up our game.

Can you tell me what you guys were working on more specifically in that break? Because donk said that you guys knew what the issues were, but you needed a few days at least to fix them. So what were those issues?

Just play better.

That's all it takes?

Usually, yeah.

I wanted to ask you about the end of Nuke. Do you think the HE impacted the match, and how easy was it to reset after that?

I'm not sure if it really impacted the match because I don't remember the setup we had against what they were doing. They were doing a nice split and I don't remember which positions we had at that moment, so I'm unsure. But if you're killing donk, it's weird. Even at 5 HP he can get a double kill, maybe triple kill.

Was there a conversation after that?

No, not really. I just went back to our practice room and I told S0tF1k that, 'Did you see the reason why I'm dropping my HEs on Nuke for outside players?' So yeah, it was just a weird thing. But it didn't affect me in any way.

Can you tell me about Legacy? You guys haven't played them before, and they have surprised so far at this tournament. What do you think about them as an opponent for tomorrow?

We have seen a lot of upsets from Legacy in the last year. We have seen them ending Vitality's win streak in Austin, we have seen them dominating Falcons today and in CAC. We have seen some, I think it was in Austin as well, a game against MOUZ where they just owned them, or it happened somewhere else.

But I feel like if you're not really prepared and you are not ready, and you're not ready to expect some things that they are doing, they might catch you easily because we have seen a lot of games like this from them.
