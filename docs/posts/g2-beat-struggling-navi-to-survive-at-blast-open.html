G2 beat struggling NAVI to survive at BLAST Open

G2 made light work of Natus Vincere to eliminate Aleksi "⁠Aleksib⁠" Virolainen's troops from BLAST Open Porto with a 2-0 victory (Ancient 13-6, Inferno 13-10).

G2 now move to the lower bracket final, where they will face the winner of Aurora vs. M80 for a playoff spot.

Match

The match started on Ancient, where G2 quickly took control after a back-and-forth start. They secured a 9-3 defensive half and won the second-half pistol, and while Natus Vincere prolonged the inevitable with a force-buy win, Nikita "⁠HeavyGod⁠" Martynenko's 1vs3 clutch netted them six map points and eventually secured a 13-6 victory.

Inferno looked to be even more one-sided, as G2 ran away to a 10-4 lead after a dominant T side. Their CT side wasn't as dominant, however, and Nemanja "⁠huNter-⁠" Kovač's troops had issues closing out the game.

Natus Vincere brought it back all the way to 10-11, but G2 rallied late on to secure a 13-10 victory and stay alive at BLAST Open Porto.

Aleksib: "Just not good enough"

The result continues a bad period of results for Natus Vincere, who have now suffered their second consecutive group-stage exit.

"Just not good enough," Aleksib began his broadcast post-match interview. "I could have done better on Ancient as well in certain situations, the way we were discussing outside and what we were typing on teamchat, maybe other individuals felt the same way.

"It's kinda obvious that we are lacking something in all of us. It's pretty rough because we really grinded for six weeks, and it feels so bad to go out like this in two tournaments."

Natus Vincere now have just over two weeks to get their ducks in order before their next outing at StarLadder StarSeries Fall 2026 set to begin on September 17. "It's up to us to dig ourselves out of this," the Finn said about the future. "We need to clear our minds and do something fresh."
