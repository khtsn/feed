The MongolZ sweep Wildcard to make Bounty finals

The MongolZ are the latest team to book their ticket to Malta and the BLAST Bounty LAN finals, doing so via a comfortable 2-0 win over Wildcard.

The Mongolian side enjoyed a powerful performance by their young star Zolbayar "⁠tikuak⁠" Chimedtseren to secure them the opening map, before a collective effort got the job done on Inferno to send them through to the LAN portion of the opening event of their season.

Dust2 saw The MongolZ race out to a 6-1 lead off the back of a force-buy win on CT side, tikuak leading the way with some impressive A holds.

Disaster teamkill by 910 grants mhL the 1v3 clutch

Wildcard managed to answer back late in the half and keep the map close, before winning and converting their CT pistol to tie up the score, but once The MongolZ got guns up they romped to victory with relative ease, with tikuak continuing to shine.

Wildcard put up a better fight on Inferno in a back-and-forth game, both teams finding wins with sub-optimal buys as the map ran close all the way to 9-9, but The MongolZ sharpened up in the closing stages with strong crossfires and sealed the series, booking their ticket to the LAN finals in the process.
