hAdji announces his retirement as player

Ali "⁠hAdji⁠" Haïnouss has announced his retirement from professional play, bringing an end to a career that stretched back to 2015.

"After years of dedication and chasing dreams, I've decided to retire as a player," he wrote on social media. He also made it clear that he intends to remain in the game and is open to opportunities on a team's staff.

hAdji first rose to prominence on Envy's academy roster before earning promotion to the senior team in February 2018. He went on to represent organizations including 3DMAX, LDLC, Falcons and Monte.
