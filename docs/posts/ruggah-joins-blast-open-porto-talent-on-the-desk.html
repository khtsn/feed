ruggah joins BLAST Open Porto talent on the desk

BLAST has announced the talent for BLAST Open Porto, which kicks off on August 26 with a group stage in Copenhagen before the field of 16 is culled to the six who will travel to Porto to play in front of the fans at the Super Bock Arena.

BLAST regulars will bring the action to life on the broadcast, but this time former Astralis coach Casper "⁠ruggah⁠" Due will join the analysis desk alongside Jacob "⁠Pimp⁠" Winneche, Alex "⁠Mauisnake⁠" Ellenberg, and Donald "⁠voo⁠" Parkhurst.

Live commentary from the server will be delivered by BLAST stalwarts Conner "⁠Scrawny⁠" Girvan and Mohan "⁠launders⁠" Govindasamy, as well as veterans Anders "⁠Anders⁠" Blume and Henry "⁠HenryG⁠" Greer.

Natalie "NatTea" Mahoney will be on hosting and interviewing duties with James "⁠BanKs⁠" Banks, while Mike "⁠DarfMike⁠" Winnick will be hosting in the arena with Alexandre "Archarom" Maia.
