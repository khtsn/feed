monSi has been in the Valorant scene since the beginning, competing as a player since 2020. He played for teams including Built By Gamers Academy Built By Gamers Academy Inactive BlackHeart Hasan Hammad monSi Ludovic Lefebvre khanartist Nabil Khan FireBallOps Cameron Tomaselli , Blinn Blinn Inactive , and most notably Shopify Rebellion Black Shopify Rebellion Black North America Rank #6 zander Alexander Dituri satellite exa Kim Lee-hwan (김이환) Smoke NaturE Nicholas Garrison . As an IGL for most of his career, he led his teams to a variety of wins in lower tournaments outside VCT, with his biggest accomplishment being his win with SRB in Stage 1 of NA Challengers in 2025. Now, he'll take his experience to the coaching booth for the first time in his career as he takes the reins of SRG.

SRG, who have been dominant in Game Changers since 2023 with back to back Championship trophies, have fallen from their pedestal this year. With long time leader meL stepping down from competitive play, SRG have become beatable in NA for the first time in a while. They took losses to SwimTrek Blue SwimTrek Blue North America Rank #49 eli venture k0rupt Wylie Watts Bob Bob Tran CHAROD Sean Zaletel in the grand final of Stage 1 and in the Swiss stage of Stage 2. They still comfortably reached the playoffs, but are no longer the overwhelming favorites that they once were.

SRG's Stage 2 playoff run will begin on Sep 1, as they fight for another appearance at the Game Changers Championship.
