xenn: "We can improve a lot of the things we did wrong here"

BIG are one of the teams that could be in the thick of what is soon going to become a gruesome race for VRS points as qualification for the PGL Major in Singapore starts to loom, although they now sit 10th in Europe with a 100 point cushion above the cut.

So far this summer they played at XSE Pro League, finishing in 5-8th place, and then qualified through the cutthroat EWC play-in, where after an opening best-of-one victory at the main event they lost to G2 and Spirit.

Now, BIG had another good opportunity to show improvement in China, but fell short in their opener against BETBOOM and, once again, against G2, exiting the tournament in last place.

The team's coach, Sebastian "⁠xenn⁠" Hoch, took some time after the match against G2 to share his views on his team's run at FPG 3 and what's to come for BIG.

1-2 against G2, a close game in the end. What did you lack to win?

In general, our CT sides were not good today. T sides were kind of fine, especially Cache was really good. Also, Ancient, last map, the comeback was good. But somehow all our CT sides were not on point. Our communication was not good enough. We had the good reads, but we couldn't get it over the line. We knew exactly what they were going to do, we had a lot of rounds where we had three guys towards B. We stacked the perfect site, but somehow we couldn't win the round. For some reasons we need to rewatch the rounds and find a solution for that. I think the T sides were fine, CT side was the main problem today.

Last place elimination for your team. How disappointing is that considering that it appears to have been your last opportunity to get some VRS points at a big LAN event this season?

Yeah, it's rough, to be fair. We wanted to go kind of deep in the tournament. Obviously, we faced BETBOOM, which is a really good team. We faced G2, also a really good team. Could have gone either way. Also against BETBOOM, on the first map we had our chances; second map, they were clearly the better team. And us, against G2 right now... Yeah, it is what it is. We cannot change it.

We have a lot of tournaments coming up like Stake Ranked and so on. We need to perform better there. We need to fix our things, find solutions for the problems we had here, and then keep going and keep fighting for the Major like we did earlier this year.

What is your plan for the remainder of the season? Specifically, what events do you think will be important for you?

From what I know, we only play the Stake Ranked tournaments, which are coming up. I think it's two or three tournaments in that case, and that's our main chances for the Major, I would say. We can still gain a lot of points. I think our position is still good, even though a lot of teams are behind us and they still have LAN wins open. But we can still qualify for the Major. We just need to perform better, get the wins in and then, hopefully qualify for the Major. That's the main goal.

What will your team be working on in the run-up to the Stake event?

Mainly we have to focus on our CT sides now, especially from this tournament. We have to rewatch a lot of things. We had a lot of weird situations, weird comms. We never had it before, even not in practice especially. And then we need to work on that, find solutions. Overall, the T sides were good, we can keep going and need to work on the CT sides.

On one hand, you have this disappointing loss here. On the other, you got through the cutthroat EWC qualifier. So what do you think of your team's performance this season so far?

To be fair, it's kind of okay-ish how we started into this season. The EWC qualifier was quite good for us. Obviously we faced G2 and Spirit [at the main event]. Also could have maybe beat Spirit in that case, but in the end of the day, it was not enough. And we still faced really good opponents, which was also not the best for us.

I think we had, after the player break, six or seven practice days, which was kind of not the best to get into the season, even though we still qualified from the open qualifier. But in the end it was not enough. We need to keep working. Now we have a few days off, then we have two weeks of practice and we can improve a lot of the things we did wrong here or bad even in EWC, and then we just keep going.
