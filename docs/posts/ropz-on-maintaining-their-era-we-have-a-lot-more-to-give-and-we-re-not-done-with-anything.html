ropz on maintaining their era: "We have a lot more to give and we're not done with anything"

Robin "⁠ropz⁠" Kool has had a quiet few months by his standards, but earned an EVP at StarSeries Fall and has averaged a 1.39 rating in ESL Pro League's group stage.

It's a frightening prospect for any opponent of Vitality in Katowice, with four of their players at or above a 1.29 rating after three matches.

For so long, ropz and company were the dominant team, and as they wait for Dan "⁠apEX⁠" Madesclaire to head home for the birth of his child they know that they must make every tournament count.

Astralis' era ended as much off the server as on it, with skipped events and personal burnout taking its toll. It's something Vitality are mightily aware of.

"You could draw some lines, [to Astralis]," ropz says. "It's maybe similar in this case, but I think the thing is we still feel like, as individuals at least, we have a lot more to give and we're not done with anything.

"We said so many times, we're lucky to have this five, and we will do anything to be good again."
