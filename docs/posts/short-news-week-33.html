Short news: Week 33

Have you ever wondered if NiKo regrets leaving G2? Or how long apEX will keep playing? If yes, then this fun piece of content from EWC has got you covered.

Aleksandr "⁠shalfey⁠" Marenov has announced he is a free agent and is open to new opportunities, primarily as an in-game leader while remaining open to other options.

The 24-year-old has been a journeyman for over a year, since he was benched alongside the rest of 9 Pandas after the organization reportedly lost a critical sponsor. shalfey has remained active despite sitting on the sidelines — playing in several mixed teams and standing in for other rosters — and has averaged a 0.97 rating over 54 maps in the last three months.

BESTIA have announced the benching of Ignacio "⁠nacho⁠" Rodriguez due to a "redefinition of roles."

The news ends the 22-year-old's six-month stint on the team, after a slow period of form which has seen BESTIA miss out on qualification for BLAST Open Porto, StarLadder StarSeries Fall, and slide outside the top 50 of the VRS.

Registration for Urban Riga Open 7, an eight-team event that will take place on LAN in the Latvian capital on September 29-30 with $5,500 in prizes, will open on August 24.
