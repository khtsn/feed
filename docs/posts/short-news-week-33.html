Short: Austin partners with FRAG for $30K LAN

Austin "⁠Austin⁠" Meadows has announced he will host "Austin LAN" in Naperville, a suburb near Chicago, Illinois, from November 13-15.

The tournament will be run with the help of Fragadelphia and boasts a $30,000 prize pool, with registration opening on August 27 and offered on a first-come, first-served basis.

Registration for the 32-team LAN will cost $2,250 and includes event entry for five players, two hotel rooms with queen beds, and three nights of buffet dinner in the same venue the event will be held in.

Short: shalfey enters free agency

Aleksandr "⁠shalfey⁠" Marenov has announced he is a free agent and is open to new opportunities, primarily as an in-game leader while remaining open to other options.

The 24-year-old has been a journeyman for over a year, since he was benched alongside the rest of 9 Pandas after the organization reportedly lost a critical sponsor. shalfey has remained active despite sitting on the sidelines — playing in several mixed teams and standing in for other rosters — and has averaged a 0.97 rating over 54 maps in the last three months.
