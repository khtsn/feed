Eternal Fire among teams to make IEM Beijing closed qualifier

The European open qualifier for IEM Beijing came to an end with Eternal Fire, Color, Nemiga, and Nuclear TigeRES securing their spots at the closed portion of the event.

The likes of BC.Game, 1win, and OG already exited the qualifier on day one, which left 100 Thieves as the highest-ranked squad still in contention.

Jack "⁠Gizmy⁠" von Spreckelsen's troops were eliminated at the final hurdle by Eternal Fire, however, who were playing their first tournament with new signing Caner "⁠soulfly⁠" Kesici.

Elsewhere in the bracket, Color, Nemiga, and Nuclear TigeRES progressed thanks to respective wins against EAC, Acend, and CYBERSHOKE.
