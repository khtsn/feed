On the ground at the Esports World Cup LCQ

Most of the games have gone back live now.

The games have been paused as the organizers are resolving a TeamSpeak issue.

All 32 teams are playing simultaneously in the first round and they need to win three series back-to-back to qualify for the main event. A true test of resilience today.

You can see how the playoffs draft was done in our video of the whole process below. First, the winning teams of each group were pulled from a bowl one by one to fill the bracket from top to bottom, and then they were paired with the second-placed teams.

The only rule was that teams from the same group could not meet each other in the first round, but that did not happen in the draft anyway.
