"We played local tournaments in Skopje, Tetovo, in Kosovo, Albania, and stuff, but with this team we've been together for one month or two months, and we're practicing a lot and just enjoying the game," USAMA said.

Gizmy posts third-highest LAN rating (3.47) as 100 Thieves ravage SadFamous

vs.

100 Thieves cruised through their opener against SadFamous in Paris thanks to a masterclass series from Jack "⁠Gizmy⁠" von Spreckelsen, who posted the third-best rating of his career on Nuke (2.27) before outdoing himself on Dust2, ending the map with a 28-4 K-D, 184.7 ADR, +19.18% Round Swing, and 3.47 rating.

That leaves him with the third-highest LAN rating of all time, behind Nemanja "⁠nEMANHA⁠" Đukić (3.57 at A1 Adria S14 Finals) and Adam "⁠Grizz⁠" Golden (3.66 at Fragadelphia Kansas City).

What is it like for a 2,000 FACEIT elo player to face professionals?

vs.

We asked that question of fellow journalist Mathis "⁠Poesys⁠" Popoff, who just played against K27 with Dark Tigre and ended the 27-round match with a 3-26 score:

"Rough. I just checked out the stats, I went 3-26, so that was rough. I played A on Mirage and they go out dry and I cannot do anything because they insta one-tapped me, so... rough."

"But yeah, very fun event. My second time playing against a pro team. Back in 2023 I did the open qualifier for the Paris Major, and we got stomped 16-0 by AVANGAR who were 200 at the time. So yeah, it's fun. Great event so far, and I like K27 as well, so it's funny to meet them that way. I hope to get more kills tomorrow, though."

Magisk: "This might be the difference between a good and a bad season"

Emil "⁠Magisk⁠" Reif after qualifying for the LCQ playoffs: "I get flashbacks to playing Copenhagen Games, bring your own computer, you know? It's really weird being back at this level. But honestly, it's insane that we have so many teams here with full setups, all playing at the same time. I don't think this has ever been done before at this scale, and I think it's amazing.

"Even though it's basically one big LAN, there's so much on the line for every team, because this might be the difference between a good and a bad season, especially for us. We want to go to the Major, but that means we need a good result right here, right now."

The full interview about Magisk's transition to an IGL and creating a new BC.Game culture will be released later today.

tabseN: "It's a nostalgic feeling"

vs.

After BIG beat Fluxo handily to qualify for the playoffs of the Last Chance Qualifier, Johannes "⁠tabseN⁠" Wodarz told us about the experience at the event and challenges that come with approaching such a big tournament.

"It's a nostalgic feeling, like having all these players here sitting next to each other. Setup is maybe not the very best, and everyone has the same conditions to make it through, so yeah, it's an old-school feeling for sure."

"I think [the biggest challenge is] just to stay disciplined. On the first day the opponents are maybe not the very best, but we just showed that we can play disciplined, and hopefully we can continue to do it through Sunday, and I'm looking forward to play the better teams as well."
