On the ground at the Esports World Cup LCQ

Giant Pandas have not turned up to play on day two of the Last Chance Qualifier and have already forfeited the first map of their match against INFINITE as a result.

They're not expected to turn up in time for the deadline of the second map, which has been set to 17:00 , by which point INFINITE will be awarded a default win and a spot in the playoffs.

OG are through to the 32-team playoff bracket after back-to-back wins over Vael, which came either side of a 2-1 loss to Echo.

"I think Echo was the team that we needed to beat to get some extra VRS points," Casper "⁠cadiaN⁠" Møller told HLTV after the decider match. "After we lost the anti-eco on Mirage, they kind of ran away with the momentum and ended up beating us."

The EWC qualifier is the second tournament of the season for OG, who nearly beat Spirit in the first round of BLAST Bounty.

"We had match point and we played well," he said. "I think right now, the issue with us is our consistency. We have a high ceiling, but our floor is very low, and that's something we need to work on.

"We can see in practice that we can definitely play with some of the best teams in the world, but we also see that we can lose to some of the teams that we're not supposed to. In the upcoming weeks, we'll be playing a lot of officials, and that will determine more where we need to be in the world ranking."

After a shaky start to the qualifier, cadiaN admitted that the team will need to step up their game to survive the grueling single-elimination bracket.

"Madness, I think," he said when asked what he expects tomorrow. "You need to have what we talked about as a Super Sunday.

"We need three out of three wins, and there's not a lot of room for mistakes. So let's hope that we show up with our best performance tomorrow."

The name of Kristjan "⁠fejtZ⁠" Allsaar should be familiar to those who have been following the game in-depth for some time.

Known for his journeymanship in CS:GO and time with teams from all over the world, the Estonian returned to activity earlier this year with Prestige, who have just qualified for the LCQ playoffs after eliminating Metizport.

"We have five players who want to grind the low levels to get back up again. We're not so young, but we're still hungry. Same for me, I didn't play for like two years, but I'm hungry to play and come to these LANs and hopefully still show something,"

"We don't get that much good practice, but sometimes we get tier-1 practice. We can compete, but we need to be on our A game," he said about their chances to qualify for the main event.

It took them eight match points, but they're safely through to playoffs.

Like many others, Vandulken came to the open qualifier to try their luck against the bigger teams, but have now been ousted after two convincing losses to Acend and Spirit HU.

HLTV spoke to one of the players, Taras "⁠tariksor⁠" Soroka, briefly about their experience and rather familiar name and logo:

"We saw the qualification was open and we had an opportunity to play a LAN in Paris, see the Eiffel tower, also. I'm a big fan tier-one and tier-two CS and I know maybe everyone who has a profile on HLTV here. I met some legends like device, s1mple, cadiaN. It was so nice to play. We didn't win, but we tried."

"I messaged Twistzz on Instagram if it's okay for us to use his last name as our team name. He didn't answer, but we sent you the name and the logo and you said it's okay, so maybe in honor of Twistzz, we were playing for him today (laughs)."

After NADE pulled off the upset of the LCQ so far against Eternal Fire, we asked the in-game leader, Usama "⁠USAMA⁠" Bajrami, and Omer "⁠Explo1nt⁠" Ismaili about where the team, which had zero matches on HLTV until EWC, came from.

"We played local tournaments in Skopje, Tetovo, in Kosovo, Albania, and stuff, but with this team we've been together for one month or two months, and we're practicing a lot and just enjoying the game," USAMA said.
