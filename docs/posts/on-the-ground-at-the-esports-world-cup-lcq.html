On the ground at the Esports World Cup LCQ

Dionis "⁠sinnopsyy⁠" Budeci stopped by HLTV's microphone after JiJieHao beat HOTU to become the last team in the Esports World Cup: "We had high, high expectations, actually. We have been preparing for a long time. From when I joined this team, I tried to make this kind of good job to make them play in this kind of tournaments. So I think this is the best feeling.

"I'm shocked. I don't know what to say, even. We played nine maps and it was all really close. These comebacks and this stuff, it's just crazy, and I'm really happy that we made it.

"I think we stayed in some important moments strong 'cause we had a really bad round that we lost that could go the other way."

In an interview with HLTV after BIG's qualification for the Esports World Cup, Jon "⁠JDC⁠" de Castro talked about the trash talk between his team and Sashi during the series.

"The banter started on the last map because they are a very scream-heavy team, and they like to do it," he said. "I think on Ancient we kind of got annoyed, to a degree where it was cheering us up to actually give them fire back. We were starting to get pissed off.

"So yeah, it was a lot of friendly banter, you can call it, but, at the end of the day, we wanted to win really badly. This is an old-school Counter-Strike environment. This is where I grew up back in the days, the same with tabseN, and it gave us kind of a nostalgic feeling for the event. It was pretty nice."

We asked Håvard "⁠rain⁠" Nygaard how it feels to go through back-to-back-to-back series in 100 Thieves' qualification run to the main event.

"I feel pretty good, actually. I think some energy drinks are fueling me right now. I might have a little crash later on, but it feels pretty good," he said.

He also spoke about the tough run, in which their hardest test came against BC.Game, where a single clutch separated them from early elimination.

"I actually thought it was over on Nuke on that clutch that Jack had. I thought it was one on four, and then suddenly I look up, it's, 'Oh, he's the only one alive.' 'Cause I was already like, 'Oh, fuck. I'm going home.' So it feels really good, yeah."

We asked Wiktor "⁠TaZ⁠" Wojtas about the narrow loss to 100 Thieves, where the smallest of margins — a Jack "⁠Gizmy⁠" von Spreckelsen 1v3 — cost them the match.

"CS is a game of small margins. I'm proud of the guys that they were fighting until the end. This is not something we were known for at the beginning of the year, and that's definitely a huge change.

"Some rounds you lose an advantage, some you win in a disadvantage, so we can't really talk much about it, we just need to go back and get better."
