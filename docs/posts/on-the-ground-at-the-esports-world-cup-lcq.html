daps: "I can't be too hard on the team just 'cause we haven't really practiced that much with the new lineup"

vs.

Damian "⁠daps⁠" Steele spoke with Milan "⁠Striker⁠" Švejda after NRG's elimination, and he was philosophical about his team's shortcomings.

daps, commiserations for the loss, of course. Tell me about what went wrong today.

It's hard to say. I can't be too hard on the team just 'cause we haven't really practiced that much with the new lineup, and even before the new lineup, we had a lot of internal issues that prevented practice, so lack of experience together, lack of depth on some of our maps we haven't been able to put much time into, I'd say, is the biggest issue.

Yeah, you're saying can't be too hard, still must be disappointing I suppose because this would have been a big opportunity for any team to go far, right?

Oh, of course. It's disappointing, but I have to put things into context to some extent, right? Obviously, if we had a two-week bootcamp before this in Europe and a ton of great practice and we lost like this, I'd be pretty sad, but at least we got some wins here and got some points and we just need to look to the future.

How's that future going to look like? Obviously, you're in a position where invites aren't easy to come by, especially to the tier one circuit. So what's the climb back going to be like?

Well, we qualified for Barcelona, but we have fl0m LAN, I think an ASUS LAN in Sweden, and then we're going to try and go to the third Stake LAN in Sweden as well. So just going to be a lot of tier-two stuff like we did a season before and try to get back to tier-one.

gafolo: "We definitely should have played better in the playoffs"

vs.

DENDELE captain Victor "⁠gafolo⁠" Andrade rued his team's inability to close out Inferno against JiJieHao as they let a 9-3 lead go to waste.

"We started the first half really well," he told HLTV after the match. "We lost the pistol round, we won the first gun round, and then I think we were a little too eager to close out the game. We weren't on the same page on the T side."

The loss cost the Brazilian team 25 Valve points, dropping them two places to 28th in the rankings.

"We definitely should have won the first match. I don't think qualifying was our obligation, but we definitely should have played better now in the playoffs.

"I think our morale will take a bit of a hit. This was a team we should have beaten. We'll watch the game together, go over our mistakes, and move forward."

By MIRAA

cadiaN on OG's playoff chances: "We will need a Super Sunday"

OG are through to the 32-team playoff bracket after back-to-back wins over Vael, which came either side of a 2-1 loss to Echo.

"I think Echo was the team that we needed to beat to get some extra VRS points," Casper "⁠cadiaN⁠" Møller told HLTV after the decider match. "After we lost the anti-eco on Mirage, they kind of ran away with the momentum and ended up beating us."

The EWC qualifier is the second tournament of the season for OG, who nearly beat Spirit in the first round of BLAST Bounty.

"We had match point and we played well," he said. "I think right now, the issue with us is our consistency. We have a high ceiling, but our floor is very low, and that's something we need to work on.

"We can see in practice that we can definitely play with some of the best teams in the world, but we also see that we can lose to some of the teams that we're not supposed to. In the upcoming weeks, we'll be playing a lot of officials, and that will determine more where we need to be in the world ranking."

After a shaky start to the qualifier, cadiaN admitted that the team will need to step up their game to survive the grueling single-elimination bracket.

"Madness, I think," he said when asked what he expects tomorrow. "You need to have what we talked about as a Super Sunday.

"We need three out of three wins, and there's not a lot of room for mistakes. So let's hope that we show up with our best performance tomorrow."

fejtZ after upsetting Metizport: "We're not so young, but we're still hungry"

vs.

The name of Kristjan "⁠fejtZ⁠" Allsaar should be familiar to those who have been following the game in-depth for some time.

"We have five players who want to grind the low levels to get back up again. We're not so young, but we're still hungry. Same for me, I didn't play for like two years, but I'm hungry to play and come to these LANs and hopefully still show something,"

"We don't get that much good practice, but sometimes we get tier-1 practice. We can compete, but we need to be on our A game," he said about their chances to qualify for the main event.

Gizmy posts third-highest LAN rating (3.47) as 100 Thieves ravage SadFamous

100 Thieves cruised through their opener against SadFamous in Paris thanks to a masterclass series from Jack "⁠Gizmy⁠" von Spreckelsen, who posted the third-best rating of his career on Nuke (2.27) before outdoing himself on Dust2, ending the map with a 28-4 K-D, 184.7 ADR, +19.18% Round Swing, and 3.47 rating.
