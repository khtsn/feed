vs.

Chr1zN on loss to fnatic: "I feel like we got overwhelmed with the tempo they were coming at us"

vs.

Milan "⁠Striker⁠" Švejda caught up with Christoffer "⁠Chr1zN⁠" Storgaard after HEROIC were knocked out of the EWC qualifier.

Commiserations for the loss, of course. Can you tell me about what went wrong today? It felt like you were out of the game for a lot of it.

Yeah. I feel like we kind of got overwhelmed with the tempo they were coming at us. They play very aggressive, fnatic, and they play very direct. And I feel like we basically just got outplayed, so to say. It's not good enough, but to be honest, that's the truth.

How much of a disappointment is it? You guys were coming in as probably one of the favorites to make it to the main event, so yeah.

Yeah, it's hard right now. I feel very empty, to be honest. I felt like we played a good first day, so also an okay first game here today. But I don't know, it's kind of like we just fell apart, energy-wise and solution-wise. It's hard.

Can you tell me about what the future is going to look like? Because at the moment I think you have nothing on the calendar, at least officially. Can you tell me a little bit about how the team will try to get back up over the course of the season?

Yeah. We haven't really had a boot camp yet, so we're going to go for that now. And then I think we have to wait and see a little bit on the declines for the next couple of qualifiers events, 'cause of course we're out of the bubble. And then depending on what we can get and what we don't get, we'll have to make our schedule basically from that. So it's a bit up in the air, everything right now, but hopefully within the near future we'll have some more set things.

cadiaN on OG's playoff chances: "We will need a Super Sunday"

OG are through to the 32-team playoff bracket after back-to-back wins over Vael, which came either side of a 2-1 loss to Echo.

"I think Echo was the team that we needed to beat to get some extra VRS points," Casper "⁠cadiaN⁠" Møller told HLTV after the decider match. "After we lost the anti-eco on Mirage, they kind of ran away with the momentum and ended up beating us."

The EWC qualifier is the second tournament of the season for OG, who nearly beat Spirit in the first round of BLAST Bounty.

"We had match point and we played well," he said. "I think right now, the issue with us is our consistency. We have a high ceiling, but our floor is very low, and that's something we need to work on.

"We can see in practice that we can definitely play with some of the best teams in the world, but we also see that we can lose to some of the teams that we're not supposed to. In the upcoming weeks, we'll be playing a lot of officials, and that will determine more where we need to be in the world ranking."

After a shaky start to the qualifier, cadiaN admitted that the team will need to step up their game to survive the grueling single-elimination bracket.

"Madness, I think," he said when asked what he expects tomorrow. "You need to have what we talked about as a Super Sunday.

"We need three out of three wins, and there's not a lot of room for mistakes. So let's hope that we show up with our best performance tomorrow."
