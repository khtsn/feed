G2 smash FURIA on Dust2 to secure FPG semi-finals

G2 have broken their quarter-final curse at FISSURE Playground 3, taking FURIA down 2-0 to make the top-four of a Big Event for the first time since winning BLAST Open London over a year ago.

The result is an impressive one for G2 as whilst FPG 3 features a relatively weak field, they have beaten one of the highest ranked teams in attendance to get to the semi-finals.

FURIA will be left ruing the fact they let Ancient slip through their fingers, as they were in pole position to secure the map at 11-7 up and were in general outplaying their opponents tactically.
