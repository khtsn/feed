Eternal Fire suffer shock loss to end day one of EWC Open Qualifier

The first day of the 64-team Esports World Cup open qualifier came to an end with NADE securing the upset over Eternal Fire in a three-map series, with Samet "⁠jottAAA⁠" Köklü and company becoming the only big name to find themselves in the elimination matches on Saturday.

After the first eight groups played out the first two rounds earlier on Friday, groups 9-16 took to the server for just their openers.
