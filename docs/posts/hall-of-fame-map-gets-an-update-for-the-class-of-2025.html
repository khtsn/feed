Hall of Fame map gets an update for the Class of 2025

The HLTV Hall of Fame by 1xBet map, an interactive museum and a digital playground designed to preserve the legacy of Counter-Strike's greatest icons, has gotten an update with a new nave and three new shrines for the Hall of Fame's Class of 2025.

Download the HLTV Hall of Fame map here.

When the map was first released to honor the inaugural Class of 2024, it included challenges to retake like Tommy "⁠Potti⁠" Ingemarsson, eco like Emil "⁠HeatoN⁠" Christensen, spray like Christopher "⁠GeT_RiGhT⁠" Alesund, and move like Patrik "⁠f0rest⁠" Lindberg. If you haven't tried them yet, they're still there.

Now, three new challenges have been added to celebrate the Class of 2025: Olof "⁠olofmeister⁠" Kajbjer, Raphael "⁠cogu⁠" Camargo, and Yegor "⁠markeloff⁠" Markelov.

  • Relive olofmeister's iconic moments: Take over Cobblestone's B site with a Tec9, shoot the sitting ducks from boost on Overpass with a Scout, and defuse a bomb surrounded by flames on Overpass' B site.

  • Dominate Dust2 like markeloff: Lock down Dust2 with the AWP on Long, ace against the Short rush just like markeloff did against SK, and hit your shots on players crossing through double doors.

  • Step into cogu's shoes: Stop an A play with the USP on Inferno, hold B Plat on Dust2, and hit your shots on an aim map to commemorate the Brazilian AWPer's dedication to practice that made him one of the all-time greats.

As we continue to celebrate the Class of 2025 with their challenges in the Hall of Fame map, the Hall of Fame committee is now in the process of voting on the Class of 2026.
