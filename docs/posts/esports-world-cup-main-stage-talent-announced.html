Esports World Cup main stage talent announced

The talent for the Esports World Cup main stage has been announced, featuring 20 talent members who will fulfill their roles across two weeks of action.

The lineup for the first week of competition features several well-known faces. Tres "⁠stunna⁠" Saranthus and Mike "⁠DarfMike⁠" Winnick will share studio hosting duties, while Freya "⁠Freya⁠" Spiers fulfills the role of reporter.

Jason "⁠moses⁠" O'Toole will appear on our screens in both an analyst and commentator capacity, joined by tier-one debutants Anthony "AllInGaming" Ferdinando Donny "Buckethead" Higgins in the latter lineup.

Week 1 lineup:

Studio Hosts/Reporter:

Tres "⁠stunna⁠" Saranthus

Analysts:

Janko "⁠YNk⁠" Paunović

Commentators:

Chad "⁠SPUNJ⁠" Burchill

The second week of play in Paris will see a few new faces join the action. Nathan "⁠NBK-⁠" Schmitt will join the analyst desk, while the hosting duties will be taken over by Iain "Iain Chambers" Chambers. Lou "Loupiote" Henguelle, and Chris "Puckett" Puckett.
