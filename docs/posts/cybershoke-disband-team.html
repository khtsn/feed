CYBERSHOKE disband team

CYBERSHOKE announced they disbanded their current roster and are set to return with a new lineup next season.

The announcement states that Roman "⁠Mokuj1n⁠" Petrushenko and Daniil "⁠alpha⁠" Demin are moved to the transfer list, while Vladimir "⁠yiksrezo⁠" Mitrofanov and Ilya "⁠FenomeN⁠" Kolodko depart as free agents.

The organization also shared that both Emil "⁠nota⁠" Moskvitin (on loan from PARIVISION) and Alimzhan "⁠Alkaren⁠" Bitimbay (on loan from HEROIC) will return to their parent organizations with immediate effect.

CYBERSHOKE were ranked No. 73 in the VRS ahead of their disbandment after a turbulent period, during which they were represented by 11 different players throughout 2026.
