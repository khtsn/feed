INFINITE part ways with Dytor

INFINITE have announced that they have parted ways with Martin "⁠Dytor⁠" Handl, with the 23-year-old rifler becoming a free agent.

"He's been a valuable asset in our fight for international glory," INFINITE's post to X read. "As of today, he's a free agent and ready for his next challenge. Thank you, Dytor. See you on the server."

Dytor arrived in INFINITE as part of an overhaul earlier this year that saw the team switch from a full-Romanian squad to an international lineup, joining alongside the likes of Henrich "⁠sl3nd⁠" Hevesi and Rasmus "⁠kreaz⁠" Johansson.

The new-look INFINITE enjoyed a very successful stint soon after they came together, grabbing three first-place finishes in United21 League Season 46, Parken Challenger Championship Season 5, and Esplay Elite Gaming Cup. This run helped them peak at 35th in the global VRS.

Results began to sour in recent times, however, the team sliding to the edge of the top 50 in the global VRS amidst a run which saw them flop a number of important LAN qualifiers.

INFINITE will have to fill the empty slot on their roster quickly, as they are set to kick off their Logitech G Play Connect 2026 campaign on September 18 against Liquid.
