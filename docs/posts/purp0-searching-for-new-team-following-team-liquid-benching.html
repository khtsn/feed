Team Liquid and purp0's split comes just over a week after the team's decision to bench him for 18-year-old prospect GSR ahead of Champions Shanghai. The decision has not been without criticism, with purp0 himself saying that he "deserves to play at Champions Shanghai".

After spending the past few years on different rosters, it looked as though the 2026 season with Liquid would be purp0's first full season with one team.
