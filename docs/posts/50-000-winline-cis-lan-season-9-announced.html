$50,000 WINLINE CIS LAN Season 9 announced

Nemiga won the most-recent iteration of WINLINE CIS LAN

MPKBK has announced the ninth iteration of WINLINE CIS LAN, which is set to run October 20-23 in Moscow, Russia and feature a $50,000 prizepool.

The event is open to signups for any team in the global VRS and set to feature 16 teams, battling through GSL-style BO3 groups, before a single elimination bracket takes over for the playoffs. The event will culminate in a BO5 grand final.

The tournament is set to take place ahead of the invite cut-off for PGL Major Singapore, making it a potentially valuable source of VRS points as the race for Major qualification spots heats up.

The most-recent iteration of WINLINE MPKBK CIS LAN concluded September 16 and saw Nemiga claim the title, as well as a healthy 117-point boost to their VRS standing, which rocketed them up 14 places to sit 24th in the global standings.

Recent VRS projections posted to X by Mischief suggest the race for the final Major spots in Europe could be close, and Nemiga's recent success helped catapult them into the hunt.
