3DMAX beat FUT to clinch Malta LAN Finals ticket

3DMAX qualified for the BLAST Bounty 2026 S2 LAN Finals by defeating FUT with a 2-0 scoreline (Dust2 13-11, Ancient 13-9).

The French squad will now make the flight to Malta for their third LAN event with Jeremy "⁠Kursy⁠" Gast, with the final-eight stage set to begin on July 30.

While their new recruit starred in their opener against magic with a 1.42 rating, he didn't have such joy in the series against Aulon "⁠Krabeni⁠" Fazlija's troops and finished bottom of the scoreboard. Instead, Kévin "⁠misutaaa⁠" Rabier took on the fragging load on himself, racking up 45 kills, 90 ADR, and 1.37 rating in the series.

FUT, meanwhile, will be disappointed to not qualify for the LAN portion of the tournament with recent signing Yasin "⁠xfl0ud⁠" Koç, but will find solace in the fact that the Turk finished the event as their second-highest player and showed signs of replacing Laurențiu "⁠lauNX⁠" Țârlea's firepower.
