Closed beta of HLTV Brain launched

HLTV has launched its AI chatbot, HLTV Brain, to a limited selection of users in closed beta.

The assistant draws from HLTV's extensive internal database of players, teams, matches, news, rankings, and stats to answer questions about Counter-Strike. It is trained on and uses exclusively HLTV's data to answer questions.

The closed beta is planned to last two weeks, with more users being added over time. To have your chance to enter, join the waitlist here.

And before you get a chance to try it out for yourself, feel free to ask your questions in the comments, and we may pick some to answer in a future article.

And if you're curious just how hard it is to win a Big Event when putting up stats like Finn "⁠karrigan⁠" Andersen did at the Cologne Major, HLTV Brain can do that too.

AIHLTV Brain

Your figures check out. karrigan was on the Falcons roster that won IEM Cologne Major 2026, and his Rating 3.0 across the 20 maps of the Major stages is 0.71, with a K-D difference of -147, 54.5 ADR and 0.42 KPR. Rating 3.0 is the version in force for this window; his rating is 0.77 on 2.0 and 0.75 on 2.1, and those versions are not calibrated against each other.

On the sample I could query, a player-event rating in the 0.80 to 0.99 band won its event only about 3% of the time, and below 0.80 the rate was about 0.6%, from one win in 155 player-events. Read that as under 1% for a player at this level, with the caveat that the sample is small and the band is thin. For comparison, the all-player average was about 7.9%, and players rated 1.20 and above won about 23.4%.
