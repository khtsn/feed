Player prices & pick stats 

  • The online stage right off the break had an interesting effect on the team list, with Spirit being the only top contender making it to Finals, and MOUZ (#10 in HLTV ranking) going next. Needless to say, it made the prices rather peculiar. And with the matchups not being decided at the time when the game was created, it makes for a perfect head scratcher.

  • By being on the absolute favorite team, Danil "⁠donk⁠" Kryshkovets goes for the highest price of $250,000. Nevertheless, the Russian star is still on every other team in the game, making him the second-most picked player.

  • Meanwhile, Liquid’s Jonathan "⁠EliGE⁠" Jablonowski is the most popular pick with his low price of $187,000. In the previous season, the American wasn’t putting up very high numbers, but he averaged a 1.49 rating in the online matches against G2 and Vitality. Now, 60% of Fantasy players believe he’s a worthy pick under the leadership of Johnny "⁠JT⁠" Theodosiou.
