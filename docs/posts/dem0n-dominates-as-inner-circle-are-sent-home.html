dem0n dominates as Inner Circle are sent home

FUT have secured a top-eight finish at BLAST Open after eliminating Inner Circle in 2-0 fashion.

The series also saw them break their nine-map losing streak on Nuke, a run that stretches back to April 11, after a dominant 9-3 CT side and both pistols meant they barely had to break a sweat on their weaker T side.

Whether FUT have improved their previously dire T side on Nuke remains to be seen, given the one-sided nature of this match, but András "⁠coolio⁠" Fercsák's message at half-time made it clear how they are planning to fix it: "Follow the strats, don't try to be smart. When we don't think, we are winning."

Inner Circle began the event by upsetting Vitality but that surprise proved to be a flash in the pan as they were outclassed by MOUZ and now FUT without hitting double digits on any of those four map losses.

Dmytro "⁠dem0n⁠" Myroshnychenko made the difference on Mirage, setting the tone with a flying Dualies kill and ending with a 1.81 rating.

"After I did that shot, I knew I'd have a good game," dem0n told the desk post-game.

Nuke was the opportunity for Inner Circle to level things up, but a 0-8 start made that all but impossible. Arseniy "⁠cptkurtka023⁠" Derevinskiy, star of the Vitality win, was 0-9 in opening duels.

Meanwhile dem0n kept up his form, immortal during that 8-0 run, and the Ukrainian finished the series on a 2.02 rating with 103 ADR and +8.82% Swing.

Now, Legacy or Vitality stand in their way to see who books one of the final two tickets to Porto for playoffs.
