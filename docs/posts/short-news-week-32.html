Short: fox announces tier-2 LAN with €12,000 prize pool

Portuguese CS legend Ricardo "⁠fox⁠" Pacheco has announced the first edition of his tier-2 LAN tournament, called FOX Legacy.

The tournament is scheduled for October 1–4 at the SAW Esports Arena in Vila Nova de Gaia. It will feature a maximum of 16 teams and a prize pool of up to €12,000.

Registration for the event will open on August 21 at 19:00 .

Short: hally: "The guys have more space to implement their own ideas"

Sergey "⁠hally⁠" Shavaev has added some clarity to his role in the new Spirit set up alongside Dmitry "⁠S0tF1k⁠" Forostyanko in a video on the team's Telegram.

"After Astana, after talking with the guys and with the CEO, it became clear that the format of our work would change," he said. "Now, first of all, I don't do everything with my own hands anymore, and second, the guys have more space to integrate and implement their own ideas. My role is different from the one I was used to."

He continued: "There's a global picture, how things should look, and I'm the one directing that. At the same time, a lot of things happen through dialogue. S0tF1k is responsible for the micro. He says how it's best to play in a certain moment, how it's best to throw grenades, and so on.

"At the same time, through dialogue with him and with magixx, we discuss how we're going to play CS on a global level. We arrive at something common, and overall, out of the suggestions the guys put forward, I try to pick what will be more optimal, but in most cases it happens through live dialogue."

Short: PARIVISION CEO on Cologne stickers: "We got three times more from Budapest"

PARIVISION's CEO, Andrii "Kimi" Kvasnevskyi, shared some insight into the recent Cologne Major sticker sales in a season recap video posted by the organization, stating that "the new system hurt us a lot financially."

In July, HLTV reported that sticker sales from Cologne were almost five times less than the StarLadder Budapest Major, after which Valve released new ranked stickers with static pricing rather than the dynamic pricing used for the initial release.
