Short news: Week 32

PARIVISION's CEO, Andrii "Kimi" Kvasnevskyi, shared some insight into the recent Cologne Major sticker sales in a season recap video posted by the organization, stating that "the new system hurt us a lot financially."

In July, HLTV reported that sticker sales from Cologne were almost five times less than the StarLadder Budapest Major, after which Valve released new ranked stickers with static pricing rather than the dynamic pricing used for the initial release.
