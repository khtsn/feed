    Short: Tauson out with reactive arthritis; plans to return for FISSURE

    Sebastian "⁠Tauson⁠" Tauson Lindelof revealed in an interview with Dust2.dk that the reason he is sitting out the IEM Beijing qualifier is reactive arthritis.

    Read more

    Maden steps in for GamerLegion in IEM Beijing closed qualifier

    "I have been hospitalized for 4-5 days now because I have a disease called reactive arthritis," he said. "It first appeared in my knee, but then it also spread to my left hand, so that is also why I can't play."

    He says doctors expect it could take up to 1-3 weeks to heal, but hopes to be healthy in time for FISSURE Playground 3 in China, which starts in a week.
