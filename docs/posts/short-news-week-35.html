Short news: Week 35

StarLadder has announced that the final stop in the tier-two Stake Ranked tournament series will be played on LAN in the Schengen region on November 17-20, with an open qualifier on October 10-11 and a closed qualifier on 19-22. Two teams will come from the qualifiers, along with six invited in the October 5 VRS update to compete at the $100,000 event.

BC.Game will not be at PGL Bucharest after exiting the second Europe open qualifier in the Round of 32 with an 11-13 loss to EAC.

Oleksandr "⁠s1mple⁠" Kostyliev and company lost to FOKUS in the quarter-finals of the first qualifier and couldn't get the job done the second time around, leaving the team with only ROG Journey Autumn and DraculaN Season 7 on its calendar.

The schedule and matchups for the remainder of the second open qualifier, which will conclude on Sunday, can be found here.

ENCE has announced that it has parted ways with 17-year-old Niki "⁠Cliqq⁠" Kinnunen, who joined the organization in 2025 to play in ENCE Academy after nearly a year in MOUZ NXT and had been playing in the Finnish organization's main squad since April 2026 until being benched in August.
