BC.Game crack top two in Asia VRS after ROG JOURNEY groups

Magisk's troops are now ranked No. 2 in the regional standings.

The group stage of ROG JOURNEY Autumn ended with twelve teams progressing to the playoffs, with the group winners earning a direct ticket to the quarters, while the second- and third-placed teams are set to contest the first round of the single-elimination playoffs later on Friday.

One of the group winners was BC.Game, who also played five games with new recruit Tyson "⁠asap⁠" Paterson, meaning they have switched from Europe to the Asia VRS.

Oleksandr "⁠s1mple⁠" Kostyliev and company are now ranked second in the Asian region and are predicted to make the Major with a 95.9% chance as per HLTV's invite simulation.

Elsewhere in the group stage, SINNERS won their group that also contained 100 Thieves and Eternal Fire, Sangal racked up the most points in Group B, and JiJieHao went flawless in Group C.
