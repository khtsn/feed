Lynn Vision dominate Chinese derby to eliminate TYLOO from EWC

Starry had the play of the game with a quick triple on Nuke to turn a round on its head

Lynn Vision won the Chinese derby against TYLOO 2-0 on Nuke (13-8) and Inferno (13-4) to secure survival in the Esports World Cup's Group C and eliminate their countrymen from contention.

TYLOO had a strong start to the series on Nuke, earning an 8-4 lead on the CT side, but went completely missing after that as Lynn Vision won out on Cedar Creek with a clean 9-0 defense to make it 13-8 on their pick.

Starry revives lost round with three quick kills in the retake

The second map, Inferno, was TYLOO's choice, but it was no contest for Lynn Vision, who posted an 8-4 half on the CT side and once again won a clean second half, this time 5-0 on the attack, to close out the map 13-4 and win the series.

Junjie "⁠EmiliaQAQ⁠" Tang shone on Inferno with an impressive 2.23 rating and +16.43% swing in his team's dominating display, which propelled him to the top of the series' leaderboard with a 1.67 rating across both maps.

Up next for Lynn Vision will be the second lower bracket match against the loser of Vitality vs. B8 for a spot in the top 16 bracket.
