Teams and format

The Open Qualifier will kick off the event with a LAN competition, featuring 64 teams split into 16 GSL groups. An all-best-of-three format will see the top two teams from each group advance to a 32-team single-elimination bracket, from which the top four will advance to the main event.

The main event will feature 32 teams, with 27 invites through the VRS plus Lynn Vision (who earned their spot through winning ACL), and four earning their spots through the Open Qualifier. The teams will compete in a double-elimination group stage, which begins with a best-of-one match before best-of-threes take over for the remainder of the event until the best-of-five grand final.

Ahead of the grand final, there will be a third-place decider with additional prize money and VRS points on the line.
