    Short: Walczaki withdraw from GLuck Moscow as earnings would go to Russian Federation

    Walczaki have withdrawn from the GLuck Moscow Cyber Games Open Qualifier, forfeiting all three of their matches in the Swiss group stage.

    "We have ultimately withdrawn from the GLuck Moscow Cyber Games tournament due to the fact that we did not notice earlier a clause in the regulations stating that a team that wins anything from this tournament would have to pay 30% of that amount to the Russian Federation," the Polish team posted on X. "We made a mistake but we are only human."
