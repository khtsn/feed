    Short: Isurus bring back pino to replace Xamp

    Isurus has announced that Rodrigo "⁠pino⁠" Manarino will take over as head coach in place of Willian "⁠Xamp⁠" Caldas, who departs the lineup after just four months.

    pino previously served as Isurus' coach from July 2021 to February 2024 before moving to BESTIA, first as a coach before later becoming the team's manager.

    By Nohte

    Short: BC.Game bid farewell to aNdu

    BC.Game has announced the departure of Andreas "⁠aNdu⁠" Maasing, who was sidelined by the organization in January when it signed the Portuguese SAW core.

    The Estonian rifler was loaned to ALGO in March, where he averaged a 1.04 rating over 81 maps before the organization chose to disband its lineup and step away from Counter-Strike, and then returned to BC.Game's bench before being released.
