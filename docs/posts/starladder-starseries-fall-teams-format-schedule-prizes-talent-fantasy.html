Teams and format

The eight-team tournament will feature a stacked lineup, as MOUZ, Vitality, FURIA, and Natus Vincere all accepted direct invites to the event.

Joining them will be Aurora and magic, who progressed through the European qualifier, as well as MIBR (SA qualifier) and NRG (NA).

The event will be played in a double-elimination best-of-three bracket, set to culminate with a best-of-five final.
