Okay, congratulations. That Inferno was insane from you. How did it feel to play that?

To be honest, Inferno was a pleasure on the map. Every second that we talked with each other, there was movement, there was, 'Let's do that. I'm going to play here. I'm going to push here.' Every second on TeamSpeak was very alive, and that's something that we know as players, that when we are talking like that, no one can beat us. It was going to be very competitive for the other team to get the round. So I was super happy and obviously, individually, me and mezii, every bullet connected to the head. A bit of jump shots, a bit of knife. So it's always nice.

Is it particularly nice when they're all 50 HP from the nades?

Yeah, yeah. I was talking to Dan. I told him, 'I'm killing all your leftovers,' and he had 14 assists in the half. He killed seven, I killed 16, and I had less damage than him, so it's crazy how much utility damage he does and how good he is at doing that. Yeah, of course. Makes it much easier.

And overall, it feels like the team's really powered up again, back with your five for. I mean, maybe apEX has to leave tomorrow, I don't know. But so far, so good?

So far, so good. We're playing really, really well with each other right now, and we're excited to play Aurora tomorrow. Obviously, they are a team that we lost to recently, and we also beat them on the same day, but it's a team that we consider competitive. They're a good team. They have some good ideas. They have good individuals, so we're really excited to play tomorrow, but I think if we bring our A game, it's going to be very tough for them to win.

The one thing that brings the A game is just courage

Shahar "⁠flameZ⁠" Shushan

When you prepare for teams, I know obviously MaT does so much work, but how much of it is just making sure you're at your A game? Because we know that when you're on top, no one's gonna stop you.

To be honest, I think the prep matters to bring the A game, but the one thing that brings the A game is just courage. If every player on the team is very willing to go for plays, willing to just do stuff, then that's when we're on our A game. So sometimes individually we're not playing that well, but we're moving so much, it's really hard for the other team to get to the face.

But also for the prep, everybody has individual preps. Dan, the IGL, MaT, Rémy [XTQZZZ], they all do really big prep and as individuals everybody chimes in with some things that will bring him confidence in the game, some ideas to play himself. So it's just like that.

And how much have you developed in that way with adding your voice and things? Because we know when you joined you were the classic entry fragger, right? But now you are much more talkative.

I think I always had talking in mind, but the one thing that for me, how I reference it, is sometimes you can be a bit lazy with the communication. Sometimes I get lazy with the communication, and that's something that I can't... I'm a chatty person. I talk a lot, and in the game it comes really well that I can talk and speak and speak, and it makes the TeamSpeak alive. So that's something that the team has told me many times they need, and I just need to keep bringing it because it's so important.

And regarding what to say, sometimes just bringing ideas, sometimes just telling, 'Yo, Dan, we can do that,' in the middle of the round, just gives him another idea in mind and it's good. It makes him think, makes him feel the game, and sometimes it's going to be to the rest of the players, sometimes it's going to tell someone to make a play, don't be scared. And those little words, they make it so... They make us win a lot of times.
