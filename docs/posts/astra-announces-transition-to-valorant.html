ASTRA announces transition to VALORANT

Mayline-Joy "⁠ASTRA⁠" Champliaud has announced her transition to VALORANT due to "female CS falling apart and the lack of tournaments coming up."

The French rifler led her BIG EQUIPA team to the ESL Impact League Season 8 title at the end of 2025, leading to her Women's Player of the Year award, while BIG EQUIPA claimed the award for Women's Team of the Year.

Following the ESL Impact circuit being put on hold at the end of 2025, however, many organizations released their female teams, with BIG EQUIPA following suit by pausing the project in May.

"I find myself missing practice and competition. Therefore, I made the decision to transition to Valorant and to put my focus into a growing scene," ASTRA wrote on X.

"My goals are the same as in CS, I only want to compete for the top. This wasn't an easy decision to make but I'm in a happy place, I'm very passionate and committed to what I set out to do and this is a challenge that I would love to take on."
