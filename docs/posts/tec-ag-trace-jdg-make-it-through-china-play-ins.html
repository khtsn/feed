Ever since Kickoff, All Gamers just haven't looked like the same squad regionally, but the play-ins revived a team that could've missed Champions after a hot start to the year.

Unlike TEC, who started by facing a tier-two team, AG began in the upper quarterfinals with a 2-1 victory over Trace Esports. Despite the series reaching three maps, all were one-sided, leading to a quick match with two convincing map wins. The victory over JDG was a lot closer than expected, but clutch performances from playoff stars f4ngeer and Shr1mp completed the sweep.

F4ngeer led all players in the majority of play-in stats, including a 1.21 match rating, 265 ACS, 1.34 K:D, 165.5 ADR, and 0.92 KPR. Shr1mp wasn't far behind, with a 1.17 match rating and a 1.26 K:D, good enough for fourth.

For Trace, the team has a similar tale to TEC: struggling regionally and bouncing back in play-ins. Unlike TEC, Trace does have some past regional and international experience, but the team has shifted away from players like heybay and Biank toward deLb and Abo , with Kai as the only mainstay of the old core.

Trace ended the Stage 2 group stage 0-5, but were victorious over TEC in the seeding match and swept A Team to start the play-ins. A f4ngeer masterclass led AG over Trace, but that was Trace's only loss in the tournament.

As the format suggested, there could be a team with only two wins that qualifies for the playoffs, and that ended up being JDG.

A close 2-1 upper quarterfinals victory over FPX was followed by a tough loss against AG, sending the squad to lowers but placing the team in a win-and-in position against EDward Gaming. The expectations of the two teams couldn't be more different, but a life game from crownfisher toppled the regional powerhouse and qualified the team for playoffs.

Out of all the JDG players, crownfisher was the most important one in this play-in run, ending it with a region-high 79% KAST. Yuicaw also improved his play for this tournament, and he knows what it's like to go on an underdog run from his Wolves Esports days.
