G2 went with a Yoru-Neon double-Duelist comp with BABYBAY on Chamber, while FURIA went with a Neon-Phoenix with Shyy on Cypher.

After dropping the opening pistol of the series, FURIA went on a run on attack, picking up seven straight rounds before entering halftime up 8-4. Once the teams switched sides though, it seemed like a completely different game. A story of one-and-a-half maps was told, with G2 going on a run in its own defense half, letting up just one round to mount a comeback in a 13-10 win.

In a matchup of what is regarded as a "should-be" superteam and a dark horse in Americas, MIBR just barely had the edge over Evil Geniuses, winning in a three-map series that was separated by five total rounds.

The series began on MIBR's pick of Lotus, a map that seemed to be a counter-pick. While MIBR had yet to play the map in Stage 2, EG was 0-2 on the map. EG tweaked its comp on this day, switching out Fade for Skye in a Neon-Raze double-Duelist comp, while MIBR went with aspas as the solo Raze in an Omen-Viper comp with Vyse.

Early on, MIBR's pick seemed to have worked out perfectly. Despite picking up the pistol and anti-eco to start the map, EG had trouble getting much of anything going on attack, letting up a run of seven straight MIBR rounds to enter halftime down 8-4. In what quickly became a battle between the Razes, with MIBR's aspas and EG's zerona , a more proactive approach showed almost immediately that EG was not dead yet.
