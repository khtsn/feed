Alliance stun FaZe at FISSURE Playground

The Swedish squad is back with a bang after almost two months of inactivity.

Alliance have upset FaZe in the opening round of FISSURE Playground 3 in a two-map series (Ancient 13-9, Nuke 13-11).

The Swedish squad will now face Legacy for a spot in the playoffs, while FaZe will attempt to avoid elimination in a lower-bracket clash against magic.

The series began on Ancient, where FaZe couldn't find their footing despite Russel "⁠Twistzz⁠" Van Dulken's fantastic performance. The Canadian finished the map with a 1.62 rating, 26 kills, and 100 ADR, but his teammates failed to provide backup as FaZe dropped the map with a 9-13 scoreline.

Nuke was a closer affair, where Alliance got the job done in the final round of regulation. The Swedish squad got off to a 7-1 start that gave them a platform for victory, with Jonatan "⁠bobeksde⁠" Persson crowning a great performance with a 4k in the final round.

Alliance, who haven't played an official in almost two months since their early BLAST Bounty elimination at the hands of Wildcard, have now risen up to No. 18 in the VRS standings to strengthen their chances of making the Singapore Major.
