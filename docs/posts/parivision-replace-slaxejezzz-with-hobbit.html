PARIVISION replace slaxejezzz with HObbit

The youngster moves to the bench and is open to loan offers.

PARIVISION have announced the signing of Abay "⁠HObbit⁠" Khassenov, with the veteran replacing Vyacheslav "⁠slaxejezzz⁠" Vinokurov on the active roster.

slaxejezzz is moved to the bench, with the organization looking to loan the 18-year-old to a different team.

"It is important to emphasize: we are not considering selling Slava [slaxejezzz]," Andrey Kvasnevsky, CEO of PARIVISION, said in a statement. "He remains a part of PARIVISION, and we are counting on him in the future."

HObbit joins the team after a short stand-in period during the summer, during which he helped the team place second at XSE Pro League Guangzhou.

He was replaced by Evgenii "⁠FL1T⁠" Lebedev shortly after the tournament, but now returns alongside the Russian rifler to reunite with long-term coach Dastan "⁠dastan⁠" Akbayev, who spoke about HObbit at EWC.

"I even think HObbit right now is stronger than, for example, slaxejezzz," he said in Paris when asked about the decision to remove the experienced Kazakh from the roster at the time.
