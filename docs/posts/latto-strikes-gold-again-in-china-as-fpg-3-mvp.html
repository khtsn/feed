latto strikes gold again in China as FPG 3 MVP

Bruno "⁠latto⁠" Rebelatto has been named the HLTV x 1xBET Most Valuable Player of FISSURE Playground 3 after leading Legacy in their championship-winning campaign in Suzhou, China.

The 23-year-old dominated the 3-0 grand final affair against G2 with a 1.67 rating to lock down the award, fending off teammate Vinicius "⁠n1ssim⁠" Pereira and opponent Guy "⁠NertZ⁠" Iluz in what started out as a tight race to the medal heading into championship Sunday.

Combined with his impressive semi-final showing against MIBR, latto was by far the highest-rated player in the playoffs (1.45 ahead of n1ssim's 1.25) and turned out an impressive 1.19 kills per round win over the six maps Legacy played over the weekend.

Overall, he ended the event on a 1.31 rating, second-best only to Anarbileg "⁠cobrazera⁠" Uuganbayar, and appeared on several other tournament leaderboards for his efforts.

This is latto's third Most Valuable Player award, all of which he has garnered in less than a year over three trips to China following Legacy's successes at CS Asia Championships in 2025 and 2026.
