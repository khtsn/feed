The American organization has been away from Counter-Strike since 2020, when they fielded an international lineup featuring players like Michał "⁠MICHU⁠" Müller, Noah "⁠Nifty⁠" Francis, and Thomas "⁠Thomas⁠" Utting.

With the signing, Envy returns to French Counter-Strike, which gave the organization their only Major title back in DreamHack Open Cluj-Napoca 2015.

The new Envy squad is currently embroiled in a close battle for a Singapore Major slot, as it is currently ranked No. 21 in the global VRS standings and set to attend Stake Pulse Beat III and DraculaN S7 before the cutoff.

They are expected to make their debut under Envy at Stake Pulse Beat III, set to begin on October 12.
