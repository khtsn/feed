Talent announced for ESL Pro League Season 24

ESL has announced the talent lineup for ESL Pro League Season 24, with a ten-strong team of well-known faces set to bring the action to life in Katowice, Poland from October 3-11.

The event will see Freya "⁠Freya⁠" Spiers and Tres "⁠stunna⁠" Saranthus presumably take up hosting and sideline duties, although ESL's post did not specify what roles each talent member would be fulfilling during the broadcast.

Chad "⁠SPUNJ⁠" Burchill, Alex "⁠Machine⁠" Richardson, Janko "⁠YNk⁠" Paunović, Harry "⁠JustHarry⁠" Russell Hugo "⁠Hugo⁠" Byron, Adam "⁠Dinko⁠" Hawthorne, Jason "⁠moses⁠" O'Toole and Henry "⁠HenryG⁠" Greer make up the rest of the talent team.
