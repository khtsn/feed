Falcons and Aurora players are most-picked in TWC 2026 Fantasy

Aurora players surge in popularity after their third-place EPL run

Thunderpick World Championship 2026

Format

  • You can create your teams on the Fantasy page here.

  • Two four-team double-elimination groups.

  • Four-team single-elimination playoffs with a third-place decider.

  • All matches are BO3 except for BO5 grand final.

Teams

Things to note 

  • Due to a presence of the third-place decider, teams making semi-finals will guarantee two rounds of play for themselves.

  • Teams making playoffs via upper final will get one round of padding for skipping group stage decider matches.

Player prices & pick stats 

  • Falcons are the most popular team with Finn "⁠karrigan⁠" Andersen being the most popular player in the game with 34% of all teams featuring the Dane. The Danish IGL is also the cheapest player in the game at $173k, but as usual, it’s worth noting that he averages a 0.77 rating in the past three months, and he was in the red in nine out of ten past maps.

  • Four Aurora players made the top-10 picks in this game, with Özgür "⁠woxic⁠" Eker being the most popular one with 28% teams featuring the Turkish AWPer. The team’s popularity is explained by modest prices, but it’s worth noting that they are set to face Falcons in the winners’ match, a team they lost to a few days ago at EPL.
