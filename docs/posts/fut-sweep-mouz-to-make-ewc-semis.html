FUT sweep MOUZ to make EWC semis

FUT are through to the semi-finals of Esports World Cup, earning their victory with confident wins over MOUZ on Dust2 (16-13) and Ancient (13-5).

With their victory FUT have now set up a semi-final matchup with FURIA, and there is a real chance this young side can make it all the way to the grand final.

MOUZ opted for a change in veto ahead of this matchup, having found themselves bested by FUT in their previous two meetings. Switching their pick away from Nuke was a bit of a head-scratcher, and it did not pay off at all.

FUT weathered a quick 5-0 start by MOUZ to take firm control of the game, their proactive and in-your-face CT style leaving MOUZ dizzy. Map control was contested, fast flanks were found, and FUT were very much in the driver's seat heading into the second half.

dem0n nearly pulls off nutty 1v4

MOUZ managed to haul themselves back into the game in the second half, excellent utility usage setting them up perfectly for multiple retakes, whilst FUT found themselves guilty of a few oversteps at times.

OT was found by MOUZ but ultimately, they were certainly second-best on this map. FUT got their heads screwed back on and confidently took OT 4-1, taking the lead heading into their own map pick.

cmtry pulls off crisp AK 3K as FUT start to pull away
