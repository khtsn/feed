BCGame qualify for Stake Pulse Beat III

BC.Game secured the last spot at Stake Pulse Beat III, which is scheduled to take place in Stockholm, Sweden, from October 12-17, by beating Sashi 2-1 in the grand final of the second open qualifier.

TaZ: "I'm proud of the guys that they were fighting until the end. This is not something we were known for at the beginning of the year"

Emil "⁠Magisk⁠" Reif's troops had faltered on their first attempt after a loss to Xcity, a team ranked #325 on the VRS, and had to stave off a comeback effort from Phantom in the semi-finals and reverse-sweep Sashi to get the job done the second time around.

The qualification follows a labored online run for BC.Game, who also suffered early exits in both PGL Masters Bucharest open qualifiers and the IEM Beijing open qualifier in recent weeks.

Their VRS rank didn't take a big hit as two of those eliminations came before the HLTV-covered rounds, helping keep their Singapore Major hopes alive, but the struggles show the team still has a long way to go with their new roster and to build on their title run at Elisa Breakout Series 1 and 9-16th exit at the Esports World Cup Open Qualifier.
