YEKINDAR after FURIA rout paiN: "After EWC, we felt angry at ourselves; I guess that actually helped"

FURIA began their BLAST Open Porto run just three days after finishing fourth at the Esports World Cup with losses to FUT and Legacy, and could have come into the tournament deflated after fumbling a prime opportunity to at least make the finals in Paris.

"After EWC, we felt angry," Mareks "⁠YEKINDAR⁠" Gaļinskis said to the analyst desk after beating paiN, speaking about an interview he gave earlier. "Angry at ourselves because there were many moments for us to actually advance to the grand finals in that game against FUT. Many of those situations were, of course, their brilliance in terms of the way they played, but also our mistakes that didn't allow us to close the game.

"The interviewer was like, 'Well, do you think it's a good thing that you're angry, or not?' and I said, 'We will see after this event.' I guess the angriness actually helped."

It was a show of force for FURIA in a convincing victory over their countrymen from paiN, where FURIA dominated the series from start to finish, picked up their first win on Cache, and swept a series for the first time in two months since beating Aurora 2-0 in the semi-finals of the Cologne Major.

paiN looked flustered on their Mirage pick, rarely getting a foothold in rounds and fumbling one of their few opportunities to time as a team-wide FURIA performance led to a landslide 10-2 half and 13-2 win.

FURIA's domination carried onto Cache, a map where YEKINDAR has been handling in-game leader duties, and the Latvian was simply unstoppable as he called circles around paiN for much of the first half, making use of timings and creating gaps in the defenses that he exploited repeatedly to run away with a 18-7 K-D, +15.68% Round Swing, and 163.7 ADR by halftime before FURIA closed not long into the second half.

Asked by Donald "⁠voo⁠" Parkhurst whether he enjoys calling after he seemed down on it at EWC, YEKINDAR admitted he would like to be an IGL, but later in his career.

"In my career, in my future, I would want to become an IGL," he said. "But I would also want to become an IGL when I understand that I'm not capable of keeping up with the role players anymore.

"Right now, in my career, I do feel that I'm still in my prime in terms of decision-making, maybe I'm not the same 'crisp' 2021 YEKINDAR or whatever, but in terms of my decision-making and the ways I can help the team as a role player, right now, I value a lot."
