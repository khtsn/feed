After carving an undefeated path through the play-ins, Enterprise faced a new challenge in a Vitality squad that earned fourth at Masters London and the Group Alpha second seed in the group stage.

EP selected Summit, which had been Vitality's permaban throughout the group stage, for the first map. With it being Vitality's first time showing Summit, they took EP by surprise with a resounding 10-2 defense. Chronicle 's Chamber in particular posed serious issues for EP as he farmed picks with the Operator in Mid, while Jamppi on Phoenix found crucial kills in Vitality's retakes.

A second pistol for Vitality made the lead an insurmountable 12-2. EP held out until round 19, where Chronicle won a bizarre knife duel to take it 13-6. The Chamber player ended the map with 22 kills and 322 ACS.

The second map was the first instance of Abyss in the VCT circuit since its recent return to the map pool. Both teams opted for double-Duelist looks with Harbor, although Vitality included a Vyse while EP forwent a Sentinel entirely in favor of an Astra.

EP controlled the first half after kozzy found a 4K to win the round two eco, starting an economic snowball that led them to an 8-4 lead. That lead quickly dissipated to an 8-8 draw, however, as EP struggled to fend off Vitality's repeated B splits, soon leading to Vitality taking the lead 11-9. After a timeout, EP dug their heels in, finally shutting down Mid and holding onto A to narrowly win 13-11. kozzy was unleashed on Jett with a 30/19/4 KDA, seven first bloods, and 225 ADR.

Lotus was the most one-sided map of the three. EP dominated from start to finish as MAGNUM and iluri could do no wrong, racking up five clutches and five 3Ks between them for a 9-3 attack half. They then won their first pistol of the entire series to make it 11-3 and end it in a statement 13-4. MAGNUM racked up 20 kills in 17 rounds as Vyse, while iluri followed just behind with 18 on Skye.

The second match of the day pitted the only tier one team to make it out of play-ins in FUT against the Group Omega second seed in Team Liquid.

FUT's pick of Haven, contrary to the norm on Haven, was a tale of two defenses. Liquid steamrolled FUT at first, shutting down B and winning every duel for an 8-0 start that FUT salvaged to a 9-3 score.

Liquid won the second pistol but FUT managed to win a force buy on defense to make it 10-5. From there, Liquid found themselves in a similar position to the one they put FUT in; their only round for the rest of the map was a kamo ace as FUT rode the momentum all the way to a 13-11 win in regulation.

Liquid's pick of Summit was FUT's first time playing the map. Both sides went for double-Duelist lineups without Sentinels; Liquid opted for Viper in its stead while FUT went for an unorthodox Breach pick. Liquid had another good start with a bonus conversion to go up 4-0, but FUT this time responded with solid shutdowns to make it a much more competitive 6-6 half.
