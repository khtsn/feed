Snappi steps down from NIP

Marco "⁠Snappi⁠" Pfeiffer has stepped down from Ninjas in Pyjamas's active roster to make way for a new in-game leader following the two sides' mutual agreement, the organization has announced.

The announcement follows an HLTV report which first unveiled that the Swedish organization was scouting the market for IGLs as well as for another player to take stand-in Roman "⁠n0te⁠" Hamze's spot on the team.

The same report also revealed Snappi was open to transitioning to a new role after a nearly two-decade-long playing career. The 36-year-old has yet to comment on the situation.

Snappi had been Ninjas in Pyjamas's in-game leader for over a year and a half after taking over from Alejandro "⁠alex⁠" Masanet at the beginning of 2025.

Under his tutelage, Ninjas in Pyjamas's biggest Big Event achievement came in the form of a quarter-finals finish at PGL Astana that year, along with smaller titles from Fragadelphia Las Vegas, HyperX Roman Imperium Cup VI and Stake Ranked Episode 2.

But otherwise, the team struggled to break through into the tier-one circuit consistently, remaining under the top-20 threshold on the VRS for the majority of Snappi's tenure with the organization.

Once known for his solid fragging for an in-game leader, the Dane struggled to keep up form, averaging a 0.83 rating against top-20 opposition in that time.

Snappi's removal makes Ninjas in Pyjamas's roster the following:

Rasmus "⁠sjuush⁠" Beck

Richard "⁠Xizt⁠" Landström (coach)

Marco "⁠Snappi⁠" Pfeiffer (benched)
