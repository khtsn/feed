iM on losing to M80: "F***ing s**t, that's the first thought"

Natus Vincere's slow start to the season, which kicked off with a round of 16 exit at Esports World Cup, just took a turn for the worse after they were upset by M80 in their opening bout at BLAST Open Porto.

"Today I think we came pretty prepared," Mihai "⁠iM⁠" Ivan stated when discussing the loss to M80 with HLTV. "We kind of knew what to do, what's our plan on Mirage. We couldn't do it for some reason, some small details. Someone is missing something, we did a wrong call in the wrong time. We also lost full USP, which definitely doesn't help us at all."

B1ad3: "We were close to making some changes"

Recently Natus Vincere's coach Andrey "⁠B1ad3⁠" Gorodenskiy admitted the team were close to making changes in the most recent off-season, but weren't able to get any worthwhile transfers over the line. Despite this, iM doesn't think that fact has affected the team negatively.

"We knew about it," iM said of Natus Vincere's efforts to bring in fresh blood. "We discussed it so many times. They decided to keep the same roster for this season, and then we see what happens. That's pretty much it, there's nothing to say. I don't think it will affect us that much."

What are your first thoughts on that series?

Fucking shit, that's the first thought. But yeah, we kind of knew what maps we were going to play. We got a bit surprised on Anubis, but I don't think that was a problem because we won it. And then afterwards, I dunno.

It's been a little bit of a slow start to the season. Do you think that decision to kind of take the first tournament of the season off and practice is paying off, or do you think maybe you're starting a bit slowly because you've had less officials than some of these other teams?

I will not say so, but we also discussed after we lost the EWC that we need some officials to play. We kind of didn't play our game that day against Legacy, because we know how we play in practice. For example, if that game will be practiced in the same scenario, we will maybe win, who knows? But we'll not play the same, I want to say.

That's a different topic, but today I think we came pretty prepared. We won the first map and then we kind of knew what to do, what's our plan on Mirage. We couldn't do it for some reason, some small details. Someone is missing something, we also did a wrong call in the wrong time. We also lost full USP, which definitely doesn't help us at all. I think overall we just need to have confidence, even though it's kind of bad to have it back now, but we'll just try our best in the lower bracket.

I think overall we just need to have confidence

Is that what you put it down to today, then? Is it the technical aspects of Counter-Strike, or is it the mental, psychological side of things that let you down today?

I think it can be both. I dunno, because if you speak about mental, what was the difference between Anubis and Mirage, for example? Or Anubis and Inferno, right? So maybe we underestimated them, let's say, after the first map and then we could not come back out of that.

Maybe some players got tilted, frustrated because of some plays, even me on Mirage, I got a bit frustrated over some timings that I got. If you speak about the tactics part, I dunno. Like I said, we knew about at least the Mirage and Inferno, that we're going to have them in the map pool, and then we still didn't do anything about it.

I don't think it will affect us that much

B1ad3 gave an interview at EWC where he said that NAVI had looked at changes in the off-season, right? Obviously it looks bad because this result comes right after that, but does it have any effect on the players when you hear your coach say something like that?

No. I think I spoke again, it was the same topic on an interview in EWC, that we knew about it. We discussed it so many times. They decided to keep the same roster for this season, and then we see what happens. That's pretty much it, there's nothing to say. I don't think it will affect us that much. I don't think, I dunno, if someone in this team thinks that he's getting, for example, kicked, will play different or will play not the same if he knows that he's getting o7 or like this, right? No. It doesn't connect, I will say.

Like you say, you've got the lower bracket here. Is it extra important to bounce back right now, considering the slightly slow start to the season and the result today?

Yeah, I would say it's important for us to bounce back. For sure we need VRS points because we are dropping down, losing against lower teams in the ranking, I would say, because it was the same with Legacy, the same with M80 now. And also confidence, we need to step up to get some confidence, even, let's say not just for BLAST, for the future tournaments.
